Elemental Swarm

Four body plans for one rule set. Seed a swarm of tadpoles with a majority of one element and the same learned rules should grow that element's creature; the reported loss sums all four runs. Every tadpole keeps its domain and breeds true, and its heart stays blue and white until it dies. Regions are painted in domain slots A, B, C: the check takes the best assignment of real domains to slots, so it rewards forming distinct domain regions, never a particular domain.
