Tadpole Swarm

Targets for a population rule: one swarm of tadpole fauna that assembles into a swimming whale, and — after losing units — a pulsing jellyfish. Every unit is the game's tadpole: an element crystal that never changes, a spindle that can stretch and curl, and a prism free to change domain, state and size.
