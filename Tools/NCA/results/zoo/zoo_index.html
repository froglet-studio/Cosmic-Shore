Swarm Zoo

Seven personalities of one tadpole swarm. Each is the designed field swarm plus a different mix of nine behaviours (breathe, travelling wave, jitter, orbit, switch burst, curiosity, hunting, rush-breeding, bristling), found by a quality-diversity search over how a creature reacts to a ship, how lively it is and how dramatic its switches are. Every one grows all four bodies (whale, jellyfish, pufferfish, dragonfly) and passes 13/13 feasible switches on the old yardstick. Open one to watch it grow, switch and react.

Glass Minnows

the shy one

The shy one. Every element flees; nothing mobs (its mob threshold sits below even a parked ship's drift), and the startle relays through 76% of neighbours, so a vessel never touches it: 0.8% of the school is ever inside a ship that crosses it (field: 14%). Fly at it and the body opens a clean tunnel well before you arrive; park beside it and it holds a ring of empty water around you (crowd 2^-2.1 of an inert body). Crisp, tight, quick to switch (10 steps), slow to heal (54 steps). A creature you herd rather than fight.

Phoenix

the showman

The showman. A committed switch begins with a radial explosion (burst 3.9) and a 200-step vortex before the new animal condenses - the swarm swells to 2.06x its radius, the most theatrical morph in the map. It loves ships: park and ~89% of the body wraps your hull in an orbiting shell (stance +3.5, the map's ceiling), every element mobbing; fly past fast and it still parts (touched 6.6%). Tight body (looseness 0.23), heals a strike past its own pre-strike score on the whale (1.33).

Hornet Cloud

the buzz

The buzz. Fastest idle motion in the named set (1.06 voxels/step - 2.6x field) from a 16-step breathing pulse plus every tadpole circling its slot on a 1.1-voxel loop, at 1.5x top speed; no alignment term, so it shimmers rather than streams. Park and roughly half the body swarms you (stance +2.6), fly fast and it scatters. Loose and alive (looseness 1.9); a body that always looks like it is about to do something.

Old Drifter

the slow giant

The slow giant. Sluggish arrival (k 0.11), strong alignment (0.52) and a 27-step dwell make it the laziest creature in the map (idle speed 0.24) and the slowest to change its mind: a switch takes 38 steps to read and 122 to finish. Its Charge and Space tadpoles crowd a parked ship like curious cattle; a passing ship ploughs through it (20% touched, the most). No startle relay at all - a strike on one side does not alarm the other.

Hydra

the regenerator

The regenerator. Rush laying after a loss plus 2.2x top speed: a strike is back within 1.2x of its pre-strike score in 11 steps (field: 16; the slowest elite: 59), and the jellyfish ends a strike crisper than before (heal 1.40). Defensive: every startled tadpole bristles to DANGER, the pufferfish inflates 1.4x (3x field's) and the dragonfly (0.8x) and whale (0.4x) swell too; its Charge plates hold their ground while Mass and Time mob. A body you cannot wear down.

Murmur

the cloud

The cloud. The loosest body in the map (looseness 2.55: a very soft arrival leaves tadpoles a few voxels off their slots, under a 16-step breath), so it reads as a murmuration that has the shape of a whale rather than a whale. Very soft arrival (k 0.11) and the fastest morph (15 steps): a switch is a quick ripple, not a show. Curious at middle distance, relays startle to 95%, crowds a parked ship mildly (+1.85).

Sparkler

the crisp one

The crisp one - lowest mean divergence in the map (3.74 vs field 6.80): sharp arrival (k 0.56) with a fast 16-step breath and a light wobble makes the body glitter while staying exactly on plan, at 1.4x top speed (idle 0.96). Charge holds and spikes, Space jets, Time mobs. The trade-off: it heals slowly (59 steps), because the arrival is so stiff the hole takes a while to re-assign.
