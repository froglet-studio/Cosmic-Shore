NCA · hybrid creatures · new Oct 8

Whale, jelly, or both?

Chimeras between learned animals, with nothing trained on mixtures. Two rules, one body: each cell runs the whale's rule at one end and the jellyfish's at the other, and they negotiate a body: a bell-headed whale, a lizard under a jellyfish bell. One rule, three genomes: one network has learned lizard, whale and jelly, and every cell carries its own genome. Blend two seeds, splice halves, plant twins, cut it in two or paint genes on a living body, and watch the genome bar to see who wins.

Final rule (step 4000): a lizard cut in half regrows as conjoined twin lizards (3.2k cells, twice one lizard) · a 50/50 lizard+jelly seed turns whale, then buds a chain of jellyfish bells along the whale that never stops growing (5.3k cells, 29% whale / 66% jelly) · spliced whale+jelly goes jelly, then a whale re-emerges at ~450 steps and they hold at 42/58 · a wounded whale sprouts jelly tentacles, then heals back to whale · lizard|jelly two-rule chimera flips to jelly at ~1,000 steps

NCA · where the week began

The NCA bestiary

The animated 3D neural cellular automata, up close. One small network runs in every voxel and reads only its neighbours; nothing stores the shape.
