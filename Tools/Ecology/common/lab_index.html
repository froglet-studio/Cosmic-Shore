Living Ecology Lab

New overnight: the flight sim has stakes. Danger strikes in a hostile cell burn your petals for good, kills drop crystals that grow them back, and two learned bodies from phase 1 now live in the cell: a tadpole swarm that morphs with its diet and a 3D neural-automaton lizard that regrows what you cut. Round 1 was six research directions on fauna and flora that behave without behaviour trees: local rules, shared fields, quorum switches and swarms that assemble into bodies. Each is scored against scripted pilots on telegraph, counterplay, variety and payoff, and every run audits conserved mass. Open any viewer to fly the camera among the results. The recorded viewers open in pilot view, over the pilot's shoulder at whatever it is facing; 1–4 switch between pilot, chase, orbit and fly cameras, and clicking a creature follows it.
