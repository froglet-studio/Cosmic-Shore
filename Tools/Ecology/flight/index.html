Ram a creature to kill it: it drops a crystal of its element; fly through it for +1 petal. Stakes: your flower holds 4 elements of up to 10 petals. In a hostile cell every danger strike (bite, burn, snap, sting; a peck is a quarter) BURNS petals from all four, for good, with a 1 s cooldown. Crystals are the only way back. Your trail is conserved mass: thieves steal it, grazers and herds eat it. Every rule is a JS port of the scored Python species, gated against it headless (flight/fidelity.py).
