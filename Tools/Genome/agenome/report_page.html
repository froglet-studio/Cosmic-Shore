Amoebius genome lab

A .kkrieger-style genome, judged by Amoebius footage

Every phenotype on this page is a few hundred bytes: an operator graph in the spirit of farbrausch's .kkrieger that compiles to a raymarched world, a flock of vessels laying prism trails, a camera, a grade and a HUD. Each one renders a 4-second clip at 256×144, and its distance to Amoebius gameplay footage is its fitness. On that scale 1.0 is how much Amoebius differs from itself.

Progress

    
How each island's best has moved

The fitness has a learned critic in it that is retrained every few generations on Amoebius frames against the population's latest frames, so it gets stricter as the population improves. A rising fitness line can mean the critic caught up, not that the phenotypes got worse.
