02 The blocking finding, unchanged

The quest and progression system is still not part of the build

Re-measured from scratch rather than carried forward. Every element of the Revision 3 finding holds exactly:

  1. 01GameModeProgressionService's only host is still MIgration_Prefabs (DELETE LATER)/PlayerDataService.prefab.
  2. 02That prefab's guid is still referenced by zero scenes, prefabs or assets.
  3. 03No AddComponent, new GameObject or Resources.Load constructs it.

03.1 The two defects that came from playing the game

Both were found on 11 September from an owner report of live play — four US players, explicitly not a latency problem — and neither appeared in any tracker, any test suite or the Revision 3 audit:

  • B18 — a client could not leave a match at all, and could not leave a Maelstrom until the whole race-to-N finished. Every exit was gated on IsServer; the correct pattern existed on exactly one screen.
  • B19 — nothing watched a client's scene transition, so a lost one was a permanent black screen.

The report that produced them — "once you get off the happy path the game is bugged… connecting is a challenge, leaving is a challenge" — outranked the bug tracker, which had neither. That is the clearest available argument for items R1 and R3: this class of defect is only visible from playing, and nobody has been playing systematically.

04 The gap that widened

Five items closed and the amount of unverified work went up, not down. That is the finding of this re-run.

The arithmetic is worth stating plainly. Revision 3 found one QA verdict against a 19-mode game. Two days later there is still one verdict, against a 21-mode game, with 57 separately-tracked unverified changes and five more landed items on top. R1 — proving a Windows player reaches the menu — has still never been run, and it gates every other verification item.

This is not a criticism of the work landing; the work is good and the prompts are being followed closely. It is that the board has two kinds of item in it, and only one kind is moving. Of the five closed, five were "Session" lane. Of the ten open, the three most blocking — R1, R2, R3 — are the ones a session cannot do.
