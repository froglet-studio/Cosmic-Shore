Where the project stands

The May 2026 punch list scoped a paid full release with an in-build monetisation rail. We have re-scoped to Early Access: a paid app sold on the strength of what already works, with no in-app purchases at launch. That single decision removed the two largest unfinished engineering projects from the critical path and converted the remaining plan from roughly ten projects into six short workstreams.

All three core minigames, Maelstrom mode, the freestyle hub, the netcode and session layer, vessel progression, and the telemetry stack are shipped and playable. The settings panel, which the prior plan still carried as open engineering, was completed and verified in the repository during preparation of this checkpoint. The residual 100.5 person-days is quest dialogue and data, business setup, build and delivery pipeline, validation passes, a stability gate, and the marketing assets a converting store page requires.

The honest read on risk Two items carry genuine schedule risk and neither is a coding problem. The Steamworks partner account has external processing time we do not control, so it starts in week one. The capsule art and trailer are 13 person-days on a single contributor inside a two-week window, the tightest constraint in the plan. Both are detailed in the risk register on page 6, each with the specific mitigation already scheduled.

Decisions this plan locks

DecisionPosition
Launch typeEarly Access, not a demo and not a 1.0. Honest framing as a party game growing new vessels and episodes.
MonetisationApp price only. Episode tokens and web checkout are cut. The checkout flow has an unresolved server-side entitlement gap and must not take money until a backend verifies orders. Crystal-earned vessel unlocks remain as the progression loop.
NetcodeUnity Gaming Services Relay plus Netcode for GameObjects. Shipped and locked. No Steamworks networking, no host migration for Early Access.
Steam SDKNone at launch. Overlay, wishlists, reviews, and forums all work without it. Achievements and Steam Input become a first content patch.
Crash reportingUnity Cloud Diagnostics. Zero new vendors, and the consent toggle already exists in the shipped settings panel.
Hardware floorGTX 1060-class, 8 GB RAM, Windows 10 64-bit. Confirmed by one profiling session in week three, then locked.

Risk register

LevelRisk and mitigation
HighArt capacity. Capsules and the trailer are 13 days on one contributor inside a two-week window, the single largest concentration of work in the plan. Mitigation: both start in week one, and the trailer cut can be split to a second resource if week three slips.
HighSteamworks processing. Identity, tax, and banking verification is outside our control, and the one-week estimate assumes a clean first pass. Mitigation: A1 starts day one, ahead of every dependent item, so a slow approval never blocks work that could have proceeded.
MediumReview bounce. Each Steam review pass runs one to five business days and a first submission can be rejected on store-page or build technicalities. Mitigation: week six is held as buffer for exactly one rejection loop.

Definition of done

All six simultaneously true.

  1. Steam page live for at least two weeks with final assets, and the build has passed review.
  2. A fresh install on the floor machine reaches a completed four-player Maelstrom, humans and bots, with zero P0 or P1 defects.
  3. A fresh account completes the quest chain, with unlocks and progression persisting through Cloud Save.
  4. Cold boot to playable meets the load-time target in every game mode at every intensity.
  5. Crash reporting and funnel telemetry visible in dashboards from playtest builds.
  6. One closed playtest run through the actual Steam beta branch.
  7. Privacy policy and support contact live.
  8. Garrett has signed the exit checklist.

What this checkpoint asks for

  • No additional engineering headcount. Every remaining task has a named owner among the eight contributors.
  • Art is the constraint, not code. If one investment accelerates the date, it is a second pair of hands on the trailer cut in weeks two and three.
  • Scope discipline holds. The cut list on page 2 is the fence. Monetisation returns after launch, once a backend verifies entitlements.
  • Wishlists start compounding in week two. Every week the Coming Soon page is live is measurable pipeline into launch day.
Summary Cosmic Shore is feature-complete for Early Access. Nine of the ten original punch-list projects have shipped, the tenth was deliberately cut, and the settings work the prior plan still carried as open is verified complete. What remains is 100.5 person-days across eight people. No features are left to build: every system, including the quest and progression layer, is engineered and in the branch. The residual work is dialogue content, finalising, testing, and launch execution, spread across six workstreams that run largely in parallel. The two real risks, external Steam processing and concentrated art capacity, are both mitigated by starting them on day one.
