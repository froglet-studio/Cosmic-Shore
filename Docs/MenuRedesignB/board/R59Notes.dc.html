How the room is made

It is the real Ark (ArkReverse.fbx, Garrett's model): its hangar in the prow, between the jaws - the bay of arched frames, the three launch pads for the three escorts (one per seat), the floor lights, the glowing back wall and its tunnel, the clam-jaw doors opened with the model's own hangaropen take - in the game's ship materials, the trim in the squad's domain. The hull's prisms sit in the model's own sockets at their real sizes. Amoebius was fixed so it runs the current game again, and the game was screenshotted in it (Compare, row 1): the prisms are the game's read (black faces, a fresnel rim), in Nocturne. Dressed raw and noisy: the squad's prisms hover over the pads (the kept seat an outline), the HyperSea board between the arches (the Sector chart, the Ark at Tumult; the galaxy opposite), heaps of debris, danger blades at the mouth, hanging chains, junk in the haze. Out of the open mouth: the cell.

The six

A Seat placards: each card over its pad's prism, a hairline down to it. B Rail: the hangar clear, the seats in one rail at the bottom. C The board wall: turned to the HyperSea board, the seats stacked right. D From the cell: outside, the real Ark with its mouth open, the seats in a column. E Slate: one glass slate lists the squad. F One seat at a time: the chosen seat large, LB / RB.

Every board is interactive: the domain chips relight the room (Jade, Ruby, Gold), the seat cards select (the Jade ring), the kept seat invites its friend (Lime) or seats an AI until then, Breakers is Kestrel's so its chips refuse, TAKE INTO ARENA is Ruby.

Recommendation: A

The three pads are the Ark's own seats for the squad, and A is the only one where each card visibly belongs to its pad, so the kept seat reads as the empty pad waiting for someone; the open mouth and the cell stay in view above the cards.
