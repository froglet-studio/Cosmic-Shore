Items 9, 10, 11 - what changes

Settings (item 9)

  • A section 10.3 window: Light border on Black, the four tabs as section 10.5 hex tiles (exactly one active), Close (B) top right. Gamepad focus lands on the first row of the open tab.
  • Rows: label left, control right. Dropdowns are slivered fields; ON / OFF is the section 10.8 two-label toggle (active Light with a Jade underline); sliders are section 10.7 (Jade fill, hex handle) and now show their value, Field of view included.
  • The restart notice is wired and shows on Performance once a restart-only setting changes. The quality preset reads 'Custom' after anti-aliasing, texture or upscaling is changed by hand (it kept the old preset name).
  • Label fixes: 'Adaptive Performace' and the stray apostrophe in 'Haptics'. The dead second 'SFX Volume' slider (really an unwired haptics slider) is not drawn.
  • Credits becomes an authored row in the General links (it was cloned from Privacy Policy at runtime). Pad scroll-into-view is added so the Performance and Other rows below the fold can be reached.
  • Content to confirm: Colorblind mode, Subtitles and Subtitle size have no consumer, so they change nothing yet.

Credits (item 9)

  • Same window style. Real hierarchy: H1 title, H2 sections, H3 entries, Body small for roles and notices (every line used to copy the CLOSE label's style).
  • The right stick scrolls the list (the pad could not scroll it); the scrollbar is Jade. The window respects the safe area, and one B closes only Credits.

Closed surfaces (item 10)

  • Per decision D4, only what a player meets is restyled: the locked Port tab (item 1), the locked Episodes entry (item 5), and their refusal toasts. Store has no surface at all.
  • The purchase confirmation is drawn blind as a section 10.3 popup: the spend line in Gold with tabular numbers, the item on the right, Spend (opaque Purchase, icon + amount) left of Cancel, both straddling the bottom border.

Revision 2 (motion and fill)

  • Settings: every option gains a one-line description under its label (proposed copy - review it), rows rise in on the stagger, slider fills grow on open and the handle and toggle underline slide (std token). Corner brackets frame the window.
  • Credits opens on a title band over the menu scene ('Froglet Inc. presents / Cosmic Shore') and its sections rise in.
  • Closed surfaces: each vignette sits on the grid; the locked tab and Episodes entry shake once (the refusal) and their toasts drop in. The purchase window's spend line gains a balance bar - what you keep in Light, the spend in Gold.
  • Toasts drop in (panel token) and carry a Jade bar that drains over their 5 s; the board draws the lifecycle (in 0.25 s, hold 5 s, out 0.2 s) as a timeline that plays.

Toasts (item 11)

  • Today every menu toast renders as the runtime fallback, a 100 x 100 square that cuts most messages off. The new toast hugs its text (360 to 720 wide), Neutral Very Dark with a Neutral Light hairline, Body 18.
  • Toasts move from the lower-left quadrant (under the party panels) to the top centre below the nav, stacked newest on top. Timing, the queue of 10, the three-visible limit and drag-to-dismiss stay as they are.
  • The API takes only a string, so the leading glyph (lock for a refusal, check for success, danger diamond for an error, crystal for a goal) needs an optional kind argument. Without it every toast draws with no glyph.
