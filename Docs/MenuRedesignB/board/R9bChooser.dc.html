Why it looks like this
Google: its own button image, untouched - dark theme, the standard-colour G, "Sign in with Google". No recolouring, no monochrome G, no style-B rim on it.

Apple: black or white only, Apple's logo file (never redrawn), "Sign in / Continue / Sign up with Apple", no smaller than any other sign-in button. Drawn here with a stand-in apple; the build uses Apple's file.

Steam: no button. A Steam build signs you in on its own; if Steam is not running the chooser says so. Valve allows only its approved artwork, unaltered and standalone.

Focus is the sky-blue ring around a button, never on it.
