Welcome to the Cosmic Shore

Born on Tumult

As a native of the planet Tumult you are one of the most adaptive creatures in the known universe, with a burning curiosity for the unexplored, a healthy sense of adventure, and a strong competitive streak.

The largest space station

You have left the planet to chase glory and fortune on the galaxy’s largest space station, where the greatest pilots and biggest names in the Milky Way compete in simple but exhilarating contests.
