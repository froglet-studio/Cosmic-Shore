Ark: Squads and Decor beside the converter

What you asked (Shombith, 2026-10-27)
  • Ark gets two tabs beside the converter: SQUADS and DECOR. Decor is locked for now.
  • Make or join up to 4 squads. The Ark shows each squad mate's vessel, element, pilot and artifact.
  • Make squad: every vessel and pilot stands on a podium to the right of the squad list.
  • Only once a squad is made can it be taken into Mission or Arena.
  • The squad's domain changes at runtime, with a nanobot transition on the vessels.
On these boards
  • Make squad (interactive): press a podium to edit that seat, then pick its vessel, pilot, element and artifact below. Rogue seats pick AI wingmate or Open for a friend. CREATE SQUAD adds it to the list, and only then do TAKE INTO MISSION / ARENA appear.
  • Domain (Jade, Ruby, Gold, the game's own colours): pressing one dissolves each hull into 34 prism-bots that scatter and reassemble it in the new colour, seat by seat. The podium light and the stage glow follow.
  • Squad made / joined: the podium is the squad. A joined squad shows whose it is; only its leader edits it.
  • Join a friend's squad: friends' squads with a seat open. You bring your own four picks into the seat.
  • Decor: the tab carries a padlock; a press shakes it and says Not open yet.
Art and data
  • Hull renders: the Hangar card art (Assets/_Graphics/Hangar/Cards). Pilot portraits: Assets/_Graphics/Pilots; Heist is the stand-in portrait.
  • Pilots and artifacts: Docs/Pilots/pilots_and_artifacts.json (two pilots and two artifacts per hull, three fleet artifacts shown).
  • Elements are words, never colours (LOCKED palette rule). Domains carry colour.
