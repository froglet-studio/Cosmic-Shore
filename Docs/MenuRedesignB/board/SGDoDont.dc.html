8 · Do and don't

Every rule has been broken once already

Each pair comes from a board on Frogma or a shipped screen. The don'ts are the reason the rule exists.

Element colours on the HUD

Don't Rev 3 HUDs: each element a colour, drifting like a lava lamp (R3HudSquirrel)

Do Today's lockup: one neutral, the element told by its glyph (AbilityLockupStyle elementColour off)

A placeholder backdrop

Don't Rev 34: the options floated over an empty sea with a THE CELL label; rejected outright

Do Rev 36: the same captain drawn over the real Home screen

Gold for a highlight

Don't Gold on a first-place row or a featured card

Do Gold only where crystals are spent: the Purchase button, the upgrade price

Glow as decoration

Don't A pulsing ring round a card nobody needs to press

Do The one glow: a free Daily Deal (lime) or a buyable one (gold)

Lime on a Ready button

Don't READY drawn as a lime call to action, as if you could change your mind

Do READY in Ruby: once pressed there is no way back

A hull pick in sky blue

Don't The picked hull in an arena lineup marked in the old sky-blue selection

Do The picked hull in Jade: you can claim it and pick again

A string in code

Don't PrivacyConsentOverlay builds its colours and words in C#

Do Every word in a serialized field, filled from a template (TemplateText.Fill)
