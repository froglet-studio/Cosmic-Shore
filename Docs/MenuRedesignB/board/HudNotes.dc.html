Item 12 - In-game HUD - what changes

What stays (LOCKED)

  • Every vessel keeps its own ability icons, in the same slots: Charge, Mass, Space, Time left to right under their element flowers, the Omni crystal card left of them on every vessel, and the Squirrel's Drift further left.
  • The lockup geometry is the shipped AbilityLockupStyle (columns 128 apart, plates 104 x 88, flower and icon 60, chip 24, 40 / 44 from the corner). The petal ladder is never hue-shifted.
  • Live gauges stay gauges: cooldown veils sweep clockwise, fuel / heat / ammo fill from the bottom, and a gauge colour never carries upgrade meaning.

What gets better

  • Plates take the style-B surface (Neutral Very Dark at 86%) with a Neutral Lightest edge band; an upgraded card (all five petals white) takes the Light edge, a raised plate and a soft bloom.
  • Input chips are style-B keycaps from the binding (LT, RT, A, B); a passive ability shows no chip. A locked slot is a dim plate with a dash and no chip.
  • Generated readouts are drawn as readouts: the Squirrel's reach ring + steal count, the Dolphin's blast profile with its pilot / fauna tally, its jaws opening with banked skim energy, the Omni blast tally.
  • The top of the HUD: goal row on a slivered glass plate (tabular numbers), domain score columns with a team-colour cap and Team Dark at 12% (not a full team fill), the pause hex wearing the volume gauge.

The match around it

  • Ready: a READY button with its A keycap, then a waiting state that names who is still picking (there was none after a multiplayer ready), then 3, 2, 1, GO.
  • Pause: one window - Resume (Start or B), Settings (the item 9 window), Leave match (Y). Guests see Resume too; Leave match reads Leave party. Main menu no longer sits on A.
  • Connecting: one progress line with real counts and the pilots' ready state, in the theme fonts (the panel used an off-spec font).
  • End: the reveal line in Light with the winning domain above it, then the scoreboard - Team Dark tint rows, crystals in Gold, a stats strip, and PLAY AGAIN (A) / MAIN MENU (B) on separate inputs (one B used to fire both).
  • Spectating: one bar under the score columns - pilot, domain and hull, 2 / 4, camera - with keycap hints.

  • Squirrel: Charge Crystal Joust, Mass Boost Ring, Space Steal, Time Skimming.
  • Sparrow: Charge Skyburst Rockets, Mass Turret Stance, Space Pulsefire Cannons, Time Afterburner.
  • Dolphin: Charge Echo Sight, Mass Crystal Seeding, Space Echo Obliteration, Time Charge Fill Rate.
  • Scarab: Charge Cavitation Blast, Mass Switch, Space Ball Forge, Time Throttle.
  • Urchin: Charge Chain Spikes, Mass Trail Rider, Space Track Projector, Time Slip.
  • Manta: Charge Sting, Mass Yastri, Space Kabloom, Time Soar.
  • Rhino: Charge (open design slot), Mass Trail Slabs, Space (open design slot), Time Ramp Spool.
  • Serpent: Charge Sniper Shot, Mass Seed Wall, Space Scope, Time Solid Fuel Pellets.
  • Butterfly: Charge Scale Dust, Mass Mass / Dust Mode, Space Dust Reach, Time Fold.

Found on the way

  • No vessel authors an upgraded sprite; the upgrade reads from the card frame alone.
  • Only the Squirrel and the Dolphin fill the Omni card; the other seven draw it locked.
  • Rhino's Charge and Space are open design slots (drawn locked); Manta's Soar and every Input 0 ability show no chip by design.
  • The fleet table in Docs/claude/VESSEL_HUD_AND_ELEMENTS.md is behind: Manta binds all four icons, and Serpent's Mass is now Seed Wall (placeholder art).
  • Many icons are still placeholders (Scarab, Rhino, Serpent, the Squirrel's Crystal Joust and Drift).
