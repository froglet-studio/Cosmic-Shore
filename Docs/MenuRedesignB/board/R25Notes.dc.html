Kept from Arcade G
Preview left, description under it; the controls top right; one glass panel with intensity (the numeral, LB / RB) and the pilots on their pedestals in their team's light; ADD AI PILOT (X) with the AI Easy / Medium / Hard row; the lime START. Team: focus your seat, left / right steps it (as built).
New: the vessel
Arena and Voyage cards seat several hulls, so every pedestal shows the vessel its pilot flies (the hull's own render, standing in the team light), and the controls panel follows YOUR vessel.
A Steppers
Under each pilot two steppers: the team and the vessel. Focus your seat: left / right steps the team; down to the vessel, left / right steps the hull. The host steps an AI's the same way. Same control the Arcade window uses for teams - nothing new to learn.
B Vessel strip
The pedestals show each pilot's vessel; under the lineup a strip of the card's hulls for the focused seat, the picked one lit. Better when a card has many hulls (Regatta has nine); costs one more row.
Six seats
Arena and Voyage seat up to six (the Arcade four), so the pedestals are narrower; the panel and START keep the Arcade window's places.
Voyage
Tandava is one crew against the creature (one team), so its seats carry no team stepper - only the vessel. Its hulls: Rhino, Squirrel, Sparrow.
Intensity
No Roman numerals (Shombith). A boards: the level as a number over its four pips - the Arcade window now reads 1 - 4 the same way. B boards: no number, just the signal sign - four bars rising, lit to the level. Either can go on any window; say which.
Not changed
The Arena roster, who may fly what, the AI's hull pick and the seat rules stay the game's; this is the window only. Maelstrom keeps its own window.
