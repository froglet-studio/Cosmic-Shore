Rev 8 - the scoreboard, redrawn
What was wrong with the built board
Every row was a full slab of team colour, so four rows read as two walls of teal and purple.
Two kinds of crystal side by side: the reward (+200) and the crystals each pilot collected, in one corner.
Three identical times: the race time is the team's, so it repeated on every Jade row.
Plain stat tiles and buttons; the banner was cut off at the top; the match feed and ability row showed through.
What every option does
Rev 6 language: the living lattice behind, element rims on every panel, element glyphs on the stats.
Element meanings: Time teal for times and records, Mass blue for crystals and rewards, Space violet for people (the rematch votes), Charge gold for PLAY AGAIN.
Your row or card wears the Rev 7 sky-blue selection.
The team time shows once, by the team, not on every row.
The reward is its own strip: +200, and the balance counting from 9,600 to 9,800.
Rematch votes show as faces under PLAY AGAIN.
The three options
A - Podium: the winning team on a podium (1st raised in the middle), the other team beside it, stats and reward under it. The most celebratory; best for 1-3 pilots a team.
B - Glass ladder: the familiar ranked list, but dark glass rows with a thin team stripe, grouped under team headers, each row's share of the crystals drawn as a Time-teal line. Scales to 12 pilots.
C - Your match first: a big card for you (place, hull, your stats against your best), standings in a side rail. Most personal; standings are secondary.
Questions for the owner
Which option - A, B or C (or a mix: say which parts)?
Show your personal bests (C's 'best 8.10s') - we would read them from the profile stats?
Keep the three stats (drift, boost, prisms) per mode as now, or let each mode pick its own?
