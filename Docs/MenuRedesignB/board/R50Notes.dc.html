Where the numbers come from

Real, from Ian's PostHog queries of 29 Oct: 25 players and 117 matches in 90 days (release), 1,284 editor matches (91%), 2.0 rows per play, rematch 64% solo vs AI and 50% solo, 2 multi-human matches, port 1 of 43 finished (2%) against 63% on Windows, Bloomrush 0 of 13, 21 of 241 double payouts.

Examples: the daily series, matches per play type on B, the last-24-hour event counts, health times and query run times.

The page reads our own server on the Oracle VM through a read key. No data sits on GitHub Pages.

Colour, and the open question

Data is one blue (blue-68). Fire marks a problem and only a problem. The Lime tick marks healthy. Jade (selection) marks the filter you picked and the focus ring. No domain colour is used, since a play type is not a domain.

Open: a chart with two or more series has no family to draw from. Every family has a meaning, so these boards use one series per chart and labels instead. Logged in the design feedback queue.
