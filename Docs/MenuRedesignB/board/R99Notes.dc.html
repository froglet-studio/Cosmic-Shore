Rev 99 - the Pilots tab as a crew PDA
AskedShombith, 2026-11-13: the Pilots tab needs designing; the pilot pictures that show captains inside the Ark give a cool view of it; "more like a PDA lying somewhere where you see the details and stories of each pilot".
What it replacesGarrett's built Pilots tab (section 92.1): a 6 x 3 grid of the crew over the Ark backdrop, and a press opens a full-screen profile page, the pilot's scene behind one panel of words. Built from his words, not drawn on Frogma first.
Kept from the buildEvery word the profile page shows: callsign, name, the two animals, faction, home, the callsign's sight, the story, and the count. One cell a callsign, eighteen, in the order they joined. The scenes' close-ups for the cells.
What changesThe tab is a place in the Ark, like every other Ark tab (Rev 67): a crew PDA lies there. The crew and the files are read ON the PDA, not on a page over the screen. Three states: lying down (the last pilot read), picked up (the crew), a file. Picking up leans the view in toward the PDA; that is the one transition, no idle motion.
The optionsOnly the place differs. A: the chart room, on the chart table under the Ark's great window onto the HyperSea (Zodiac's and Needle's rooms). B: a captain's cabin, on the desk under a porthole, one warm lamp. C: the hangar, on a workbench, the bay running out of the open mouth into the cell (Galleon's view).
Recommended: AIt shows the most of the Ark (the window, the HyperSea, the room's trim and prisms) while the PDA still sits clear in front, and a chart table is where a crew's files would be kept. B is the quietest and most personal; C reuses the hangar the Squads tab already shows.
PadA picks up; on the crew, the D-pad moves between cells and A opens a file; on a file, left and right step the crew and B goes back; B on the crew lays the PDA down. LB / RB still change tab.
For the buildThe place is a live set piece in the Ark scene like the Rev 85/86 rooms; the PDA is a small mesh whose screen is a world-space canvas (or a render texture) carrying the existing ArkPilotsPage cells and PilotProfileModal's words. The renders here are Blender stills (r99_ark_pilots_render.py, the Rev 67 kit); the screen is drawn live on the board, mapped onto the glass.
Type on the PDAThe lying PDA's rest screen keeps two sizes off the scale (96 and 40 px on its own 1200 px glass) so the callsign still reads through the table's foreshortening, about a third of that on screen.
OpenWhether the PDA shows the pilot's whole scene (as the profile page does now) or the 4:5 crop drawn here. Whether recruits without art keep the "Pictured:" line on the PDA.
