Squads: the deck (Rev 29 C, picked)

Every state of the Squads tab in the deck layout. The domain chips rebuild the hulls in the new colour (the nanobots), the only motion. The Unity build follows these boards.
Make squad (interactive)The cards step back and the picker opens under them: vessel, pilot, element, artifact, and for a rogue card AI wingmate or open for a friend. Create squad adds it to the list; only then does Take into appear.
Squad madeThree cards, the leader raised in the middle. Take into Mission (locked, as on Home) or Arena. Edit reopens the picker; Delete asks for a second press.
A joined squadKestrel's squad, you fly Rogue One. Only the leader edits it or changes its domain; Leave replaces Delete.
No squad yetThree dashed card outlines and Make squad.
Join a friend's squadFriends' squads with a seat open, each a small fanned deck. Join takes the open seat with your own four picks.
Decor lockedThe Decor tab shakes and says Not open yet.
