The three layouts
  • A Vault (interactive: press an entry): a list on the left, the crystal alive on a stage in the middle, a solid reading panel on the right. Gameplay types in first, every hull's ability for the element, lore last.
  • B Field Guide: a two-page plate. The crystal on the left page with labelled callouts; its page on the right; every entry along a strip under the book.
  • C Orbit: the Omni crystal at the centre, the four elements orbiting it, ecology and lexicon on the outer ring. Picking a node slides its page in from the right.
Fixed already (the build, pushed)

Rules kept
  • Solid panels under all words: no sea behind text (that is what made the build hard to read).
  • Elements carry no colour; the crystals are their real lime renders. Selection is the blue.
  • Motion only on the pick: each element's own entrance (Charge jolts, Mass drops, Space warps, Time ticks), the crystal's real loop, words typing in. Pick one and it is built.
