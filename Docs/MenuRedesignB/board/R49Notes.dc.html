What the I card does stays: the voyage pager, the squad's formation with the presets beside it (a preset or D-pad up / down swaps it), Set sail, Ark crew, Story. Less is written: the summary is one line (the rest is in Story), three numbers, and the waypoints show only the two ends, lit, with the count between. The art is the Voyage Board's own renders of where you are going.

Recommendation: A

The destination's colour fills the card without fighting the text, and the squad reads as standing in the place it is about to sail to. C shows the most (the real course) but repeats the map beside it; B is the calmest; D is the most like a game card.
