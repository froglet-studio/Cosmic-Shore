The Launch Console, as the tool you release with
Six pages in one look: press Play, the left rail switches pages, the swatches in the top bar switch the theme, and only the call to action glows. Status keeps the live console's rule: Lime for go, Fire dashed for at risk, Fire solid for no-go. No way back (Promote, Release) is Ruby.
The six pages
Mission
The stage you are in, its gate, every platform's go or no-go, the next steps, and Start a release.
Pipeline
Bleeding Edge, Development, Master, Release side by side with their vessels and what each feeds. Promote opens what would move.
Builds
Every platform on every branch. A build's steps live, where it goes (Drive, Play, TestFlight, Steam, the Toybox), its size over time.
Release
Perform a release in five steps: what, checks, notes, audience, launch. A check that blocks says how to get past it.
Signals
Who is playing by access tier, the gate meter, retention by build, data from the builds, and the feedback inbox.
Theme
Deep Sea, Shore, Nocturne and Dart Frog, each previewed. Motion full, calm or off; density; the sea and the cell behind.
What each would read
Mission
The launch-status board, READINESS.md and the stages in the launch-flow skill, as the console reads them today.
Pipeline and Builds
The branches, the Bleeding-edge guard, the release-builds and sync-build-branches workflows, the build profiles.
Release
The release-promote workflow, the checklist, the runbook and the changelog already in the console's store.
Signals
Feedback from the Bug Ledger and the in-game button. Retention and sessions need an analytics source; none is connected yet.
Theme
The style guide's tokens and the colour-set themes the analytics console already generates. Saved per person.
