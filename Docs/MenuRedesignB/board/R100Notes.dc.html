Rev 100 - the Voyages tab, new, in three pages
What was asked

Shombith, 2026-11-13: everything on the Voyages tab is gone; a new design set in three pages. 1 the HyperSea map (a table top and other options, all zoomable) with the voyages your pilots recommend; 2 the voyage opened - its story, route and rewards, animated; 3 your Ark - your lobby first, a squad only when there is no lobby, then Set sail. Voyages use difficulty 1-9, not intensity. Quests and Saga / Episodes are drawn in other rounds.

Page 1 - the HyperSea

Every map zooms the same way: the ladder (Galaxy, Arm, Sector, Course), + and -, and the wheel. A press on a place, or on a pilot's card, picks the voyage; Open voyage goes on. A Table top (recommended): the chart on a table, the edge-on view as its front, the places as beacons whose height is the difficulty. B Orrery: the galaxy as a disc with a slow ring of bearings. C Bridge: flat and full-bleed, the cards along the foot. D Dive: three glass sheets; a zoom is a dive. The foot of every page 1 is left for Saga and Episodes (Rev 103).

Page 2 - the voyage

Who offers it, what job it is, its story in the pilot's words, its stops, what it pays. Difficulty 1 to 9: the pilot's recommendation is marked, any other can be picked, and the crystals it pays follow it. A Briefing (recommended): the portrait and story, the route drawing itself, the rewards arriving. B Journey: one lane across the window, the Ark sailing it, each stop telling its part. C Dossier: the map stays; Story, Route, Rewards slide open.

Page 3 - your Ark

A Lobby (recommended): the lobby's three seats as they stand; a friend's seat shows Ready or Choosing; your own seat changes in place (a vessel, an element: one press). B No lobby: the same page with a squad picked from the list, and Invite friends. C Formation: the three vessels in formation over the course. Set sail is the one Lime on every page.

New rules to confirm

A voyage is a job a pilot of your crew offers (the slice's 31 jobs), one card per pilot. Difficulty 1-9 replaces intensity 1-4 on voyages, and scales the crystal reward (drawn as base x (0.55 + 0.1 x difficulty)). With a lobby, the lobby is the squad; squads appear only without one.
