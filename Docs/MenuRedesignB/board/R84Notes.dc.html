REV 84 NOTES

Flight certificate and element tiers

Name (suggested): the Flight Certificate, with three stages a vessel climbs: Launch Ready (the minimum viable pass), Fleet Ready, Arena Ready. Below Launch Ready it is In the hangar.

Five checks, 20% each, read from the game's own files by the codex (never typed): 1 all four abilities in the map; 2 the level-5 upgrades, 5% per wired one; 3 a Time game; 4 a Mass or a Space game (either); 5 a Charge game. A game is an arcade card whose element is that element.

Launch Ready = checks 1, 2 (all four wired) and 3. Fleet Ready = and 4. Arena Ready = all five.

Tiers S, A, B, C per element: S the best of the fleet in that element's game, C the least. A dash where the vessel has no game of that element. The letters on A, B and C are placeholders: every vessel is rated by you first.

Only solo vessels. Crews (Goby & Pistol, Wrasse & Grouper, Orca, Honeyguide) and the Termite (no map) are left out.

Fleet view (B): sort by completion or by one element's tier, and filter by stage and by an element's tier letter, each on its own and together (your ask of 2026-11-10). Colour: ink for done; Gold only for Arena Ready and an S (a first place, the style guide's Gold); no element colour. A is recommended: the certificate sits where a reader already looks at one vessel; B and C can come with it as the fleet views.
