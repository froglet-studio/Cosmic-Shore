The story

Set sail on the HyperSea

The HyperSea is one map for everyone: named places joined by lanes. Much of the work out there is carrying news, which only couriers can move faster than light. Voyages are the fourth place to play, after the Toybox, the Arcade and the Arena.

1

Port

Your pilots drum up jobs for their factions. Pick one and load the Ark.

2

Lanes

Fly the Ark down the lanes between places, the way the Arkway flies.

3

Places

Vault fields, lairs, wrecks, contested cores and harbours, each a real cell.

4

Meetings

Cross another squad’s path and play it out together, fight or deal.

5

Home

Whatever is aboard the Ark when you get home is yours.

Your squad

Three pilots, one Ark

A squad is three vessels. Each seat holds four cards: the vessel, its element, the pilot and an artifact. The Ark carries the artifacts, and the payload you came for.
