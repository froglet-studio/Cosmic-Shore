The fourA Poster (recommended). One window over Home: the Ark on the HyperSea, three features under it, the fixes folded away, Set sail. One look and you know what is new.B Pages. One feature a page, LB / RB to page, Set sail on the last. Bigger art, more presses.C Changelog. Every update in a list, the picked one beside it. Also the page Settings opens; older entries are placeholders.D Card. A small card slides in at Home’s corner; it opens into a tall drawer on the right. The quietest after an update.

The green mark stays on the row until it has been opened once.Closing it says where it lives (“stays in Settings, under Other”). Set sail opens Voyages on the Ark.
