The Launch Console in the Unity editor: what it reads and how it decides

The window

FrogletTools > Launch > Launch Console. It answers "can we land, and what is in the way?" and gives every launch action one button. It only reads: it fetches the launch-status branch's status.json in the background and opens pages in the browser. No API call, no token, nothing written.

Milestone, date, repo, each platform's component ids, action links and rollback, the audit's verdicts, the section 10 blockers and the "Needs a human, in order" list sit in one config asset (LaunchConsoleConfigSO), with the artifact's links.

The build follows Docked, and switches to Narrow below 640 px, unless you pick otherwise.

The pill rule

Each record: a failure is No-go; a success older than 48 hours is At risk; skipped, cancelled or running is At risk; a fresh success is Go. A platform is the worst of its records and the audit's verdict for it, and its line names what decided. With neither, it reads Unknown.

Ages count from the record's event date when it carries one: the eight records on the branch were all written on 6 Nov for events up to 24 days old. Every figure shows its source and age.

Real data only: status.json at 8a36d99 (8 records) and READINESS.md, measured 2026-11-05. Today that is 4 no-go, 3 at risk.

Where it departs

It draws in Unity's editor font and greys (FrogletEditorPalette), not Aldrich on Deep Sea, so it sits in the editor like every Froglet tool. The families keep their meaning: Lime for Go and the build steps, Fire for No-go, At risk as a dashed Fire edge with its words, Ruby for Promote and Roll back, Jade for the line under the picked platform. Every card, pill and button keeps the corner sliver.

The live checklist is in the artifact's store, which the window cannot read, so a button opens the console. The audit snapshot is the one hand-kept figure, and it shows its date.

The artifact draws the picked pill with an outline that its own sliver clips away. Here the pick is a line inside the pill.
