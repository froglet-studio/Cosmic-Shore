Rev 105 - the Artifacts tab without cards
AskedShombith, 2026-11-13: "The UI cards on top of each screen. I do not like them at all. ... For the artifacts screen, lets see what you come up with." The backdrops stay ("fire for now"); the Ark takes its light, motion and design from the pilot scenes set inside the Ark.
What goesRev 86's two cards: the list box on the left and the picked artifact's box on the right. No option draws a box behind its words: text sits on the room with a soft shadow, and the only lines are a leader, a meter and a lens rim. The mockup colour-set switch is gone too.
Three levelsEvery option breaks the tab into three steps, one press each, B to go back: the hoard (which artifacts you have), one artifact (what it is and does, who carries it, Give), and feeding it (its element's crystals and the level they buy). The level shows in the tab row's trail. Each level shows only what it needs, so there is never a wall of words.
Real contentThe hoard is four unique artifacts as the game rolls them (INVENTORY.md 2): a kind, a name, a serial, ONE attuned element on the diminishing curve, three fixed tracks. Values come from the shipped kinds. Crystals start at 10 of each, Shombith's default for now; a feed takes 5 (ArkArtifactsPage.feedBatch). Feed works on the boards: the crystals fly in and the level rises.
From the pilot scenesB's lens is Jugger's and Scales' dome: a round glass over the room in which the game itself plays, prisms and trails in depth, the payload of the picked artifact doing its thing. A warm light under the rim stands for the brass and lanterns. A and C keep the scenes' rule that the words belong to the object: labels on the pedestals, a caption under the piece.
Recommended: BIt is the only option that SHOWS what an artifact does (Hydra's heads fan out and burst; the number of heads is the level), which is what a player needs before feeding one, and it is the most HyperSea. A is the quietest and keeps the room whole but crowds when the hoard grows past what the pedestals hold. C reads best on a phone (one big thing, one caption) but hides the hoard.
