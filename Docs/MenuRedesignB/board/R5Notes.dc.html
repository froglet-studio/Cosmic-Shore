Rev 5: the AI seat info, in style B
Whose feature
Yash built it (bleeding-edge, 2026-10-17): a host can tell AI hulls apart - hover names the hull, (i) opens a card. It works today with generated boxes. These boards only give it the style-B look; what it does stays his.
Hover
On our seat ROWS a 1.35x grow would cover the neighbours, so the row stays put: its border lights in the domain colour and the role word swaps to the hull's name. Mouse only, as now; the pad reads the hull from the row's icon.
The (i)
A small hex inside the row, left of the X (kick), in the domain colour - not a blue square floating on the corner.
The card
A glass card with a glowing domain-colour border beside the seat it describes, with a notch pointing at the row. Art from the Hangar stage, the seat and element line, the name, the Hangar's line, and the four abilities as element hexes with their controls (the same rows the Hangar's Abilities tab shows). X or a tap outside closes it, as now.
Words
The card's words become authored fields (the seat line template, the ABILITIES heading), as every player-facing word must be.
