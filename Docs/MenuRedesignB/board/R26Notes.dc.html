Rev 26 - the Codex
Asked
The Codex brought up from the design board, with mockups of how it shows the ethirions and everything else: pilots, artifacts, elements, ecology (Shombith, 2026-10-26).
Where
CODEX is the first tab of the top bar (section 84: CODEX, HANGAR, HOME, ARK, PORT). It is locked today and has no screen; these boards are that screen. LT / RT reach it once it opens.
Chapters
ETHIRIONS, ELEMENTS, ECOLOGY (Flora / Fauna), PILOTS, VESSELS, ARTIFACTS, TOOLS. LB / RB step chapters; the grid on the left, the open page on the right, the same split as the Hangar.
Data
Ethirions, ecology and tools: Assets/Resources/Codex.asset (CodexSO, Garrett's, Docs/CODEX.md), read through CodexSO.Load(). Pilots: SO_Captain_<Hull>_<Element>. Vessels and elements: element_ability_table.py. Every number on these boards is the shipped value.
Not in the game yet
Discovery (Docs/CODEX.md section 5: every entry ships unlocked; UnlockedByDefault and DiscoveryKey exist but nothing reads them), so the counts, NEW badges and silhouettes show the proposed shape. Artifacts have no game data: Afterburner Coil and Deep Missile Bay come from the Rev 24 Hangar board. Descriptions are blank in the Codex asset and show as [bracketed] slots.
Colour
Elements carry no colour: their petals are white. Blue is the selection; gold is a level-5 upgrade; lime is NEW and the one call to action (Fly with Shrapnel); fire is the risk on an artifact.
Proposed copy
The one-line meaning of each element (the strike you carry, what you build, how far you reach, how fast you move) is read off the fleet's ability table, for review.
