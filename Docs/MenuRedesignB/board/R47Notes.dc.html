"Rework the Ark squad screen. What was wrong with the colours

Built (section 95): SquadNanobotSwap tints the whole hull Image 45 percent toward GetDomainSignalColor, which is the palette's TrailHighlightColor pushed to full brightness: teal 13FFF2, magenta FF00F9, amber FFA700. The picture under it is the Hangar card, in the silver class livery (a card claims no team), so body, glass and engines all take the cast. Jade comes out teal and Ruby pink, while the ship itself wears azure and violet plates (ShipColor) on a gunmetal body. Three Jade / Ruby / Gold sets disagree: the ship's plates, the trail highlight, and the style foundation's 00D4FF / A600FF / FFAE00.

Here every hull and the Ark are rendered from their FBX models in each domain with the game's own colours (body, glass and engine from vessel_colors.py; the domain slot from the palette's ShipColor1 / ShipColor2), lit for a showroom: a key, a fill and a rim light on the gunmetal, the domain plates glowing. A domain change swaps one correct picture for another. The chips, the edit line and the bots use the style foundation's Jade 00D4FF, Ruby A600FF, Gold FFAE00. The ground is the menu's deep blue with its sea lines.

The Ark

ArkReverse.fbx, Garrett's Ark (the Arkway's and every voyage's), rendered as a slow orbit (36 frames, a 6 s loop). In the game it would be the model itself on a RenderTexture, the way the converter tab draws its 3D picture, with its turn takes playing. The 108 hull prisms the game lays in its sockets are not part of the model and are not drawn here.

The six

A Escort: the Ark centre stage, the three escorts round it, the seats along the bottom - nanobots. B Hangar: seats stacked left, the Ark large right - warp. C The deck, kept: the built deck unchanged, the Ark above it - nanobots. D Bays: the Ark over three launch bays - warp. E Dossier: the Ark left half, three seat rows right - nanobots. F Cinema: the Ark fills the screen, the squad floats over it - warp.

Every board is interactive: press Jade / Ruby / Gold on Skim Wing to play the change; Breakers is Kestrel's, so its chips refuse. Press the squad rows to switch squads.

Recommendation: A, Escort, with the nanobots

It is the only one where the Ark reads as what it is - a big ship its three escorts protect - while the seats keep the four rows the deck has. The nanobots are already built (SquadNanobotSwap) and need only the colour fix; the warp is new UI work (a lens ring and a throat over each picture). C costs the least to build if the deck should stay.

Differs from the build

The hull picture per domain replaces the tint (a render per hull and domain, or the model on a RenderTexture); the Ark's 3D picture joins the tab; the domain chips stay where the leader is; Make squad, Join, Edit and Take into (Mission, Arena) are unchanged from section 95. The build waits on a pick.
