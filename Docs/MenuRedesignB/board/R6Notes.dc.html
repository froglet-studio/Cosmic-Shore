Rev 6: an elemental UI
The lattice
The blueprint grid behind panels (Ark recipe, leaderboard, Profile card, Toy Box, Toy configure) becomes a living crystal lattice: two layers drift at different speeds, energy pulses in the four element colours run along its lines, a few nodes glow. Windows crystallise in: the lattice grows from the centre as a window opens.
The four elements as meaning
Charge = threat and energy: START, PLAY, a match about to begin. Mass = substance: vessels, upgrades, crystals, prices. Space = reach and presence: friends, parties, invites, who is online. Time = rate: timers, quests, records, leaderboards. Omni (all four chasing round a rim) for Home, the Ark and anything about every element.
Frames
Prism-cut panels (chamfered corners) instead of plain slivers. A panel about one element carries a comet of that element's light round its rim; an omni panel carries all four. Buttons are the same shape, lit in their element.
Friends
Option A: the FRIENDS tab goes; a presence orb sits bottom-left (you, your party bonded to you, online friends orbiting) and blooms into your party as a molecule plus who is online. Option B: a bond rail on the left edge, hover for a friend's card. Both keep everything the panel does today: party seats, invite / join / watch, requests.
How it is built in Unity
One UI shader draws the lattice procedurally (no textures, one quad per panel; the pulses are a time uniform), so it costs a fraction of the grid sprite's overdraw. The rims are a second small UI shader (a rotating conic mask on the sliver sprite). Element colours come from the existing AbilityLockupStyle palette - the same colours as the HUD and the Hangar.
