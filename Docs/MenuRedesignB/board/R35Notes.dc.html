Rev 35 notes - hover
Asked. "Then we need some new hover animations."
Today. KitButton lightens its fill 8 % and its border 10 % on hover; KitHoverTint lifts a word to TextLight (nav tabs, keycaps); tiles and cards have no hover of their own. There is no motion, so the pointer gets almost no answer.
The rules every option keeps. Hover is white (it is not a choice yet, so it is never the Selection blue, and elements carry no colour). It plays once on enter and rests; nothing idles. It is pointer-only: the pad and keys get the sky-blue focus ring, unchanged, and a control under both shows focus. Touch gets none. Locked controls (PORT) answer nothing.
Recommended: A (Lift). It is the plainest 'this one': the rise and the rule say it without a second colour, and it is cheap (one transform, one width). B and D are the quiet choices when the control must not move (dense lists, the HUD); C looks richest but tells the player least.
For the build. One kit component, KitHover (replacing KitHoverTint), on KitButton, KitTile, the card and the nav tab: the look and its timings live on the theme asset (UIThemeSO), so a pick changes every control at once, and the animation runs only on enter/exit (no per-frame cost while resting). Numbers per option are on its board.
