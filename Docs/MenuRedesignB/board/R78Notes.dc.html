Shombith, 2026-11-08, on Rev 76's five transitions: "I mean the UI should move with the phone as we rotate I guess". So the UI does not go out and come back: it moves, piece by piece, with the turn.

Follow vs Glide

Follow is the one that moves WITH the phone: the pieces are halfway when the phone is halfway, and they stay level. It needs the screen to stop the OS turning it and to turn itself at the end.

Glide moves AFTER the phone: the OS turns the screen as today, then the pieces travel. Cheaper, certain, and still never a cut.

What only a device shows

How the OS behaves when the app turns the screen itself (Follow), and how the motion feels at the real speed of a hand. A one-day prototype of Follow on one iPhone and one Android decides it; Glide can be built straight away.

Every other vessel still stays landscape; only the menu and the Goby and Pistol turn.
