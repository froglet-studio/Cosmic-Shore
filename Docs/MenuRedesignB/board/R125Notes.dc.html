Rev 125: E, split into pages

You picked E. It is now a site of seven pages, so no page carries everything. The header nav moves between them; the Cosmic Shore / Tumult switch, the nine themes and Motion are on every page.

A Home: the hero, the story, four ways to play (Toybox, Arcade, Arena, Voyages), a fleet teaser, Press Start, get the game, the latest post.

B Vessels: the racing lane; every card and the Open the Fleet Codex button open the codex full screen, and you switch vessel inside it.

C Arcade: all 49 arcade games in the Fleet Codex look, filtered by Time, Charge, Mass, Space or Party and sorted by element, A to Z or vessel.

D Arena: its own page: Regatta, Broadside, Brood Rush and Astro League, which hulls fly each, and how a match works.

E Voyages: the story (port, lanes, places, meetings, home), the squad of three and one Ark, marked In development.

F Pilots: the Pilot Codex: all 18 callsigns, filtered by what they see, each with its scene. G Studio: blog, the team and contact.

Tumult and the background

Tumult stays one smaller page behind the switch: hero, the world, how it plays, Press Start. It still says it is in beta and under live development.

No flat colour behind the content. Cosmic Shore: the hero bleeds down the page as a blurred, theme-tinted nebula, glowing skim trails with light pulses run down it, prisms drift and spin, streaks fly past, and a grid floor runs under the vessels.

Tumult: its shore, blurred and tinted gold, with tide lines rolling across, contour rings turning and dice drifting. Both follow the theme and stop with Motion Off.

How the codex stays current

The Fleet Codex is already rebuilt from the game (fleet_codex.py, refreshed from bleeding-edge). The site hosts the codex's own generated files at froglet.games/fleet-codex/, copied by the same refresh, so the site and the codex artifact never differ.

The Arcade and Arena pages read the codex's fleet.json, so a new game or vessel appears on the site the day the codex has it. The pilot list would come from the pilot spec the same way.

In this mockup the codex views are captures of the live codex (in Photic); on the site it is the real page.
