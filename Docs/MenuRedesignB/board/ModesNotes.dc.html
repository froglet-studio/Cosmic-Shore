Item 8 - what changes

One shell, four windows

  • Arena, Toy Box and Mission take the Arcade layout from item 3: the shared party panel on the left (party, invites, online list) and the content on the right, under the standard header with Back.
  • Back closes through the window like gamepad B (ModalWindowCloseButton), so the Window Out animation plays; it used to deactivate the window and navigate HOME.

Arena

  • Four cards, so they draw large: art, name, the objective line, the pilot and domain counts, and EVERY hull the card seats as a strip of class tiles (the old card showed only the first hull, so a multi-hull card did not read as one).
  • Content found: Astro League's description says 'Rhino only' while the card lists three hulls.

Toy Box

  • Today's activity and Shuffle become the two feature cards. The activity is drawn in the section 10.6 Free / CTA state (green glow, CTA 'Try it +10') until claimed, then owned (lavender Done, 'New activity in 7h 12m').
  • Toy cards carry the toy's accent as a 3 px strip, the codex portrait, the group (Pilot / World / Creation, which no label showed) and the description. Unavailable toys read Inactive, with a label - the overlay was unwired.
  • Four columns, so all seven toys fit without scrolling at 1920; the grid gets KitScrollIntoView anyway.

Toy configure

  • Same frame as the game configure window: preview left, the toy's text under it, and the variations on the right as slivered tiles (selected: Light border, Neutral Dark fill). Branch rows end in a chevron; inside a branch a Back button and a breadcrumb replace the '< Back' row.
  • The commit button is the opaque CTA with the toy's verb (Switch, Start, Resume, Spawn); it is inactive until a row is picked and hidden for Domain Changer, where a tap applies. Navigate keeps its place; its A glyph goes, because the binding fired on every A press.
  • B steps out of a branch before it closes the window. Today the close button's own B binding closes the whole window even inside a branch.

Revision 2 (motion and fill)

  • Every window opens with the panels rising in and the cards following on the 40 ms stagger; the party panel sits on the blueprint grid with corner brackets and its online dot breathes.
  • Toy Box: today's activity glows (state glow: free reward) with a live 'Ends in 7h 12m 41s', the painting pans, 'Try it +10' carries the sweep; Shuffle spins its icon and cycles through candidate results before landing; toy portraits float on the grid.
  • Arena: card art pans slowly and each card's hull strip pops in tile by tile.
  • Toy configure: the toy portrait floats and sways (the game's preview orbits at 12 deg/s), the variation tiles rise in, the panel takes the grid and brackets, and the commit button carries the sweep.

Mission

  • Restyled with its one card, 'Protect Mission'. The hub button is still Unavailable, so nothing reaches it; the card also cannot be wired as things stand (an SO_MissionList, not an SO_GameList).

For you to decide
