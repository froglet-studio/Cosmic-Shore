Rev 27 - the Port
Asked
Open the Port with one tab, Faction messages: a chat for the faction. Factions will be clans later; today every player is in one faction (Shombith, 2026-10-27). Then: the weekly leaderboard can go to the Port too, for now.
Where
PORT is the top bar's last tab (CODEX, HANGAR, HOME, ARK, PORT). Its lock is lifted, so LT / RT reach it. Two tabs inside: FACTION MESSAGES and WEEKLY LEADERBOARD, stepped with LB / RB like the Codex chapters.
Faction messages
One faction, All Pilots, with its card, the three rules and the controls on the left; the feed (the last 50 messages, newest at the foot) and the message field on the right. Your own messages carry a blue bar and YOU. Enter or X sends; A on the field starts typing (the on-screen keyboard on a phone or pad).
How it works
Unity Vivox text chat (decided 2026-10-27): one text-only Vivox channel per faction (faction_all today), joined while the Port is open and left when it closes. Opening reads the channel's last 50 messages from Vivox's history; new ones are pushed live. Vivox signs in with the UGS player id, so reports and mutes name the same pilot everywhere. The game holds the rules: 140 characters, one message every 3 s, control characters removed.
Safety
Y on a message (or a click) opens Report message and Mute. Report goes to UGS Moderation (verbal abuse), the queue the profile popup's Report already uses, and carries the Vivox channel as context. Mute hides a pilot on this device only. An account whose recorded birth year says it may be under 13 can read but not send (proposed default; the age screen records the year). Colours: your own messages are marked in neutral, never the selection blue; problems are fire, never gold.
Weekly leaderboard
The same board as the weekly challenge's LEADERBOARD window (World / Regional / Friends, podium, your best run, rewards), drawn as a Port tab with no BACK. The configure window keeps its own LEADERBOARD button.
Not in the game yet
Factions of your own (clans): everyone is in All Pilots, and the faction id is a field so a second faction is data, not code. No word filter: reports and mute are the tools for now. Messages are sample text; pilot names and avatars are the leaderboard board's.
Decisions for you
1. The faction's name (All Pilots is a placeholder). 2. Under-13 accounts: read-only (drawn), or no Port chat at all. 3. A standard Vivox text channel seats about 200 at once; past that, ask Vivox for large text channels or split the faction into rooms. 4. Vivox must be switched on for the project in the UGS dashboard before messages work; until then the feed shows Offline.
