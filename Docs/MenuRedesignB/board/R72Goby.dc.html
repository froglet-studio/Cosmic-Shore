Upright

Flying the Goby, hold the phone upright: the screen turns and you are in the Pistol's side view, the Goby in the small square (its AI flies it). Turn back to landscape: the Goby again, the Pistol in the square.

On a phone only, in a game or the Toybox, through the crew's own seat change (GobyPistolDuo.TakeSeat, the same move as the swap). A seat picked with the swap or a double tap stays until the phone turns again.

GameCanvas lays out upright for it and fades the same way (out, 2 frames, 0.28 s in). The Pistol's buttons sit round the right thumb as they already do (TouchButtonLayout re-lays on a resize).

The Pistol is its model's render; the plane, the trail's blocks and the ring are DRAWN here - the game renders them from the real prisms of the Goby's trail.
