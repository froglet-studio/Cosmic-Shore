"On changing the vessel will the model behind the image change since this is a video so should be put actual vessel models inside and check?"

Today: no

Each Squads clip (one per colour set and domain, section 190) has the Dolphin, the Sparrow and the Serpent rendered into it (1). Changing a vessel changes the words, never the picture.

The fix, checked

Render the same hangar with the pads empty (2) and draw the squad's real models on the pads live, from the same camera (3). Laid over the video (as in the game) it matches the Rhino rendered into the room in one pass (4) to within 2 of 255 per pixel on average.

In the game: the empty-pad clips replace the nine Squads clips; a camera at the render's camera draws each seat's vessel prefab on its pad into a transparent layer over the video, rebuilt only when a seat's vessel changes; offline hulls powered down, open pads empty.

Still to match in Unity: the hulls' light (the room's lamps and pad glow as real lights) and a little haze over them. Renders here use stand-in pictures on the wall boards and windows.
