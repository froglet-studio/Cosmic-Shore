Item 5 - what changes (revision 2)

Profile screen

  • Pilot card on the blueprint grid: the avatar (opens the avatar picker), the name at Display 48, Change avatar / Rename, and a pilot record - crystals, games completed, flight time, sessions - rising in with the card (Q6: drawn now; say if it should go).
  • Episodes is a full-height locked card: hex lock tile, 'Arrive with the full release'. A press shakes it (std token) with the Denied sting and the toast.
  • Quests: an overall progress bar, the Jade rail with the travelling spark on reached segments, and five cards that each list their real goals - 'Unlock intensity 3: Win a Joust match at Intensity 2' - with done / next / to-do marks; the Vessel Hangar lists the four quests it needs.
  • States: Completed (art dimmed, lavender tag), Ready to claim (green glow pulsing - state glow - and a CTA Claim with the sweep), In progress (intensity meter, Play), Locked (grey, lock, 'Unlocks after ...').
  • Claim is one of section 7's two ceremony moments: the card pops (500 ms OutBack), the rail fills, the toast reads 'Quest Complete!'.

Profile window

  • One window, two tabs (Avatar / Display name, section 10.8 labels; the Jade underline slides). A preview column on the left shows the avatar and name as others see them, and the party-slot chip.
  • Avatar: the 18 icons rise in (40 ms stagger, capped at 8); 'Saved to your profile.' confirms a pick.
  • Display name: the section 10.2 field with a live character count, the four rules ticking as you type, the danger strip dropping in for an error, and a spinning hex while the name is checked. Submit carries the sweep once the name is valid; Enter submits.

Open questions

  • Q5 quest art (mode card art instead of the legacy stills), Q6 the pilot record, and the 'HEX RACE' name, as before.
