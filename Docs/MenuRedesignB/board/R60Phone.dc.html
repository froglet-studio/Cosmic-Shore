Quests
One row of cards at the phone's own text size, 220 x 318, three in view and the fourth peeking, so the row reads as more. The arrows (and a swipe) step it; the two dashes say which half you see.
Each card: the mode's art and number, its state, the title, a two-line description, the two goals with a tick or a diamond, and ONE button: Claim (Lime, a call to action) when done, Play quest (Jade) with the intensity bars, or Locked.
The Lime line over the row is the episode's progress (as the PC track).
Tabs move up into the header (as built); Back stays on the right; nothing under the rail or the notch.
