The fourM1 Frame + Light trail, M2 Frame + Hero shot, M3 Frame + Diorama, M4 Frame + Chase cam.Each is the acting card from Rev 130 or 131 with a real frame of the game at the back of its picture, graded so the vessel and its act stay the brightest thing on the card.
The framesThey are the same game frames S1 uses, real but not yet one per mode. A pick means capturing a frame per mode at intensity 2 (the look sheet, /unity-look), framed so its centre is where the act happens.
In the gameOne frame image per mode under the acting layer: a path or a scale, one hull sprite, one prop and a bloom. The frame moves by a transform only (push in, zoom, parallax), so hover costs no more than the acting card alone.Touch keeps the rest state, as every card does.
