REV 55 · NOTES
The four menu screens on a phone, drawn from what ships
WHY THIS ROUND
  • Rev 52's Home, Arcade, Configure and Hangar were drawn from the older Rev 21 boards, not from the screens that ship. These four are drawn from the built screens (Home, Arcade, Configure, Hangar boards; their generators) with their real content.
  • The frame and the rules are Rev 52's: an iPhone sideways, 974 x 450 units, the mobile type column, 52-unit controls, the colours of the style guide.
WHAT EACH KEEPS AND WHAT MOVES
  • Home: everything it has - the pilot chip (Online, crystals; Go offline? under it), Up next, the party invite, the hint, the four hubs (Mission dimmed). The five nav tabs (Codex, Hangar, Home, Ark, Port) become the left rail; the friends orb and Settings sit at its foot.
  • Arcade: the weekly challenge and Maelstrom cards, then all 28 games, four across, scrolling. The party panel becomes a sheet from the Party button (its molecule as a row of four seats); Replays and Back stay in the header.
  • Configure: the picture, objective and briefing on the left; the class, intensity I-IV, the three teams with + AI, and Start game (Ruby) on the right. The controls list moves into a sheet; Leaderboard stays in the header. The Arena window swaps the class for the hull strip and Claim (Jade: a pick you can change); Maelstrom swaps it for its pool the same way.
  • Hangar: the vessel list on the left; the vessel, its words and Unlock (Gold, a spend) on the right. Abilities move into a sheet; the spend window is unchanged.
WHAT CHANGES IN UNITY (once approved)
  • author_menu_topnav.py: the rail (the nav band's layout on a phone).
  • author_menu_home.py, author_menu_arcade.py, author_menu_configure.py, author_menu_hangar.py: each screen's phone layout (PhoneRect), its sheet and its phone-only buttons.
  • author_menu_phone.py: each built screen leaves the fallback list; the rest draw as today until their own boards.
  • Then Menu_Main's Has Phone Layout goes on.
