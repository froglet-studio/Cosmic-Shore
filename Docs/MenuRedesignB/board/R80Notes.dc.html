Rev 80 - B Logbook, taken further
Asked"B looks good make it better more mockus inspired from B" (Shombith, 2026-11-09, on Rev 79).
Kept from BThe order: the voyages as a list on the left, the picked voyage's course in the middle with the edge view under it, the squad on the right, one Set sail. The tabs as large words. The vessel grid: one press picks.
B1 Refined (recommended)Each row shows its course, who brought it and its numbers, so a voyage is recognised at a glance. Each seat shows its pilot beside the hull. The maps' colour is turned down so the course reads first.
B2 Wide seaThe course map takes most of the window with the voyage written over it; the squad is a bar along the foot.
B3 CrewThe squad leads: three tall seat cards, the pilot and the hull large; the course is smaller.
B4 QuietThe calmest: the list is words only, the maps muted, the most space between things.
PilotsThe seats use the four pilot portraits on the canvas (Shrapnel, Feist, Longshift, Flux).
