Rev 13 - what changes
Top bar
Four tabs: Hangar, Ark, Home, Port. The Profile tab and its screen go.
Profile
Opens from the pilot card on Home (click, or Y on a pad) as a window over Home with Back. Rev 10's style, with Episodes and Quests taken out: the pilot card takes the full width and gains Wins, Crystals and Best streak; Favourites and Fly time by vessel share the middle; the Audio Studio and Now playing sit at the bottom.
Voyage
Replaces Mission on the Home buttons and in the hub strip (Toy Box, Arcade, Arena, Voyage). It holds the episode, its quest track, and the voyages the quests lead to. Tandava (PR 1546, now on bleeding-edge) is Voyage 1 and is open from the start, with its own card art; on bleeding-edge its card is parked in _Archive and on no roster, so the Voyage window becomes its home. The Protect mission becomes Voyage 2, opened after Tandava.
Sea
Brighter: a sky-to-periwinkle gradient along every line, layers about 50 % stronger, and the veil a gradient (deep blue at the top to near-black at the bottom, a soft glow up top). Still 95 % opaque and the same slow 90 / 68 / 45 s.
