ZODIACIvelia StarreachHuman / Ocelot / HeronSaltmark Consortium · From Starwake
Story

Ivelia Starreach was apprenticed to a cartographer in Starwake, and now keeps the chart house of a trade barge crossing the HyperSea.

The Saltmark Consortium charts the trade lanes and sells passage along them. Fly with Ivelia and the Consortium will give your crew dispatches to carry, and convoys to bring to the ports its lanes have left short.

The charts are never finished. Neither is the work.
