Friends tray: the party and the rows
Board · Rev 39 · Notes
Asked (Shombith, 2026-10-31)
  • "Change the UI of friends: just the look and the center of the branch like animation."
  • "The invite and other buttons overlap."
  • "Keep the feature where it extends as a tray into game cards."
What every option changes against the build
  • The party molecule (leader in the centre, three seats bonded at 155 / 25 / 270 degrees, flowing Bond dashes) is gone, and so is the orb's turning orbit. No idle motion: the only movement is the tray sliding.
  • Each online row is a fixed grid: avatar, name and status (ellipsized), then fixed action slots. A button never takes its width from its label again, so INVITE and SPECTATE can no longer overflow the 202 strip.
  • The tray keeps docking: open, it pushes the hub window's cards right (FriendsDrawerDock); closed, the cards centre again. Same anchoring under the nav, same 520 width.
  • Kept: seating order (host first, PartyRoster), the domain colour per seat, Leave, the Invites list with Accept / Decline, the host-only remove.
Why A (recommended)
  • It answers both complaints with the least: one row of seats reads at a glance, and one verb per row is the only layout that cannot overlap at any label length or language.
  • The row's state already decides the verb (Invite, Join, Spectate, Cancel), so a second button was only ever a second way to be wrong.
  • B keeps two verbs where a pilot is in another party (Join or Invite), at the cost of a narrower name. C is the most visual, but shows four online pilots before scrolling, not five.
What the build would touch (after a pick)
  • author_menu_friends_drawer.py: the seats, the handle (replaces the orb), the rows' slot widths; its gates grow a no-overlap check per row state.
  • ArcadeLobbyList / KitPartySlot: seat layout only; seating and presence facts unchanged. The Presence and Bond shaders leave the drawer.
  • OnlineInfoEntry / KitOnlineRow: one action slot (A) chosen from the row's state; no logic change to invite, join, spectate or cancel.
  • Board to check: press Tray open / Tray closed on A, B and C to see the cards move with the tray.
