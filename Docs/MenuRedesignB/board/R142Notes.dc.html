Asked (Shombith, 2026-11-24)“I want to redesign the toybox UI in a more innovative way, spawning or switching or navigating should have animations and the whole cards (bg is good) orientation inside each main card and the preview screen with a navigate animation which takes you inside the preview screen and spawns you on a cool way. Create 4 different UI variations for this.”Kept: the card background (navy, the sea lines, the toy’s codex portrait), the seven toys, their words, Toy configure’s variations and its two actions (the toy’s verb, Navigate).Changed in every option: how a card lays out its words and portrait, and five moments that are cuts today: open, switch, the verb, Navigate, and arriving in the world.Every option board is playable: open a toy, pick a variation, press its verb, then Navigate. “Board: start again” (bottom left) goes back to the Toy Box.
The fourA Portal. Tall cards dealt in a row. Pointing lifts a card and slides the verb up. Open grows the card into the preview on the left (the Configure layout picked in Rev 33 / 43). Navigate: you fly through the toy’s ring and your vessel streaks in from the horizon.B Orbit. The toys stand on a ring around you; LT / RT turns it, and only the card facing you has words. Open flips the card. The preview turns like a cube; the verb builds the thing from prisms; Navigate is a warp and your vessel is built from prisms.C Slide open. Seven strips, names up their spines; the one you point at slides open (Rev 117’s “cards that slide open”). The strip widens into the window, a diagonal wipe switches, petals bloom on the verb, and the window splits into slats as you leave.D Lens (recommended). Each card is a lens onto the cell with the toy inside. Open irises the lens into a big round preview with the variations on a dial; switching blinks the lens; Navigate grows the lens past the screen and you rise out of a ripple. It is the only one where “inside the preview” is literal: the same circle carries you from card to world, and the ripple matches the avatar ring picked in Rev 127.
What each costs in UnityAll four are UI tweens (DOTween / the kit’s KitMotion) on RectTransforms, CanvasGroups and one mask; nothing runs at rest but the portrait bob already shipped (Card_PortraitBob.mat).A one scale + position tween from the card’s rect to the preview; the dive scales the preview RawImage to full screen while the camera pushes toward the station ring.B seven cards on a 3D ring (one rotation tween; world-space or a perspective canvas). The prism bursts are a pooled UI particle set. The most work of the four.C a HorizontalLayoutGroup with preferred-width tweens; the slats are eight Images; the petals are one sprite turned five times.D one circular mask whose radius is tweened (a SoftMask or a cutout shader on the preview RawImage); the dial is a fixed set of six anchors; the ripples are one sprite.Navigate in every option is the UI handing over to the camera: the preview camera (ToyPreviewCamera) blends into the gameplay camera at the station, and the vessel’s arrival is the spawn effect at that point. The board fakes both with the cell frame.Built only after a pick, through /frogma-to-unity on Toy configure and the Toy Box generators (author_menu_toybox.py, author_menu_toyconfigure.py).
