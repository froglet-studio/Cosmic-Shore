Item 6 - what changes (revision 2)

Kept

  • The live menu cell stays full-bleed behind everything, with the translucent nav over it. The four hubs keep their order and targets; A opens the Arcade, X opens Settings, LT / RT page, a tap on the cell starts freestyle.

Revision 2

  • Hub row: each hub carries an icon (Toy Box cube, Arcade pad, Arena crossed blades, Mission target). Arcade is Home's one opaque button (section 10.1) with the Play gradient, the A keycap and the highlight sweep; Mission is Inactive (Unavailable). Hubs rise in on a 40 ms stagger and show the CTA focus ring.
  • Up next (new, Q10): two cards above the hubs - the weekly challenge (Skim Race, 'Finish the race', a live 'Ends in' countdown, your best time) and the current quest (Joust, its intensity meter, the next goal). They open the configure window and the Profile screen. Both read data the game already has.
  • Pilot chip: avatar, name, Online / Offline (wired), crystal balance; the online lamp breathes. The lamp asks 'Go offline?' / 'Go online?' with a line on what that changes, Yes / No straddling the edge.
  • Party invite drops in under Settings: the host with a CTA ring, the party count and its members, Join / Decline, and a Jade bar that drains over the 3 s the invite stays up.
  • 'Tap the cell to fly' breathes at the centre so freestyle can be found (new, Q11). The settings gear turns slowly.

Open questions

  • Q7 avatar and name open the profile window; Q10 the Up next cards; Q11 the freestyle hint; the 3 s invite and its missing pad binding, as before.
