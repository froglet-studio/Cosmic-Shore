Asked (Shombith)

"Design the new voyage page after taking in Garrett's new PR. On the left the whole map preview and the edge on view below, opaque, high res. Each voyage is given to you by your crew; the card has 3 levels: the voyage (title, description, waypoints, the preset), the configuration (the 12 settings, a separate level), and the story (optional). Saga and Episodes keep their content, with LB and RB."

Shared by all six

The map and the edge view are the Voyage Board's own render at 2x (Neighbourhood tier, the 20 ly cells, Tumult to Lantern Reach), drawn opaque. Level 1: title, the crew's summary, who found it, course / sail / intensity / pays / finds / odds, the course cell by cell, the waypoints, and Your Ark: the Arks you configured (Skim Wing, Breakers) to choose from, or Configure. Level 2: three seats x vessel, pilot, element, artifact = the 12 settings, a preset switcher and Save as Ark. Level 3: who brought it, the place, the news, the water - optional. A Set sail, X Ark crew, Y Story, B back.

Every word is the slice's or the Voyage Board's (voyage_slice.json job.hunt.sighting, the crew's line, the place's blurb, the seed news).

Recommendation: A, the drawer

The map and edge view never move or shrink at any level; the card slides in like the arena preview's tray, each level is one press deeper, and the spines show where you are and take you back. B keeps the tray literally but hides two thirds of the map at levels 2 and 3. C and E are the quietest; E reads best with a pad. D gives the 12 settings the most room but covers the edge view. F shows brief and crew together at the cost of a narrow map.
