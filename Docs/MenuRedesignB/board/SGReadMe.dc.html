Frogma style guide · start here

One look for everything Froglet makes

The rules every new screen, board, deck and page is checked against. The guide in words is Docs/UI_STYLE_GUIDE.md; the colours come from Garrett's colour chart (colour_domains.json) and the game's UITheme.

Rule 1

Colour is information

Six colour families, one meaning each. Nothing is coloured to look nice.

Rule 2

Elements have no colour

Charge, Mass, Space and Time are told by shape and motion, never by a hue.

Rule 3

One shape

Two opposite corners cut. Nothing is rounded. Hexagons only for icon tiles and handles.

Rule 4

Still unless it means something

The sea lines and the card drift are the only idle motion.

Rule 5

Every word is authored

All text a player reads lives in a field a person can edit.

Rule 6

Shared prefabs stay shared

Change the prefab or make a variant. Never park overrides in a scene.
