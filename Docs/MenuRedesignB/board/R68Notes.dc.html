The Fleet Codex for the website, four looks

Asked: the move should not be below the fold, nicer type, every animation smooth, game filters and the vessel's story. In all four, picking an ability plays its move beside it: a small scene of it happening, with the controller button, key and phone touch pressing on the same 3.2 s clock (passive abilities show the thumbs just flying). Only transform and opacity move, on an eased curve. Games filter by All, each element, Arcade and Arena. The story is the Squirrel's class asset text. Colours: the Nocturne set; the other two sets swap in as themes.

A Showroom (recommended)Unbounded + SoraThe fleet as lit hull cards, six across; the window puts the vessel, its five abilities and the picked one's move side by side, so the move plays the moment you pick, with no scroll. Recommended: the clearest website page, and the move is the biggest thing on it.
B Trading cardsOxanium + ManropeEvery hull a collectible card with its number; the abilities are five cards that rise when picked, the move plays in a strip under them. The most playful; the most to read.
C Flight deckSaira Condensed + SairaReads like the game: a big selected hull on the fleet page, the window as a HUD with the four-icon row bottom right, the briefing, missions and the move as glass panels over the flying hull.
D EditorialSpace Grotesk + InterA magazine: the fleet as a big-type index where the hull slides in on hover; the window as a spread with the story as a pull quote and each ability a line.

Type departs from the style guide (Aldrich and Chakra Petch) on purpose: the pick decides the codex's type. A longer story per vessel needs a writer; the boards use only what the class asset says.
