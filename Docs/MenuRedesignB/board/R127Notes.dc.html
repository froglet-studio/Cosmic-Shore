What the round decides
The problem

The domain halo is a round glow 1.22x the avatar's rect (PartySlotDomainGlow in the party panel, ConnectingPlayerRoster and RematchVoteRoster in matches). The avatar art is square, so its corners stand outside the circle and its border is a square: nothing follows the glow.

What every option shares

The avatar is masked to a circle. The ring sits on that circle's edge. The halo is centred on the same point at 1.3x (up from 1.22, so the glow has room outside the ring) and starts at full colour exactly at the ring. A pilot whose domain is not known yet gets no halo and a quiet 2 px line ring, as today's "draws only what it knows" rule.

Recommended: A Breath

It keeps the motion the game already has (alpha only, 0.7 Hz, the halo's own breath) and makes the ring part of it, so it is the quietest answer and costs one ring sprite. B and D are the next choices if the party should feel more alive; C, E and F are louder and read best large, at 150 px and up.

Motion per option
  • A Breath: Alpha only, 0.7 Hz, ring and halo in phase. Nothing moves or turns.
  • B Orbit: One arc, one turn every 4 s, linear.
  • C Dial: Outer segments one turn in 18 s, inner in 26 s the other way.
  • D Ripple: Each wave 2.4 s, eased out, scale 1.0 to 1.3 (the halo's size).
  • E Current: The gradient turns once every 3 s; the glow travels with its bright part.
  • F Corona: One turn every 12 s, linear; the halo is brighter on the crescent's side.
What the pick changes in Unity

One round mask on the avatar Image, one ring Image over it, the halo resized to 1.3x and its sprite starting at the ring's radius. The three halo call sites become one component (Docs/PartySystem/TODOS.md TODO-12), so the party panel, the connecting roster and the rematch vote all get the same border. Colours are the style tokens' Jade, Ruby and Gold.
