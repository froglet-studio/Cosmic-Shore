Rev 34 notes - the captain guides the first session
Asked. "Create FTUE mockups - look at the old design; the captain/pilot should come from the side and guide the players."
The old design (Rev 21). The same six steps (Home, Arcade, Configure, Lesson, first race, first finish) were guided by a caption strip under the lit control (FIRST FLIGHT - 1 OF 4, Press A). No one spoke: the guide, the Lesson and the Mentor were text. The Mentor's face was an open question for Shombith and Caleb.
What changes. A person guides: the captain of your hull (Trace, the Squirrel's pilot - the first hull) enters from the side, says one line, and leaves when you press. The path is unchanged and the LOCKED rule holds: one control lit in lime, everything else dimmed and dead except Settings, and the player presses it. The captain never presses, never skips ahead, never navigates.
The side rule. She enters from the side AWAY from the lit control, so she never covers it: left for Home, Arcade, the race and the finish; right for Configure and the Lesson. Her entrance is the one motion of the step; nothing idles.
Recommended: A (Wingmate). It is the clearest 'a pilot is here with you', and the leader line ties her words to the control without a second spotlight. B is the fallback for the match (it covers least), and the in-match steps could use B even if A is picked for the menu.
For the build. The words go in the guide asset (MainQuest / the Lesson seed), one line per step, with the captain picked from the hull's pilot roster (a portrait the roster already has). The dialogue system (DialogueSpeaker, MainMenuDialogueView) carries speaker and line today; the side and entrance are new view work. Open: the captain's name and voice (Shombith, Caleb), and whether the Mentor's tips in the preview become hers too.
