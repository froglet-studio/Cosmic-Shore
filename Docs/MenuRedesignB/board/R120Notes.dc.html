Rev 120 notes
The Ark wears its numbersA lit plate over the hull, in the world: crystals aboard, the hull as 16 lit segments, and one state line (+7 to bank, Sails in 5, Under attack, Leaking, Goal met). It is a game object riding the Ark, so it scales with distance and hides behind things like the Ark does.
Three moodsSea blue at rest, red while the Ark is hit or leaking, lime when the goal is met. All three come from the colour set (domain-free: the Ark's own colours).
The chip, top leftThe same numbers in one line (crystals, carried, hull, clock), for when the Ark is behind you or far away. On Stray it gets an outline.
ToastsA feed under the chip, 20 / 16, sliding in and out. The centre of the screen stays clear for flying.
Escort and the way homeOn an escort the plate's number is the hull (it is the score); on the way home it is what is aboard.
UnchangedPause and the end board keep the shared windows at normal sizes. Threat marks stay ArkAttackerMarks in the world. The vessel HUD keeps its corner.
To buildVoyageHud's objective box becomes the chip and the toast feed; a world-space plate parented to the Ark (billboarded, lit like the Ark, colour-set colours) shows the rest. Rev 118's parked code is replaced.
