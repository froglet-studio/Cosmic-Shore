Rev 78 Follow - Follow the turn (recommended)

Every piece travels from its landscape place to its upright place WHILE the phone turns, and stays level in your hand the whole way: the nav band slides to the top, the cards stretch to the width, the four hubs fold into a 2 x 2. In the game the Goby's view shrinks into the small square as the Pistol's view grows out of it, in step with the turn.

How it is built: the screen does not let the OS turn it. It reads the phone's tilt (the accelerometer the touch tilt already uses), and each PortraitRect blends its landscape pose into its upright pose by the angle, with the canvas turned against the tilt so it stays level. At the end it sets the orientation itself. Risk: whether iOS and Android then swap without their own rotation animation - a one-day prototype on a device answers it.

A drawing of the motion, not the game. The way back to landscape plays the same in reverse.
