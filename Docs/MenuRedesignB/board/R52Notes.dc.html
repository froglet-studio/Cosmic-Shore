REV 52 · NOTES
Phone layouts: one design per screen, drawn at the phone scale
THE FRAME
  • An iPhone 14 sideways: 844 x 390 pt. The canvas matches height, so 450 units tall and 974 wide. Each board is that screen at 2x.
  • Safe area: 54 units on each side (the island is drawn on the left), 24 at the bottom (the home bar). The dashed hairline is its edge.
  • An iPad (820 pt tall) and every PC keep the 1920 x 1080 boards: nothing here changes them.
THE RULES EVERY BOARD KEEPS
  • Type: H1 24, H2 20, H3 16, Body 16, Button 16, Button small and Label 12 (STYLE_FOUNDATION 4, Mobile).
  • Every control is at least 52 units (45 pt) tall; the big actions 56.
  • Width is the room a phone has: the four tabs become a left rail, columns sit side by side, nothing stacks under a fold.
  • One toast at a time. Side panels open as sheets from the bottom (Configure's controls).
  • Colours as the style guide: Lime calls to action, Jade is the pick and focus, Ruby is no way back (START GAME, Play again), Gold is crystals.
WHAT EACH BOARD CHANGES IN UNITY (once picked)
  • Sign-in: author_start_flow.py, one right-hand column over the art.
  • Home: author_menu_hub_nav.py, a phone rail instead of the top band; the pilot chip, friends and one toast at the top; the four modes alike.
  • Arcade: author_menu_arcade.py, a 4 x 2 grid of 186 x 165 cards.
  • Configure: author_menu_configure.py, two columns; the ability list moves into a sheet.
  • Hangar: author_menu_hangar.py, the hull large and the fleet as chips.
  • In game: author_ingame_hud.py, the four-icon row as 64-unit thumb buttons (the LOCKED structure, bigger), the race facts in one top-centre plate, the left half a steer zone whose stick starts where the thumb lands.
  • The finish: author_ingame_hud.py, the Scoreboard as one column and two buttons.
  • Then Has Phone Layout goes on: GameCanvas once the HUD and finish are built, Menu_Main once all its screens are.
DECIDED (SHOMBITH, 2026-11-05)
  • The four tabs are a left rail (a 52-unit top band would take 12 % of the height).
  • The four modes on Home look alike: no one of them is Lime.
  • Steering: the stick starts wherever the left thumb lands on the left half.
