Chapter01The story

Leave theplanet.Chase glory.

As a native of the planet Tumult you are one of the most adaptive creatures in the known universe. With a burning curiosity for the unexplored, a healthy sense of adventure, and a strong competitive streak you’ve left the planet to chase glory and fortune on the galaxy’s largest space station. Here, the greatest pilots and biggest names in the Milky Way compete in a series of simple but exhilarating contests. Show off the finest flying in the Galaxy, or have the most fun trying!
