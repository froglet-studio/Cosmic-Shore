REV 53 · NOTES
The HUD on Garrett's touch scheme; sign-in as it really is
WHY THIS ROUND
  • Rev 52's Hud drew a stick and four tap buttons. Phones already fly on Garrett's touch scheme (Docs/ABILITY_LOCKUP.md, On a phone; Docs/Reports/MOBILE_VESSELS.md 8).
  • Shombith, 2026-11-05: "What garett has for phone controls that should be the one." The floating stick is withdrawn.
  • Rev 52's SignIn drew only Play as guest. The real chooser (Rev 9, R9Phones) also offers Apple and Google, and as one column it is taller than a phone.
THE HUD ON A PHONE
  • Thumb-lifts hulls (Squirrel, Dolphin, Manta, Rhino, Tiger Moth, Thresher and the crews): both thumbs fly anywhere; lifting one fires its card. The row stays, and each lift card shows its glyph (touch_glyph_art.py). Those cards are not buttons.
  • Totem-buttons hulls (Urchin, Sparrow, Serpent, Grizzly, Scarab, Shrike, Brittle Star, Stoat, Angler): the cards are the buttons, laid round the right thumb by TouchButtonLayout in a 36 % x 55 % corner, 10 mm each, down to 7 mm. Cards that press nothing step down to a 6 mm strip.
  • Each hull's scheme is its row in author_touch_profiles.py. The glyphs here are stand-ins for the real art.
WHAT CHANGES FROM THESE
  • The HUD's own layout only: the race facts in one top-centre plate and pause top left (author_ingame_hud.py). TouchControlsHud, TouchButtonLayout and the profiles are not touched.
  • Sign-in: two columns on a phone, the welcome left and the chooser right (author_start_flow.py). iPhone puts Apple first, Android Google first. The provider buttons are their own art, untouched (drawn here as stand-ins).
STILL STANDING FROM REV 52
  • Compare, Home (the rail, the four modes alike), Arcade, Configure, Hangar, Finish: approved, unchanged.
  • Rev 52's Hud and SignIn stay on the board as the record; these two replace them.
