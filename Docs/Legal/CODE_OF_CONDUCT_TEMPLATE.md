Cosmic Shore is built for fair, friendly play. By playing online you agree to these rules.

## The rules

1. **Play the game as it was made.** No cheats, hacks, modified game files, memory editors,
   speed changers, or tools that change what the game sends to our servers.
2. **Play yourself.** No bots, scripts, macros or automation that play for you, and no farming
   rewards, currency or rankings with them.
3. **Earn what you get.** No exploiting bugs to gain scores, currency, unlocks or rankings.
   Report a bug you find instead.
4. **One of you.** No making accounts to dodge a measure on another account, and no selling,
   buying or sharing accounts.
5. **Respect other players.** No hate speech, slurs, harassment, threats, or sexual content,
   in display names or anywhere players can see. No impersonating Froglet staff.
6. **Finish what you start.** Don't repeatedly quit matches, go idle, or sabotage your own team.

## What we do about it

We use automated checks and player reports, and a person reviews before anything serious
(see the privacy policy, §5a). Depending on the rule and how often it is broken, we may:

- place your account in **separate matchmaking** with other flagged accounts;
- keep your scores off **public leaderboards**;
- **withhold rewards** earned by breaking a rule;
- **reset** an offensive display name;
- **restrict ranked and public play** for a period.

These can start without advance notice. **None of them removes access to anything you paid
for**: you can always play what you bought on your own and with AI teammates. Threats to anyone's safety may be reported to the authorities.

## Notice and appeal

When a measure applies to your account we tell you which rule it concerns and how long it lasts.
For anything you wrote, we tell you straight away. You can appeal at
**[APPEALS CONTACT / IN-GAME APPEAL]**; a person reviews every appeal and replies within
**[e.g. 14 days]**. If the appeal succeeds, the measure is lifted and the record cleared.

Clean play also clears most measures over time.

## Reporting

Use **Report** next to a player's name to tell us about a rule being broken. False or abusive
reports are themselves a breach of rule 5.
