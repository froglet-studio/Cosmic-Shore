| `BROADSIDE.md` | `_Scripts/Controller/Arcade/` | Broadside technical reference (the ARENA brawl — seven hulls, one score). **Read before scoring any new vessel-vs-vessel weapon, before adding a `CombatHitClass`, or before assuming a hull can land a scoreable hit** — measured on the shipped containers only FOUR of eight could, and it records the three container wirings that fixed it (the Urchin's `projectileShipEffects` was EMPTY), the new `Strike` verb, the skimmer combat-hit reporter and why its AUTHORITY differs from a projectile's (a skimmer overlap is observed on every peer, so an unconditional raise double-credits), and the latch-window model — **a brawl is more balanceable than a race, because the mode owns the windows**. It also owns the fleet's **elemental drain table**, whose rule is now one constant — **TEN POINTS IS ONE PETAL, on every element the hit touches** (a direct rocket strike drains 3 petals each; ten bullets drain 1). Two things make that shippable and both are the interesting half. **PER ELEMENT, not per total** — "three petals in each element" is a statement about each element, so how WIDE an attack spreads its drain became part of what the attack IS rather than something normalised away (the Manta's two-element bloom now bites the same per element as the Dolphin's four and half as much in total). And **a drain lives in one of exactly two places, which must stay DISJOINT**: the classes whose bite is a pure function of price (Bullet, the three missile tiers) drain straight off the HIT REPORT through **`CombatHitDrain`**, because ONE rocket lands in three ranked classes against one victim and a per-blast asset would stack all three — six petals for a thirty-point event, and a base rocket taking a different total from a heavy one for the same hit; `VesselCombatHitLatch` already pays the best tier once and reports what an upgrade SUPERSEDES, so draining from that seam nets the same way the score credits the difference. The classes whose drain carries design a price cannot express — *which* elements (the Manta's Mass+Space) and whether it mirrors as an ally BUFF (the Squirrel's overtake) — stay authored per weapon: Debuff and Strike. `author_combat_debuff_magnitudes.py` (`--check`) owns both, asserts the two sets are disjoint and that the price list `CombatHitDrain` restates matches `BroadsideScoringRule.asset`, and prices the SUSTAINED pressure off each drain's real rate limiter (an asset cooldown, or the LATCH WINDOW for a reporter-driven class). ⚠ **The Debuff anchor MOVED 4.2× lighter** (−0.5 → −0.12), retiring a gate that pinned it: **The Bends and Undertow are scored entirely on that drain** and want a playtest — their SCORING is untouched by construction, since both pay for the hit landing rather than for its depth. ⚠ **Two of this row's statements have since been superseded by `Docs/ELEMENTAL_ECONOMY.md`, which is now the authority on what a drain DOES:** a drain is no longer a decaying debuff but a permanent TRANSFER (this table still sets its SIZE; `ElementalTransfer.FormFor` decides where the petals go — contact verbs steal, ranged verbs eject as collectable crystals), and *"the Rhino's sword is the one scoring verb with no drain path at all"* is closed — it carries `RhinoSwordStealBySkimmerEffect`, a second asset of the Squirrel's own proven overtake type with the new `requireOvertake` flag off, priced identically because a Strike is one VERB and two hulls landing it must take the same petals. This is still the one place to look before hand-editing any `debuffMagnitude`. |
