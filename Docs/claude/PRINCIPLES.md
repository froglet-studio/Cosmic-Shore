Every design rule the four owners have stated, in their own words, with the line it was said on, so a session can
check a design BEFORE building it instead of discovering the rule when the build is retired
(`Docs/SKILL_TRAINING_PLAN.md` 2.3 and 3.3: eight things were built and retired in five weeks, six of them for a rule
that was written down only afterwards).

**Status: every entry is DRAFTED from the record (2026-11-07) and waits on its owner to confirm or strike it.**
Nothing here was confirmed by anyone. An entry's words are verbatim; its rule is one session's reading of them.

- The data: `Docs/claude/principles.json` (271 entries). The tool: `Tools/Build/principles.py`.
- Read your area before you design: `python3 Tools/Build/principles.py --area vessel` (areas: vessel, ui,
  world-look, modes, ecology, ai, multiplayer, camera, audio, prisms, economy, ftue, process, other).
- The gate: `python3 Tools/Build/principles.py --check` fails when an entry's words are no longer within five lines
  of its source line (the record moved; the entry is unfounded), when a LOCKED heading in `CLAUDE.md` or
  `Docs/claude/*.md` has no entry, or when two entries share an id. It passes on 2026-11-07: 271 entries, 0 failures.
- The brief: `Docs/claude/DESIGN_BRIEF.md`, the one page written before a mechanic, a camera behaviour, a mode rule,
  a world effect or a screen is built; it cites entries by id.
- Regenerate the table: `python3 Tools/Build/principles.py --md`.

## How a rule gets here

1. **A dated owner quote** in any doc (`Garrett, 2026-10-18: "..."`): mined on 2026-11-07 from `CLAUDE.md`,
   `Docs/`, and `.claude/skills/`; only entries whose words were found at their source line were kept (272 of 273
   mined, 3 dropped as duplicates).
2. **A LOCKED heading** in `CLAUDE.md` or `Docs/claude/`: `principles.py --seed-locked` adds one for any heading no
   entry covers, so the gate stays complete.
3. **A retirement**: when a thing is retired, its design brief gets one line and this ledger gets the principle that
   was missing (the retrospective rule in `DESIGN_BRIEF.md`). That is how the ledger grows the way a LESSONS file
   grows: from what cost a round.

An owner strikes an entry by deleting it from the json (the gate is happy as long as LOCKED headings stay covered)
and confirms one by changing its `status` from `drafted` to `confirmed`.
