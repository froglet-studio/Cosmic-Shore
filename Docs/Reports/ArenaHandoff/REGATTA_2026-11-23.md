### Meerkat, round 2: autopilot logic that keeps exit x3 (09:00-09:55 UTC; Plan step: time-arcade)
Developed and judged in Burrow Run I1-I4 only (seeds 11-12), then one shuffle race. The patch is unmerged: `proto/meerkat-carry-exit3-autopilot-brake.patch`. It contains the night-3 carry, exit x3, Boost Out x1 and the two changes below.

1. **A bug in the carry prototype.** The way out of a tunnel was capped at exit x the throttle target, and a carry raises that target, so a chained ride left at exit² x cruise. At exit x3 that is 9x, and the Time-5 rush fills the gap. The cap is now exit x plain cruise while a carry is on. With that fix, Burrow Run I2 finishes (140 s), but I4 still times out.
2. **The I4 failure, traced.** The autopilot orbits ring 3 at the carried 180 u/s (3x cruise) for 250 s.
3. **The autopilot brake (AI only).** While the ring it is after is within `aiCarryBrakeDistance` and lies more than `aiCarryBrakeAngle` off the nose (a bad approach, the orbit shape), the autopilot drops the carry and flies plain cruise. A human always keeps the whole carry.
