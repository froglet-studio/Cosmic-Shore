The SOM is not a share of SAM someone picks. The Steam Playtest earns nothing (Valve forbids charging for access in any form), so its whole job is to grow the first input.

How these markets actually pay out

Steam is a hit market

About 20,000 games launched on Steam in 2025. About 300 crossed $1M gross; the median earned about $249.

The Flight tag is narrower still: 85 games have ever crossed $1M on Steam and fewer than 20 reached $10M, with almost no middle class.

Co-op and multiplayer is the exception that pays: co-op games grossed about $8.2B on Steam in 2025 and held 11 of the year's top 20, and multiplayer games supported by streamers keep the longest tails.
