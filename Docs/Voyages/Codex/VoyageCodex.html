Voyage Codex

Every voyage you can sail across the HyperSea: the story behind it, who offers it, the squad they ask for, how a run plays from cast-off to dock, and how it changes from calm water to the abyss. Four voyage types and twelve named voyages sail in Milestone 2; the rest are sealed until later.

Before you sail

The sea, the Ark and the danger

The Ark

She burns crystal to sail

Every squad sails with an Ark: home, hold and engine. She crosses the HyperSea cell by cell, burning elemental crystal, and everything she carries is lost if her hull breaks. Your three vessels fly beside her.

The sea

Cells, membranes and lanes

The HyperSea is a foam of living cells. Places sit at their nuclei; lanes run through the cells between them. A voyage flies exactly the cells on its lane, 1 to 6 of them, and docks at a real place. Pause, and the map shows where your Ark is.

The life

Everything in it is hungry

Fauna graze mass, and the Ark's hull is mass. Cells held by another domain send their creatures at her. Between cells, raiders and the Leviathan's spawn wait in open water.

Difficulty is how dangerous the sea is

A voyage's difficulty is not a timer and not a stepper. It is the water you choose to sail: every voyage card carries a difficulty from 1 to 9, the same scale Escort the Ark has today, and the number says how hostile the cells on its lane are. Every voyage type uses the same scale; the table groups it in pairs to show what changes. Length comes from the lane, so a run lasts 3 to 5 minutes on average, longer on a long lane, never because the rung is higher.

Run length

Measured from the code on 20 Nov, a 4-cell Crystal Run took about 8 minutes to its stop: 30-second anchors in every cell and a slow Ark inside 3000 u cells. To land at 3 to 5 minutes, a cell should take about 45 to 55 seconds, cast-off and docking about 15 seconds each. A 1-cell lane is a 1½-minute sprint; a 6-cell lane is a 6-minute haul.

Milestone 2 · four types, twelve voyages

Choose a voyage

Salvage, Escort, Bounty and Hold the Core are voyage types: the rules a run plays by. Players sail named voyages, each tied to a real place on the map, its zone, its danger and its length. A new voyage is a new name on a lane, not new code.

Sealed until later

Voyages still in the dark

Designed and on the board, locked in Milestone 2 (VoyageToyDefinitionSO.lockJobs). Each opens by adding its job to openJobs once its piece is built.

Lair Hunt · Brisk Amadou

Fell the brood

The Maw is crawling. Cull predators near the Ark until the pack leader rises, then break it. Destroyers and Shepherds. Uses job.hunt.kaiju.

Liberate the Pen · Sable Okoye

Burn the fence

Raze another domain's pen and lead the freed herd to a nursery. Shepherds lead; Wardens hate it.

Courier Race · Merrin Tal

First to Tollmoor

Beat a rival courier to the port through tunnels and slingshots, Ark intact. Pathfinders lead.

Sail Home

Bring her in

Already sails: when the Ark lies away from your harbour, sail the lane home and dock to bank everything owed.
