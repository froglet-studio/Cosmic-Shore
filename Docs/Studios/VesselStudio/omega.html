Omega Crews

The Omega Run: your crew of three collects the course's crystals. Three are lit at a time, so flying apart collects faster. Every third crystal is LOCKED inside a shielded wall that only the omega form's weapon breaks. Hold LT with the crew close to combine; the meter drains while you are combined. Left / right swaps your seat.

Crew

Seats 3 seat · you take one, the AI the rest

A 3-seat vessel (the Orca's rule: every empty seat an AI). Pick the seat you fly and each AI seat's level; swap seats any time with left / right. "Nobody" lets the whole crew fly itself, to watch it.

AI

Each AI body flies for the lit crystal nobody else in the crew is going to. The crew combines when the next crystal is a LOCKED one and the meter has enough for the trip (lab rule, Game Config > Crew numbers: combine at), and splits as soon as no lit crystal is locked. Easy and Medium are Hard plus SkimRaceDifficulty.asset's two mistakes in what each body BELIEVES (late notice, a misjudged crystal) and a lab-only late press.
