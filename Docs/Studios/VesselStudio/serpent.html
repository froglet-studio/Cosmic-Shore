Serpent Studio

Round 2 · Serpent Time, the dirty race

The Serpent's Time game: a ring race where every gate holds several rings, everyone can see them, and any Serpent can plug a ring with a wall. Cloak just past a ring, or stop in it, and a seed wall grows from the loose mass around the gate. Rivals shoot it down with the Sniper Shot, ram through it, or take another ring. Some rings are grown over with prisms, mostly plain at low intensity and more of them danger as it rises: shoot a way through, ram it and risk the danger hit, or find the open ring. The Serpent's kit is exactly the shipped one (the mode never changes the vessel); its numbers are the shipped ones; the race around it is a design (lab-only) for the team to decide.

Thread one ring of every gate, in order; the first Serpent home wins. Plug a ring behind you with a wall (cloak just past it, or stop in it), and get through the walls the others leave: shoot them down, ram them, or take another ring. Covered rings: shoot a hole, ram (plain slabs break free, danger ones debuff you), or fly the open one. Add AI Serpents in Game Config (+).

Where the numbers come from, and what is not modelled
  • Flight: Serpent.prefab VesselTransformer: speed = throttle × 50 u/s × boost, never under MinimumSpeed 10; pitch and yaw 80°/s, roll 20°/s. No drift.
  • Solid Fuel Pellets (Time, A): ConsumeBoostAction.asset: a press burns a quarter of the tank for 3 s (× 1 → 1.6 at Time 10); burns overlap, each adds 2× cruise (1 pellet 3×, 4 pellets 9×). Fifth Pellet (Time 5): a fifth of the tank each, five in it. The tank refills 0.07 a second (Serpent.prefab resource "Boost"). SERPENT_FUEL_PELLETS.md.
  • Abilities as Garrett recut them (#1642, ElementalAbilityMaps/Serpent.asset): Charge = Scope, Mass = Seed Wall, Space = Cloak / Sniper Shot, Time = Solid Fuel Pellets.
  • Sniper Shot (Space, scoped RT): SniperShotAction.asset: 12 s recovery, set by CHARGE (× 0.45 at Charge 10), 3,000 u, a 1.5° cone with a 6 u floor on its radius. As SniperShotActionExecutor does: the round goes the full range through every hostile prism in its cone, super-shielded ones included, never your own. A rival pilot in the cone is stripped one level of each element (vesselStripPerElement 0.1). Its cooldown is its only limit: the mode rations nothing.
  • Scope (Charge, LT): SniperScopeAction.asset: 60° unscoped, 12° at full zoom; the trigger's depth is the zoom. Hunter's Sight (Charge 5, afterimages of rivals) is not modelled.
  • Seed Wall (Mass): SeedWallAction.asset: bricks 6 × 3 u (× 1 → 2 at Mass 10), stolen from loose prisms within 40 u of its edge (× 1 → 2 at Space 10), one sweep every 0.3 s. Stop (B) seeds a shielded wall that grows only while you stay stopped; the cloak (RT unscoped, CloakSeedWallAction.asset: 15 s) seeds a super-shielded one that grows until its seed is broken. A broken seed lets its bricks go as loose prisms. A crystal shields your growing walls (the ripple). A bare brick a hull flies into breaks and the hull keeps its speed (VesselDamagePrismEffect); a shielded brick or a seed bounces it. Lockdown (Mass 5, danger panels in closed holes) and Shield Steal (Space 5) are not modelled.
  • Covered rings (SerpentTimeController, SerpentTimeCluster, the same hashes): 1 of 3, 2 of 3, 1 of 2, 2 of 2 rings covered per gate at I1-I4, by a grid of 7 × 7 × 1.5 u slabs 7 u apart, 15 / 30 / 50 / 70 % of them danger, scattered; one slab grows back per covered ring every 1.5 s, the same kind. A plain slab breaks free on a ram; a danger slab breaks and debuffs every element by a level for 4 s, 1 s between hits (VesselElementalDebuffByDangerPrismEffect.asset, its tuned value read as one level). The Sniper Shot clears either.
  • Lab-only, the mode itself: the four courses (8 to 12 gates, 2 or 3 rings a gate), the loose debris round each gate (the wall's fuel), the trail every hull lays (a prism every 8 u, gone after 30 s), crystals 150 to 350 u off the racing line, a wall's 45 s life and 160-brick cap, and your own walls letting you through. The game races in Rampage's four living cells (its flora and fauna); the page's open space has none.
  • The AI: input-only, through the same sticks, triggers and buttons a pilot has. Its play styles are brains over the four element levels (when it walls, stops, snipes, how many pellets it stacks), lab-only. It picks the ring of each gate it can get through cheapest, threads a gap in a part-built wall, and shoots or rams a wall it cannot go round. A ring whose danger slabs block its pass points counts as walled; plain slabs it rams.
  • Not modelled: the wall's herringbone holes and its pull animation (a stolen prism lands at once), the Lockdown danger panels' damage, Rampage's flora and fauna (the game's arena), elemental crystals' petals, the eject of stripped petals as crystals, team domains, lifeforms, sound. Read every time as relative: between styles, levels, rules, and walls on against walls off.
