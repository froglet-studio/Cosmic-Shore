Brittle Star Studio

Round 4 · the Unity build

The Brittle Star, the Falcon's comeback: a one-touch flyer with a cockpit dart inside a pentagon support ring of five engines and five guns. Swap between Speed, Creation and Destruction modes, fire one stream or all five, and launch the ring itself as a bomb. Fly its two design minigames (Demolition Derby and Fauna Frenzy) at four intensities, against AI Brittle Stars at Easy, Medium or Hard. Flight, gun and trail are the last Brittle Star built (an unmerged branch); the kit is the design document, and every number neither gives is marked lab-only.

Input feel

How far the arrow keys push the aim stick. The two readings of the stick's distance disagree: the design document opens a spread, the branch's GunRingTransformer moves the point all five guns converge on (70 + 300 |stick|² u ahead). Fly both and record which one stays.

Ability row

The design's four icons: the three modes and Ring Detonation. Element order on the game's row (charge, mass, space, time) is not assigned yet: the modes are not element abilities in the design.

Hull

Unity build is what ships (BrittleStar = 16): its rules (Cruise plus three exclusive modes, four modes (the five heat-limited guns, the five whip cords), a trail per ring corner, the nucleus-wide ring explosion) and its numbers (the Unity build numbers card, SHIPPED in this page, which studio_to_unity.py clones into BrittleStarConfig.asset). The other two fly round 1's design kit, for comparison: the branch's BrittleStar.prefab (Philip Appoh, May to July 2026, never merged) and the older Falcon.prefab Manta copy.

The concept art: "a pentagon instead of a circle; each point of the pentagon is an engine in the back and a gun in the front", the ring held to the cockpit by suspended energy, not struts. Element looks from the design: Mass a larger cockpit, Charge larger thrusters and guns, Space longer barrels, Time a ring that curves into a circle.

Where the numbers come from, and what is not modelled
  • The Unity build (the default set, round 2): VesselClassType.BrittleStar = 16's rules and numbers, ported line for line from BrittleStarExecutor / BrittleStarRules (contract: R_VesselActions/BRITTLE_STAR.md). Cruise 45 u/s; A / B / X select Speed (cruise x6, x8 at Time 10), Creation (x0.5, the slowest; a trail behind each ring corner) or Space (X: the five guns, RT's pull setting their rate, heating as they fire; overheated, they stop and the trail lays danger prisms) or Charge (RB: five cords, 2026-10-16 - 120 u whips with RT released that carry slow waves from every turn and ring flick, shortening to 80 u straight sabers as RT is pulled; they slice every prism they touch and cool the guns), and press again for Cruise; in every other mode RT fires the one aimed turret, no spread; LT throws a spare ring (one per crystal, two at most) and the mounted ring regrows; the stick's direction turns the ring in every mode. RT outside Destruction: 12 rounds/s at 70 u/s from the aimed turret or the cockpit. LT throws the ring (+240 u/s, 1.1 s fuse): a flat disk in its plane, as thick as the ring (0.22 u x Charge 1 to 4) and as wide as the nucleus (392 u here), swept out over 0.7 s, breaking every prism it passes and scoring one petal hit per rival hull. A stored charge or a crystal rebuilds the ring over 0.5 s; a crystal while charged is 6 s of Overdrive. Camera straight behind, no height. Every number is in SHIPPED (the Unity build numbers card).
  • Flight (round 1's design sets; Vessel Config > Flight numbers picks the source). The branch's BrittleStar.prefab (origin/claude/falcon-brittlestar-reintro-oyx1q1): SingleStickVesselTransformer, speed = 10 + 25 × boost, so 35 u/s cruise and 110 u/s in Speed mode (VesselStatus.boostMultiplier 4); there is no throttle axis. Pitch and yaw 80°/s, bank into the turn 30°/s, each plus 0.1 × speed (RotationThrottleScaler). Falcon.prefab: 60 u/s cruise, 210 boosted, pitch 60°/s, yaw 40°/s, bank 15°/s. Both: the nose follows the stick's command at 1.5/s (VesselTransformer.LERP_AMOUNT) and speed eases at the same rate; the stick eases as the game's gamepad strategy does, 1 − cos(x·π/2).
  • Gun (authored, the branch): FullAutoAction 30 rounds/s per muzzle; round speed 1500 → 4000 u/s with Space (its speedValue ElementalFloat) and a 0.3 s life. The round decelerates as the game's Projectile does (cos(πt/2T)), so its range is v·2T/π: 286 u at Space 0, 764 u at Space 10. Hit radius 0.5 (FalconProjectile.prefab). The five guns ride GunRingTransformer: the ring spins 20°/s and the guns converge 70 + 300 |right stick|² u ahead. Trail: SparrowPrismController, a (2, 2, 5) block every 7 u, 4 u behind; camera SparrowCameraSettingsSO, 50 u behind and 10 up.
  • Modes, the gun's turrets, Ring Detonation, the crystal charge and overdrive, the element parameters, the personas and both minigames: the design document "Class - Brittle Star" (Notion, Class Design Documents). Its words are quoted in Docs/Studios/BRITTLE_STAR.md; every number it does not give is lab-only and sits in The kit or Barricades and fauna.
  • Collisions (lab-only): a round destroys the first prism it meets (prisms have no health in the game); a shielded prism loses its shield instead; a super-shielded prism (Derby I4) shrugs off rounds, and only the ring's blast core strips its super shield. The hull ploughs through an ordinary prism at a speed cost and is stopped short by a super-shielded one.
  • AI: input-only (one stick, the aim stick and the buttons a player has). Easy and Medium are Hard plus SkimRaceDifficulty.asset's two mistakes in what the AI believes: a late notice of each new target (0.5 / 0.25 s mean) and a misjudged one (9.9% / 4.5%); and two lab-only Brittle Star mistakes: it reads a barricade later (170 / 240 u ahead against Hard's 320) and changes mode later (0.7 / 0.35 s). The style brains (STYLES[].brain) and the local planner that fans a few headings round each barricade are lab-only.
  • Not modelled: the game's prism pools and growth animation, elemental crystals and comeback, the HyperSea ecology (the Frenzy's creatures are a lab stand-in that eat trail), networking, sound, the ring's flight being blocked by anything but prisms. Read every time as relative: between modes, styles and levels.
