Where the numbers come from, and what is not modelled
  • Courses: the four Skim Race tracks of MinigameSkimRace.unity, laid as SpawnableWaypointTrack lays them (a prism every ~12 u, straight or Catmull-Rom); crystals at the CrystalManager anchors with 35 u jitter; target = waypoints × laps (24, 30, 56, 54).
  • Flight: Squirrel.prefab: throttle × 60 u/s × boost; pitch and yaw 120°/s, roll 130°/s. Drift: SquirrelDriftAction ×1.8 turn, grip 0.25.
  • Boost: +0.1 per prism skimmed × Time (1 → 2), 1–5×, decay 0.3/s. A hull contact resets it and slows the hull 10%.
  • Trails: two rails at ±9.66 u, a pair per 7 u, own-trail grace 1 s. Pickup ring: 8 shielded prisms, radius 8.2, 8 u ahead on each capture (the crystal morph).
  • Boost Ring: SquirrelTubeAction 8 danger prisms, radius 8, 100 u ahead, 20 s × Mass. The AI places it as SquirrelAIBoostPolicy does: below 3× boost, lined up with the line ahead.
  • AI: a simplified stand-in for SkimRaceDriver; it follows the line and turns for its crystal through the same sticks and triggers a player has. Easy and Medium are SkimRaceHandicap: slow reaction (0.5–1.5 × the level) and misjudged passes (believed 2 capture radii + 4 u off the line, for up to 6 s).
  • The difficulty gaps are smaller here than in the game (there, Medium is 14–19% slower than Hard and Easy 23–54%: SKIM_RACE_AI.md §10). The studio pilot plans only ~100 u ahead, so a late notice rarely costs it anything, and it turns back for a missed crystal in about 2 s where the game's pilot loses about 10. The mistakes themselves (how many, when) are the game's.
  • Not modelled: the game's racing-line planner, team play, lifeforms, crystal capture shapes beyond a 25.5 u reach, sound. Read every time as relative, between difficulties and types.
