Drift feel

Space (or a tap of LT on the phone) drifts at this depth. A gamepad or the LT handle is analog: the drift is exactly how far it is pulled. Full pull turns ×1.8 faster with grip 0.25, so the course lags the nose.

Where the numbers come from, and what is not modelled
  • Flight: Squirrel.prefab VesselTransformer: throttle × 60 u/s × boost; pitch and yaw 120°/s, roll 130°/s; vector flight model.
  • Drift: SquirrelDriftAction Mult 1.8, grip 0.25, lerped by the LT pull (singleTriggerDrift).
  • Boost: SkimmerBoostPrismEffect +0.1 per prism skimmed, × Time (1 → 2), clamped 1–5; decays 0.3/s (BoostDecayRate).
  • Boost Ring: SquirrelTubeAction 8 danger prisms, radius 8, 100 u ahead, cooldown 20 s × Mass (1 → 0.5, floor 0.35); Twin Rings at Mass 5.
  • Steal: skimmer sphere 15 → 30 by Space; shielded prisms lose their shield, or at Space 5 (Iron Grip) change hands and keep it.
  • Crystal Joust: an overtake steals petals × Charge (1 → 2.5, VesselOvertakeBySkimmerEffect.stealScale), 1 s cooldown.
  • Not modelled: lifeforms (so no Shepherd), the game's Skim Race AI (the two rivals follow the line at a fixed speed), the real course and crystal spawns, prism lifetimes, the omni-crystal ring morph, the game's touch scheme, sound. The trail plates are drawn smaller than the game's so the stage stays readable.
  • Level scaling is read as linear between level 0 and level 10, as ElementalFloat authors it.
