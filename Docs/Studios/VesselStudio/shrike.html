Shrike Studio

Round 1 · design studio

The Shrike, the fleet's sniper: a one-touch flyer that flies very slowly so it can aim, docks to any block it runs into, and builds nests to dock in. The Void Lance pierces everything, even super-shielded blocks; the Void Sling anchors a tether on a block and hurls the Shrike there in a second; the Targeting Array paints a target and every nest fires homing missiles at it. Fly its two design minigames, Den Defense and Thread the Needle, at four intensities, with AI Shrikes at Easy, Medium or Hard. The kit is the design document; the game's Shrike is still a Manta copy, so every kit number is a lab-only proposal.

Where the numbers come from, and what is not modelled
  • The kit: the design document "Class - Shrike" (Notion, Class Design Documents, Sparrow Chain). Docking: "the Shrike will dock to any block it runs into"; one button undocks, or (flying) builds a nest; "docking anywhere on the nest should snap the Shrike to the correct location in the center"; "while docked the Shrike gets a cooldown reduction bonus on all of its abilities, increased on super shielded blocks". The nest: super-shielded blocks round the hull (here a cup behind it, so the nose is always clear) that "rotates with the Shrike", and a turret flora on top that holds health blocks, regrows them (faster while you are docked in it), fires the missiles and, destroyed, reveals its crystal. Void Lance: "a long range pin point shot that pierces through everything and destroys any block it hits, even if super shielded; friendly fire is enabled; a short cooldown". Void Sling: "a long range shot that slings the Shrike across the map to its target; it automatically docks on arrival; cannot tether to crystals or vessels; a moderate cooldown; a 1 second fixed boost pointing at the target". Targeting Array: "paint a target and homing missiles launch from each active nest; one health block per missile, never the last; enemies first, else the centre of mass near the aim; no target, no missiles; the missiles are faster than any class but turn wide". Trail: "thin long blocks that get thicker while boosting with the void sling". Ark range: "medium = 1 cell diameter; at the edge a countdown and a gentle pull back". Every number for these is in The kit, a lab-only proposal.
  • Elements (the design's descriptions): Charge "wider radius on void lance"; Mass "more blocks in the nest and shorter trail wavelength"; Space "larger lock-on radius for targeting array"; Time "shorter cooldown on void sling". No persona names or level-5 upgrades are given: the play-style names here (Lancer, Nester, Painter, Slinger) are the studio's.
  • Flight: Shrike.prefab on bleeding-edge, a flat Manta copy: SingleStickVesselTransformer (DefaultMinimumSpeed 10, DefaultThrottleScaler 50, so 60 u/s; PitchScaler 60, YawScaler 40, RollScaler 15, RotationThrottleScaler 0). The nose follows the stick's command at 1.5/s (VesselTransformer.LERP_AMOUNT) and speed eases at the same rate; the stick eases as the gamepad strategy does, 1 − cos(x·π/2). No CameraSettingsSO: the chase camera sits 30 u behind, the Manta's (Docs/VESSEL_TAIL_AND_JETS.md), plus a lab-only 6 u lift.
  • PvP is petals only (LOCKED, Docs/claude/IMPACT_EFFECTS_AND_AUDIO.md): the design's "slowed, disrupted steering, trail disabled" on a vessel hit by the lance or a missile is retired. A rival hull hit is one scored petal hit, at most one per victim per 0.05 s. Friendly fire still breaks your own and allied MASS. Den Defense is co-operative, so its pilots never hit each other.
  • Minigames (the design's two): Thread the Needle ("use your void sling to traverse through obstacles and collect all the crystals"; I1 windows narrowing, I2 a wall-sized panel hiding each window, I3 panels then a mass cluster at the last, I4 a cluster at every window) and Den Defense ("defend your nest from blocks on collision course and fauna"; a wave every 20 s; I1 asteroids, I2 plus single creatures as boss waves, I3 sizes and speeds vary (big ones slow), I4 the creatures more aggressive). The layouts' numbers are lab-only (Course and waves). Each window's anchor plate is a fixture (lab-only) so a lance through the window cannot strand the course.
  • AI: input-only (the stick and the four buttons a player has). Easy and Medium are Hard plus SkimRaceDifficulty.asset's two mistakes in what it believes: a late notice of each new target (0.5 / 0.25 s mean) and a misjudged one (9.9% / 4.5%: it believes a window or a threat is off to one side, and shoots or slings there); and one lab-only Shrike mistake, a small aim error per target (0.5° / 0.2°). The style brains (STYLES[].brain) are lab-only.
  • Not modelled: the game's prism pools and growth animation, elemental crystals and the comeback system, the HyperSea ecology (the creatures and asteroids are lab stand-ins), networking, sound, an asteroid striking a docked hull outside the den, missiles' own pathfinding beyond a look-ahead swerve. Read every number as relative: between levels, styles and kit settings.
