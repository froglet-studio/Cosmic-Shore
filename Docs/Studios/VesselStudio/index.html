Gibbonround 2 · two modes

Round 2, after Garrett's review: two modes. TIME mode feels like flying: the Gibbon plants its own anchor prisms ahead, alternating sides, and yanks itself along them on short elastic tethers (the Tether prototype's auto-tethers). Squeeze a trigger and you are in SPACE mode: each trigger fires its own tether and its stick aims it, then reels it. One tether is a swing round its anchor, two is a bolas round the line between them, none is a fling through space until time mode takes the sticks back. Every tether is a light sword: what it sweeps through, it cuts.

Fleaconcept · round 1

The ricochet. It crawls at 40 u/s on its own; speed comes from bouncing off mass. A hull contact reflects it instead of resetting it, and a press of RT inside the window round the contact is a perfect bounce: ×1.35 and a growing chain. Between bounces it coasts almost ballistically, so you aim the next bounce with the nose. Crouch with LT and release to hop when no mass is near.

Albatrossconcept · round 1

The soarer. No engine at all: it carries its own ballast, so it falls, and its wings turn speed into lift. Dive to gain speed, pull up to spend it as height, and ride the updraft that rises over every piece of mass (the course ribbon is a thermal corridor). LT spreads the wings for lift and drag, RT folds them for a clean dive. Energy management, not throttle.

Remoraconcept · round 1

The drafter. Alone it is slow (55 u/s). In a rival's wake its speed cap becomes their speed ×1.3, so it catches anyone it can get behind. Hold LT within reach behind a rival and it latches: towed at their speed, the draft meter filling, taking petals if its Mass is up. Let go, or press RT, and it slings past at ×1.7 their speed plus the draft. Never leads until the last straight.

Moleconcept · round 1

The burrower. The whole game turned inside out: in open space it is slow (40 u/s) and turns badly; inside mass (within 16 u of any prism) it is fast (150 u/s), turns on a dime, and passes straight through. The course ribbon, the mass beside it, every rival's trail is a highway, and the more others lay, the faster you are. LT digs a tunnel of your own mass for the next lap; RT as you leave mass is a timed breach for a kick.

Marlinconcept · round 1

The drifter. Velocity and nose are two different things: the sticks turn the hull freely while it keeps sliding, RT burns along the nose, LT is a retro burn against the velocity, B flips it 180° in a third of a second (flip and burn). A drift assist bends the velocity gently toward the nose so it reads as 3D drifting rather than a physics puzzle; turn it to 0 in Vessel Config for pure Newtonian flight.

Eelconcept · round 1

The body. A head leading a train of segments that follow its exact path: the body IS its trail, shielded mass a rival's hull bounces off. Longer is slower (72 u/s at 8 segments, 45 at 30), and it grows on crystals and skims. LT coils: the turn doubles and the body bunches up behind you; let go and the tail whips straight, cutting any rival or neutral mass it sweeps. RT sheds five segments as standing prisms for a speed burst.

Octopusconcept · round 1

The pulse jet. Hold LT to inhale: it slows, the mantle swells, the charge fills in 0.7 s. Release and it jets, up to 205 u/s, then fades toward a 20 u/s drift. The beat is yours: release at the same interval as your last two pulses and the chain grows, each pulse ×1.1 more, up to ×1.8. RT spends a pulse of charge on an ink cloud of danger prisms behind you. Charge-and-release movement with a rhythm layer you set yourself.
