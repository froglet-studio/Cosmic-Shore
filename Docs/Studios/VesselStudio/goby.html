Goby & Pistol

Duo lab · round 13

The first duo vessel: one squad slot, two craft. The Goby flies the 3D world on two thumbs like the Squirrel and lays the path; the Pistol (a pistol shrimp) plays a 2D sidescroller unrolled from that path, rides its hills Tiny Wings style and blows things up. Fly either half while the AI flies the other, and swap with D-pad left / right or keys 1 / 2, the same swap as the arena's. A design lab: the brief is .claude/skills/duo-vessel/references/designs/goby-and-pistol.md.

The duo seat

One seat, two halves (the duo contract). Rival duos and rival single hulls come in a later round. The swap keeps everything: charges, cooldowns, the path.

AI

Goby AI: 1. the crystal, 2. hunt flora and fauna (and enemy walls), 3. lay the Pistol a path (it eases off when the Pistol falls behind, and weaves in height to give it hills). Pistol AI: dive on downslopes, let go on upslopes (Tiny Wings), launch at the densest target ahead, Snap a vortex ring at the densest group ahead, ghost through friendly blocks. Both write the same inputs a player does.

Trail and hills

Every block of the Goby's trail is laid along its path; every Nth block is a hinge tilted up or down in turn, and the hinges are the control points of the Pistol's ground (a Catmull-Rom curve, the same curve Trail.Project rides). How hinges become hills is the lab's reading of the brief, not decided.

Decisions

Where the numbers come from, and what is not modelled
  • Goby flight: a two-thumb flier like the Squirrel (Squirrel.prefab VesselTransformer: cruise 60 u/s, pitch and yaw 120, roll 130, speed eased as the Squirrel studio eases it; a lab-only 10 u/s floor). The sticks go through the game's dual-stick mix (InputController).
  • Straight boost: the Rhino's ramp (RhinoRampBoostAction.asset: up at 220 u/s², bleed 300 u/s²) to a lab-only ceiling of ×4, against the Rhino's ×16.8 ("like the Rhino's but less").
  • Swap: PilotSwap (cooldown 0.75 s) on PilotSwapGesture's keys: D-pad left / right, 1 / 2.
  • AI levels: Hard is the skilled pilot; Medium and Easy notice each new target late (0.25 / 0.5 s mean) and misjudge 4.5% / 9.9% of them (SkimRaceDifficulty.asset, the Vessel Studio's universal levels).
  • Everything else is lab-only: the straight boost, the primer, heat and flares, the Pistol's gravity, dive, launch, Snap (the vortex ring) and ghost, its vision band and body, the hills, and the world (clusters of flora, fauna, enemy walls and friendly blocks, seeded per intensity).
  • Not modelled: rival ships (the brief says ships are hot by default), the element meter and its mappings, network ownership of the two halves, co-op (two humans: the offer / accept swap), death and respawn, sound, the real game's prisms and their shields, the phone flip (a Swap button stands in).
  • The Pistol's world is drawn by the same rules as the game's side view (GobyPistolDuo, PistolWarp, PistolWorldMesh, PistolWorld.shader; the artifact is the source of truth, Unity its clone): every prism in a depth window of −0.5 to 3 × the parallax depth is drawn as the game draws it (Garrett, 2026-10-18: as true to the game as possible): its own mesh and material, lit under the same sky, through a real perspective camera whose off-axis frustum IS the parallax; what the Pistol can reach is warped through the unroll (its eight corners mapped through the ribbon round its own segment, as far along the path as the bend there stretches it); the deep background is rigid; the foreground is real and opaque (it may only not cover the Pistol); no fog, no tint, no glass - the primed / hot / held marks are rings; a prism the Goby loops round holds the loop's centre and is drawn as the bar of the whole run of path it is beside; the forecast past the Goby extrapolates the ribbon's last segment. Everything is laid on an orthographic screen with 2.5D parallax about the Pistol. The Goby's trail is the ground: its line (downslopes warm) and its tiles, rigid on the line at their own depth (no floor, no curtain); the sky and stars are turned by the frame at the Pistol. Not mapped: the drifting background field. The Snap is a vortex ring (the self-explosion was dropped, Garrett 2026-10-15): prisms near its core are pulled into a toroidal flow and carried, then broken when it collapses (lab-only, a setting). The world keeps seeding clusters just ahead of the Goby (lab-only, Trail and hills).
  • The Tangent plane (round 13, the default). Nothing is unrolled or warped: the Pistol is in the real 3D world and its sidescroller is a plane through it that holds the Goby's path where the Pistol is, seen by a real camera from the plane's side. The plane's normal is the path's own carried along it without twisting, so the path bending within the plane is a real hill or dip and the camera only yaws as far as the path leaves the plane (eased over Plane turn s); which way is "along" follows the chord of the path round the Pistol, eased over Camera roll s, so the camera moves as little as it can. The trail's hills are real in 3D (the hinges lifted along the path's up) and the Pistol rides their projection onto the plane, exact where the plane touches the path; far ahead the projection and the drawn trail part where the path leaves the plane (the Unroll test's "Trail off plane"). Prisms near the plane are their true boxes; out of it their faces part and fade (the game's PrismDepthPlane look, the Wrasse's and the Starlings'). Not modelled: the dither corridor in front of the plane.
  • The lab's prisms are plain boxes, so the warped box is the warped prism; the game deforms each prism's own mesh inside the same box. Near tight turns two passes of the path compete for a point: the Unroll test counts how often (the view's fast map may pick the other pass at such a fold; the sim's map never does).
