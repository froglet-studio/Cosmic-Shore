Goby & Pistol

Duo lab · round 1

The first duo vessel: one squad slot, two craft. The Goby flies the 3D world on two thumbs like the Squirrel and lays the path; the Pistol (a pistol shrimp) plays a 2D sidescroller unrolled from that path, rides its hills Tiny Wings style and blows things up. Fly either half while the AI flies the other, and swap with D-pad left / right or keys 1 / 2, the same swap as the arena's. A design lab: the brief is .claude/skills/duo-vessel/references/designs/goby-and-pistol.md.

Intensity

The duo seat

One seat, two halves (the duo contract). Rival duos and rival single hulls come in a later round. The swap keeps everything: charges, cooldowns, the path.

AI

Goby AI: 1. the crystal, 2. hunt flora and fauna (and enemy walls), 3. lay the Pistol a path (it eases off when the Pistol falls behind, and weaves in height to give it hills). Pistol AI: dive on downslopes, let go on upslopes (Tiny Wings), launch at the densest target ahead, explode on 3+ targets or a primed one, ghost through friendly blocks. Both write the same inputs a player does.

Trail and hills

Every block of the Goby's trail is laid along its path; every Nth block is a hinge tilted up or down in turn, and the hinges are the control points of the Pistol's ground (a Catmull-Rom curve, the same curve Trail.Project rides). How hinges become hills is the lab's reading of the brief, not decided.

Decisions

Where the numbers come from, and what is not modelled
  • Goby flight: a two-thumb flier like the Squirrel (Squirrel.prefab VesselTransformer: cruise 60 u/s, pitch and yaw 120, roll 130, speed eased as the Squirrel studio eases it; a lab-only 10 u/s floor). The sticks go through the game's dual-stick mix (InputController).
  • Straight boost: the Rhino's ramp (RhinoRampBoostAction.asset: up at 220 u/s², bleed 300 u/s²) to a lab-only ceiling of ×4, against the Rhino's ×16.8 ("like the Rhino's but less").
  • Swap: PilotSwap (cooldown 0.75 s) on PilotSwapGesture's keys: D-pad left / right, 1 / 2.
  • AI levels: Hard is the skilled pilot; Medium and Easy notice each new target late (0.25 / 0.5 s mean) and misjudge 4.5% / 9.9% of them (SkimRaceDifficulty.asset, the Vessel Studio's universal levels).
  • Everything else is lab-only: the closeness boost, the primer, heat and flares, the Pistol's gravity, dive, launch, blast and ghost, its vision band and body, the hills, and the world (clusters of flora, fauna, enemy walls and friendly blocks, seeded per intensity).
  • Not modelled: rival ships (the brief says ships are hot by default), the element meter and its mappings, network ownership of the two halves, co-op (two humans: the offer / accept swap), death and respawn, sound, the real game's prisms and their shields, the phone flip (a Swap button stands in).
  • The 2D world's prisms are drawn where the unroll puts them, at a parallax by depth; prisms off the band are hidden unless primed. Near tight turns two passes of the path compete for a point: the Unroll test counts how often.
