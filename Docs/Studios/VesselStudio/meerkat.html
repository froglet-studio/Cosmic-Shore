One ability, flown on one thumb: the left stick flies at full throttle, so the right thumb is always free. The cyan grid on your nose is the tunnel RT would form now: the right stick bends it any way (further = shorter and fatter), LT shortens it, RT forms it and you ride it, coming out with the speed you went in with.     Wormhole · shape MeerkatWormholeConfig

    The reticle is always on your nose: the tunnel RT would form now, reticleAhead in front of you. It is a straight shaft, then a bend - an umbrella handle: LT sets how long the shaft runs before it bends (shaftMin released to shaftMax squeezed), and the right stick sets which way it bends and how far, up to bendMaxDegrees, round a bend of radius bendRadius. RT confirms. Its size is fixed: a throatRadius throat between mouthRadius mouths, however it is shaped.

    Wormhole · ride MeerkatWormholeConfig

    Pull RT and the tunnel ripples out of its centre to both mouths (formSeconds) while you dive into it. The ride is your speed before the tunnel times three multipliers: rideEntryMultiplier going in (1 = no change), rising to rideMidMultiplier at the throat and easing to rideExitMultiplier at the way out - a little boost you come out with, on the tunnel's last heading, before you ease back to cruise. Out, your reticle comes back after reticleDelaySeconds, growing out of its centre over reticleGrowSeconds; the tunnel stays staySeconds, then collapses back to its centre over collapseSeconds. One tunnel at a time: forming a new one collapses the last.

    Reticle · feel & look MeerkatWormholeConfig · yours alone

    Only you see your reticle: the tunnel RT would form now. It eases toward what the right stick and LT ask for over reticleSmoothSeconds (0 snaps), and it is a see-through frame of thin spindles: reticleRings rings of reticleSpindlesPerRing spindles round its throat and reticleRibs ribs along it, reticleSpindleThickness thick, cyan at the way in to white at the way out, over a faint glass (reticleGlass). Fully grown, the lime line you will fly runs through it (reticlePath). Faint at rest (reticleIdleAlpha), bright while you shape it.

    Wormhole · look MeerkatWormholeConfig · the bridge, everyone's

    A formed tunnel shows everyone its bridge: rings and ribs of spindles over a glass surface, in its maker's domain colour (the game will build it from its Spindle prefabs), condensing out of its centre as it forms and evaporating as it collapses. bridgeVisibility 0 leaves only the bent space.

    Space · look MeerkatWormholeConfig · the bent space, everyone's

    Off by default (round 4: all lensing off; the bridge is the tunnel's look). Raised, everyone sees a tunnel bend space-time: the space round its tube bent away from it (warpStrength), its centre line clear, and each mouth a dent as wide as the mouth (dentStrength). Its collapse sends a subtle circular ripple across the screen's edges only (rippleStrength, from rippleEdge out, over rippleSeconds), never the centre where you aim, weaker the further away it is.
