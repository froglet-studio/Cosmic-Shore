    Omnicrystal · the network MeerkatSolitonConfig · rounds 16-26

    The Meerkat's Omnicrystal ability: the network. Fly through an omnicrystal (gold, on the course line; omniCount of them) and a big network of wormholes opens at once from where you took it, spreading every way in 3D (labyrinthSpread), and you fly straight into it (omniAtOnce; 0: it arms your next RT instead). Round 26 (Yash, 2026-10-28: "it will always be large, despite the last wormhole's length ... if it has full mass, then it will be the largest, covering the entire cell, and we can transport through those network of wormholes from one place to other"): your Mass sizes it, never the last wormhole. Its reach is networkReachAtZero × the cell's radius at Mass 0 (half the cell) up to networkReachAtFull × at Mass 10 (the whole cell, its openings on the race shell), and never under labyrinthRadiusMin; it is centred that share of the way from you to the cell's centre, so it always lies inside the cell. Its forks and openings grow with its reach (labyrinthBranchPointsMin / labyrinthOpeningsMin at labyrinthRadiusMin to the ...Max numbers at labyrinthRadiusMax): 30-40 wormholes at Mass 0, 78 at Mass 10. Its forks are laid for transport, after Garrett's Omnicrystal Network Lab: each fork lies on the straight line toward the openings it leads to, so the way to an opening runs close to the straight line (median 1.13-1.22 × it). Every wormhole looks exactly like a wormhole: its glass, its calm inner wall (tubeInner), the bent space round it. They taper to rootTip at the openings and wander (rootGnarl). Inside, you fly yourself on the left stick (rounds 20-21): you cruise along the tube on your own (rootCruise), your nose following it; the left stick moves you round the tube, left and right, up and down, the way it would turn your nose (rootMove, calmed by rootDrag); RT pushes you faster (rootThrust, up to rootMaxSpeed). The walls bounce you off (rootBounce). Steer into a wormhole at a fork to go down it (the one on your side is lit, forkHintSeconds before); fly back past a fork and you are in the one before it. Out of an opening you fly on along it. The old outer skin (rootBarkVisibility), the old inner wall (rootOpacity) and the old ground are off; their sliders stay.

    Space · look MeerkatSolitonConfig · the bent space, everyone's

Its collapse sends a subtle circular ripple across the screen's edges only (rippleStrength, from rippleEdge out, over rippleSeconds), never the centre where you aim, weaker the further away it is.

    Kits · prisms plain, shielded, super-shielded · lab

    In Garrett's kit and the Mix the world carries seeded prisms round the race line beside the field (the same on every load for a course): plain (blue), shielded (pale) and super-shielded (gold, never moved). A soliton swallows them (and the field's) at its way in and fires them out of its way out as moving prisms: two plain ones that meet both go, scored to the soliton's maker; a shielded one bounces (Docs/PRISM_MOTION.md 1a). Mass is moved, never deleted, but for the plain pair a hit destroys.

    Kit · Charge · Sentinel mode Y · Garrett's and the Mix · lab

    Y toggles Sentinel mode. On, each tunnel you make leaves a sentinel at its way in: an afterimage of the Meerkat standing up on watch. Its soliton stands as long as it does. With the pool used up the oldest retires, and its soliton with it. In its guard (guardShare x the opening radius) it zaps rival vessels, knocking out zapPetalsL0 to zapPetalsL10 of a petal a zap by Charge (sub-petal ticks: whole petals leave as ejected crystals, and the hit silences its victim for what it is worth), and zaps your own domain's plain prisms shielded. An empty sentinel's AI zaps aiZapRate a second; in Garrett's kit you can sit in one and tap.

    Kit · Space · the soliton RT · surface area, swallow and fire · lab

    The wormhole is a soliton: a standing tunnel anyone rides. Space sets its surface area (areaL0 to areaL10), so the throat is b = A / (2 pi L g(k)): a long tunnel thin, a short one fat (the mouth stays 3.25 x the throat). Round 23: it pulls every prism and crystal within suckRadius of its way in (at suckSpeed, faster as they near), swallows them within swallowShare x the opening radius (shielded too, never super-shielded), carries them at the slide's top speed (rootMaxSpeed; round 25: the same speed as a rider, and never past one ahead of them), stretched along the tube (up to 1 + carryStretch at its middle, squeezed across, twisted carryTwist turns), and fires them out of its way out at fireSpeed, gliding to rest over fireGlideSeconds. Mass no longer sizes the tunnel here: only the surface area does (Garrett).

    Kit · Mass · exit portal passive · lab

    Once per soliton, as it forms: six small prisms of its maker's domain on a regular hexagon square to the way out, portalShare x the exit radius out, each laid along its edge. Mass sets their size; at Mass L5 they are danger prisms (a rival touching one loses dangerPetals). Mass is conserved, so the count is kept small.
