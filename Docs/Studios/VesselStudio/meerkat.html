Round 8 · the four-ability kit (proposals)

Running on PC

On this phone the stage opens in the touch layout: hold it sideways; your left thumb flies, your right thumb bends the tunnel, the RT handle forms it; ✕ comes back to the settings. On this PC fly with a gamepad or the keyboard (Input tab); a phone opening this page gets touch sticks instead. The Meerkat is the wormhole vessel (Yash, 2026-10-27: the Stoat's Gate mode, rounds 19-26, became its own vessel; the Stoat keeps Mass and Time). One ability, flown on one thumb: the left stick flies at full throttle, so the right thumb is always free. The cyan grid on your nose is the tunnel RT would form now: the right stick bends it any way (further = shorter and fatter), LT shortens it, RT forms it and you ride it, coming out with the speed you went in with. Only you see the grid; everyone sees the tube as bent space-time. A ridden tunnel stays a while, then collapses to its centre with a subtle ripple at the screen's edges. Every parameter shows the game's name for it under its slider (MeerkatWormholeConfig); save any section as a profile. Every setting has an ⓘ. Round 8 (proposals for Yash to judge, not approved for Unity): the full four-ability kit, one per element, in Vessel Config (its Section dropdown): Charge · Lookout (Y: marks crystals, shows every rival's reticle and next ring; L5 Alarm Call draws the crystals to you), Mass · Hardened Bridge (LB arms the next tunnel: its bridge becomes a lasting prism tube others cannot cross; L5 Shielded Bridge), Space · Cave-in (B: your standing tunnel collapses now, destroying every prism near it; L5 Burial costs a rival inside it a petal and silences it, never pushes it) and Time · the wormhole (L5 Chain Dig: RT late in a ride digs the next tunnel from the far mouth). Their numbers are lab-only. The race is now Warpline, the Meerkat's Time race: 5 rings a lap, 2 laps.

    Runs on this course

    No finished race yet. A run is one race: all the rings, every lap, against the clock.
    Wormhole · shape MeerkatWormholeConfig

    The reticle is always on your nose: the tunnel RT would form now, reticleAhead in front of you. It is a straight shaft, then a bend - an umbrella handle: LT sets how long the shaft runs before it bends (shaftMin released to shaftMax squeezed), and the right stick sets which way it bends and how far, up to bendMaxDegrees, round a bend of radius bendRadius. RT confirms. Its size is fixed: a throatDiameter throat between mouthDiameter mouths, however it is shaped (set here, never by the sticks).

    Wormhole · ride MeerkatWormholeConfig

    Pull RT and the tunnel ripples out of its centre to both mouths (formSeconds) while you dive into it. The ride is your speed before the tunnel times three multipliers: rideEntryMultiplier going in (1 = no change), rising to rideMidMultiplier at the throat and easing to rideExitMultiplier at the way out - a little boost you come out with, on the tunnel's last heading, before you ease back to cruise. Out, your reticle comes back after reticleDelaySeconds, growing out of its centre over reticleGrowSeconds; the tunnel stays staySeconds, then collapses back to its centre over collapseSeconds. One tunnel at a time: forming a new one collapses the last.

    Reticle · feel & look MeerkatWormholeConfig · yours alone

    Only you see your reticle: the tunnel RT would form now. It eases toward what the right stick and LT ask for over reticleSmoothSeconds (0 snaps), and it is the wormhole's own wireframe: the bridge's rings, spindles, ribs and thickness (Wormhole · look), in the same places, with no glass. Its colour is your domain colour toward white (reticleWhite); faint at rest (reticleIdleAlpha), bright while you shape it.

    Wormhole · look MeerkatWormholeConfig · the bridge, everyone's

    One geometry for the wormhole and your reticle: your reticle is this frame, bare. A formed tunnel shows everyone its bridge: rings and ribs of spindles over a glass surface, in its maker's domain colour toward white (bridgeWhite) (the game will build it from its Spindle prefabs), condensing out of its centre as it forms and evaporating as it collapses. bridgeVisibility 0 leaves only the bent space.

    Wormhole · trunk MeerkatWormholeConfig · its outside, for a third eye

    The tunnel's outer layer is a dead tree, for everyone (its rider too). Pull RT and the tree forms with the tunnel, rippling out of its centre to both mouths; each bare branch grows out of the trunk's top half the moment the tunnel reaches its root. The way in is the trunk's top cut and the way out its bottom cut, the mouths in the cut faces among the tree rings. It is the wall's own glass and bent space (see-through in the middle, bright at its outline, the world behind it bent and flowing like water), so the tunnel shows inside it. It grows with the tunnel (throatDiameterMax, mouthDiameterMax).

    Space · look MeerkatWormholeConfig · the bent space, everyone's

    Off by default (round 4: all lensing off; the bridge is the tunnel's look). Raised, everyone sees a tunnel bend space-time: the space round its tube bent away from it (warpStrength), its centre line clear, and each mouth a dent as wide as the mouth (dentStrength). Its collapse sends a subtle circular ripple across the screen's edges only (rippleStrength, from rippleEdge out, over rippleSeconds), never the centre where you aim, weaker the further away it is.

    Flight & course

    Course seed 7

    The course is picked in Game Config (Intensity): Warpline (the default), the Meerkat's Time race, 5 rings a lap; or the Stoat studio's stand-ins, Circuit (8 rings on a circle) and Hairpins (8 rings that alternate direction, then a long straight home).

    Ability · Charge · Lookout Y · lab

    Y sends a pulse: for lookoutSeconds every crystal within lookoutReach is marked, and you see every rival's reticle and its next ring, through anything. L5 Alarm Call: the crystals inside the reach are drawn to you (alarmPullSpeed), and your team-mates see the marks too. Then lookoutCooldownSeconds. A proposal: no number here is in the game.

    Ability · Mass · Hardened Bridge LB · lab

    LB arms the next tunnel (press again to disarm; the Mass icon shows ARMED). That tunnel's spindle bridge sets as real prisms: a tube lasting wallSeconds (after the tunnel itself has collapsed) that no other team can cross through its wall; they go round, and you and your team are never stopped by it. wallPrismDensity sets how many prisms make it. L5 Shielded Bridge: its prisms are shielded, immune to Cave-in and drawn shielded (pale). Why a toggle and not LB held with RT: RT is pulled while the right thumb is bending the reticle, so a chord asks both index fingers to land in the same instant (and on touch RT is a drag handle); a toggle is one press any time before, and the HUD says what the next RT will make.

    Ability · Space · Cave-in B · lab

    B collapses your standing tunnel now (not while you ride it): the bridge implodes to its centre line, and the collapse destroys every prism within caveInReach of the tube along its whole length, the field's and any unshielded hardened wall's (yours too). L5 Burial: a rival inside or touching the tunnel takes a scored hit: a petal lost and its abilities silenced (harmless) for burialSilenceSeconds. Nobody is ever pushed (PvP is petals only). Then caveInCooldownSeconds.

    Ability · Time · the wormhole RT · lab upgrade

    The wormhole (rounds 1-5, in the game) is the Time ability; its numbers are in Play Style Config. L5 Chain Dig (proposal): from chainShare of the way through a tunnel you ride, RT forms the next tunnel from its far mouth, bent by the right stick and LT as the reticle would be, and you go straight into it at throat speed (the ride stays at its throat speed through the join, then eases back to your entry speed). The tunnel behind you collapses as you leave it.
