Rivals lab stand-ins

Rivals

Rival vessels in the Ruby, Gold and Blue domains fly the course carrying petals (the ring of small crystals round each). They take no crystals; they are here so the Sting's Tentacle Lash has something to strike. A lash that strikes one sheds a petal as a crystal; with Mass at level 5 (Trawl) the Bract can net it and the colony digests it as a point.

The loop apart, then the colony, then apart

LIGHT (the Bell) → OPEN (the Sting) → NET (the Bract) → FUSE (hold B, zooids in range) → DIGEST and RAM (the colony) → SPLIT (the hold empty). No part scores alone; the colony collects nothing. The numbers are in Vessel Config > Loop numbers.

Seats 3 zooids · you take one, the AI the rest

One vessel with three seats (the Orca's rule: every empty seat an AI). Pick the zooid you fly and each AI seat's level; swap any time with left / right (fused, the same seats are Helm, Lance and Ward). "Nobody" lets the colony fly itself, to watch it.

AI

Each AI zooid flies for the crystal its one verb applies to in the window of the next three: the Bell a dark one, the Sting a lit one, the Bract a freed one; with nothing to do it flies with the Bell. The zooids call the fuse when the hold is full, when nothing in the window can be worked apart, or when a wall is next; the colony splits itself when the hold is empty and no wall is ahead. Easy and Medium are Hard plus SkimRaceDifficulty.asset's two mistakes in what each zooid BELIEVES (late notice, a misjudged crystal) and a lab-only late press.
