Where the numbers come from, and what is not modelled
  • Flight: each hull's prefab in Assets/_Prefabs/Spacevessels/: cruise, pitch, yaw and roll rates, trail wait and prism size, its VesselStatus.boostMultiplier. The Manta's yaw is capped at its maxYawDegPerSec (60). The Scarab's 216 u/s is ScarabVesselTransformer.baseTopSpeed, a C# default (not serialized). The Termite carries two transformers; this page flies its single-stick one.
  • Boost, by hull: Squirrel, Stoat, Thresher skim energy (+0.1 a skim, decay 0.3/s, up to 5×, the Squirrel's shipped numbers); Dolphin drift-charge then discharge (ChargeBoostAction.asset: 3.636 s to full, 2.5 s out, up to 2.259×); Rhino ramp on a straight stick (RhinoRampBoostAction.asset: +220 u/s², up to 16.8×); Serpent fuel pellets (ConsumeBoostAction.asset: 3× for 3 s, 4 pellets, one back every 3.6 s); Sparrow and Manta hold-to-boost at their boostMultiplier (5, 4; the Manta's Soar halves its yaw, lab-only); Urchin rides its own colour at FriendlyTerrainSpeed 300; Scarab a 100 u/s Snap Dash for 0.4 s; Grizzly Rush (3 charges, 2 s apart, burst lab-only).
  • Signature moves (lab-only stand-ins, ranges named where the game's are known): Grizzly trigger bomb, 100 u blast reach (GRIZZLY_TRIGGER_BOMBS.md), own trail spared; Serpent sniper, 1.5° cone, pierces everything (SERPENT_SNIPER_SCOPE.md); Sparrow guns, one rocket per 25 prisms shot (SPARROW_SKYBURST_BAY.md); Squirrel Boost Ring, 8 danger prisms 100 u ahead every 20 s (SquirrelTubeAction); Rhino energy sword; Urchin chain spikes; Manta Sting bombs armed by skimming, set off by a crystal; Butterfly dust steals mass; Thresher wrecking ball; Stoat slingshot kick. Dolphin's and Scarab's moves are their boosts. Falcon, Shrike and Termite are planned hulls with no moves; the Sparrow's prefab has no prism trail and the Termite's trail never starts (startDelay 100000), and both are flown that way.
  • Element levels (lab-only, one rule per element for every hull): Time ×1 → ×1.5 boost and top speed at 10 (the fleet table measures ×1.3 to ×1.6); Space skimmer 15 → 30 u and move reach ×1 → ×1.5; Mass prism size ×1 → ×2; Charge move cooldown ×1 → ×0.5. No level-5 upgrades are modelled.
  • AI: one simplified pilot for every hull, through the same sticks and triggers a player has. It picks the crystal it can reach soonest, steers round rival mass when its ram costs it, hunts rival mass when it carries a weapon, and works its boost and move by its kind. Easy and Medium are the Skim Race levels (SkimRaceDifficulty.asset): Easy notices each new crystal 0.5 s late on average and misjudges 9.9% of them; Medium 0.25 s and 4.5%. The game's per-hull pilots (AIPilot plus each hull's boost policy and autopilot driver, Docs/AI_SYSTEM/ARCHITECTURE.md) are richer; read every result here as relative, hull against hull.
  • Cell ladder (lab-only): radius 700 / 900 / 1180 / 1500 u (1180 is the BandOuterRadius 16 of the game's cell configs share), 10 / 14 / 18 / 24 crystals, 90 / 120 / 150 / 180 s.
  • Score (lab-only): 10 a crystal, 1 a rival prism destroyed, 2 a rival prism stolen. Not modelled: lifeforms, flora and fauna, shields beyond one hit, super-shields, petals and elemental crystals, teams scoring together, level-5 upgrades, sound.
