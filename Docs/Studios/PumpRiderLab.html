Ride the pump track

Your thumbsticks are your legs. Sticks apart = knees bent, together = legs straight. Bring them together (stand) as each amber bar crosses the line in the pump lane, pull them apart (crouch) on each blue bar. From a stop, tap A to push off with a foot; once you are pumping you will not need it. Every stroke is rated.

Pad: as the Urchin. Apart / together = knees, one stick up and one down = roll round the rail, both the same way = look, A = push. Keys: hold Space (or the mouse) = knees bent, A / D = roll, arrows = look, F = push. Phone: a thumbstick on each half of the view, and a PUSH button.
