# Omni Motion Lab

A browser lab for the next round of the omni crystal's element effects (round 5: thicker plates and chained flux arcs; round 4: the Charge crystal seam walk and the omni flux arc; round 3: hop clear for Space and Time, bolder Mass that rises while hidden, the Wandering arc in the seams between the Charge blooms). It covers four asks:

1. **Mass:** the triangle prisms seal fully once they cross into the camera's visible hemisphere.
2. **Space:** the squares make two moves per loop instead of three. The two triangle steps become one smooth move.
3. **Time:** the rhombus flips are timed into the squares' stops, so squares and rhombi never move at once.
4. **Charge:** a gallery of 17 effects for the pentagons. Each plays on the omni and on the elemental Charge crystal, so you can see Charge brought into parity with the other three.

The lab has styles, sliders for each ask, a beat timeline and a side-by-side omni / Charge crystal view. A "compare every Charge effect" grid is on both subjects. There is also a headless collision scorecard and a shared decision log.

## Round 5 (2026-10-13): thicker plates, chained flux arcs

The designer: "This is near perfect. Let's thicken the squares, rhombuses and pentagons (not the triangles) on the omni crystals by 10%. The omni crystal arcs should always use the previous sink as the new source, to keep a continuity of hopping arcs. When this translates to Unity, I hope the thick part of the beam looks better."

**The option:** style **"Round 5: thicker plates, chained arcs"** is Round 4 with two changes:
- **Plate thickness 1.1** (Squares tab). Each square, rhombus and pentagon (or wedge) is thickened about its own centre along its own current normal, AFTER it is posed, so a square mid-hop and a rhombus mid-flip thicken the right way.
  - Measured with `plateThicknessAt`: exactly 1.1000 for squares, rhombi and pentagons at rest, mid pentagon turn and mid wave; triangle prisms 1.0000.
- **Chained flux arcs:** each discharge starts from the pentagon the previous one landed on (never straight back to the one before). The arcs hop on round the crystal, and the landing pentagon stays lit through its own recharge.
  - It is one seeded sequence, extended as needed.
  - This is now how the Flux arc behaves everywhere, so Round 4 chains too.

**The thick beam.** The lab now draws it as three layers on one strip:
- a soft halo about 1.9× the beam width at low intensity;
- the body at beam width;
- a hot core about ⅓ of it at the top intensity.

The **Ship it** tab says how to carry that into Unity. Use one strip with the falloff computed across its width in the shader, so the beam reads round rather than flat, in HDR so bloom catches the core. Do not draw it as one wide flat quad, which is what made the round-4 beam read thin and flat.

## Round 4 (2026-10-13): seam walk on the Charge crystal, flux arc on the omni

The designer, after round 3: "Give me an option that keeps everything the same, but:
- for the Charge crystal, it just persists with a bolt randomly travelling through the triple junctions forever (no Tesla core or discharge);
- for the omni crystal, slow down the pentagonal movement by 10%, and use a different effect similar to the dipole, but don't ripple the lit state; just light up the pentagon that is charging or discharging;
- it should look good where it mates with the pentagon on both sides, smoothly fork to all 5 points of the pentagon at both ends, and keep the single thicker beam in the middle of each arc."

**The option:** style **"Round 4: seam walk + flux arc"** is Round 3 with three changes:
- the Charge crystal wears the Seam walk;
- the omni wears the Flux arc;
- the squares' pentagon turn is 1.56 s → **1.733 s** (10% slower).

"The pentagonal movement" is read as the squares' turn round the pentagon, the only motion of that name; Mass's triangles move with the camera, not a clock.

**New: the omni can wear its own Charge effect** (`chModeOmni`, Charge tab, "Same as the Charge crystal" by default). The split view labels each side with its effect. The gallery's Show button sets both back to one effect.

**Seam walk (effect 19).** This is the round-3 arc's crawl with no start and no end.
- One bolt walks the 30 seams between the 12 blooms forever, through the 20 triple junctions. It never goes straight back, and never takes a fifth seam round one bloom.
- Each seam is struck in 0.07 s and held 0.1–0.28 s, flickering; the trail fades over 0.85 s.
- The walk is one seeded infinite sequence, cached as it is needed, so any t gives the same picture.
- No core, no escape, no discharge. The petals beside the bolt light.

**Flux arc (effect 20).** Every 0.9 s (÷ Rate) a discharge starts:
- **Charge:** a pentagon charges for 0.45 s, and only it lights.
- **Strike:** an arc strikes in 0.06 s to a pentagon 55–125° away.
- **Hold:** the arc holds 0.45 s while the sink lights.
- **Fade:** both pentagons and the arc fade over 0.45 s.
- **Nothing else:** no other pentagon lights (no ripple, no tumbling axis), and nothing moves.
- **Shape:** the arc's centre runs on the great circle between the two pentagons, lifted by 0.16–0.46 of the radius depending on how far apart they are.
- **The fork:** five strands leave the five corners of the source pentagon and merge smoothly into the centre over the first 30% of the arc, then split smoothly onto the sink's five corners over the last 30%. Between, the arc is one beam three times as thick as a strand.
- **No crossing strands:** each strand is paired with the sink corner it would reach if the source pentagon were turned onto the sink. On the Charge crystal the "corners" are the bloom's five triple junctions.

## Round 3 (2026-10-13): hop clear for Space and Time, bolder Mass, the arc in the seams

The designer, after round 2:

- "For Charge, the wandering arc is good, but it shouldn't run along edges. The arc should look more like the chain lightning arc, not a travelling light. When it dissipates it should be a smooth transition, not a sharp branching."

**Space and Time.** "Rigid arcs that hop clear" (round 2's numbers) is the base of every round-3 style. Glide, low hop, the keys, rounded keys, the round-2 arc and four beats move to an **Archive** group on the Styles tab, superseded rather than deleted.

**Mass: what capped "more out".**
- Taller columns alone barely changed how many prisms you can see standing out: about 2 at a time, round 2 or 3× high.
- The shipped ratchet holds a prism that is moving away sealed until it is deep behind (the reseat), so only the approaching quarter of the crystal is ever out.
- Taller columns need a deeper reseat to stay hidden (a top at height h clears the silhouette while √(1 − d²) > 1/h), which cuts the count further.

**New: rise while hidden** (Triangles tab, lab).
- A prism may climb its column, at up to *Hidden rise rate* column-heights a second, whenever its column top at the new place is hidden behind the body. It is never seen rising.
- Every hidden prism is then out, and the ones coming round the edge come in from the top.
- Reseats become a fallback (reseat at −0.97).
- In the game this is one ray-sphere test per column per frame in `CrystalDepthPrisms`, plus two profile fields.
- Also new as sliders: *Column height* (`_Reach`) and *Size at the top* (`_PlateScaleStart`).

## Round 2 (2026-10-13): the squares glide, and the Wandering arc

The designer, after round 1:
- "The pentagonal movement is great, but the triangular one is still not smooth, and they both seem to undergo more scale changes than necessary. It should look like each square is taking a smooth movement to the next location."
- For Charge: one arc starts in the centre like Tesla core, circles a petal's perimeter, then arcs to another petal like Chain lightning or Dipole field. After a random number of hops it escapes like Lightning rod and branches into a dissipated look. No petal moves; petals light where the bolt is concentrated, and their neighbours catch some of it. Then it resets.

**Squares: what was wrong with round 1's "hop clear".**
- It lifted the squares 0.15 R (pentagon turn) and 0.44 R (triangle turn) off the shell, which reads as the square growing.
- The triangle lift carried the art file's whole Step1 offset, sideways part included, at 2.3×, so the square wobbled sideways as it rose.
- The shipped blend also changes the square's distance from the centre (it dips 11.8% R) and shrinks it to 0.85.

**New on the page.**

| Control | Values | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| *Ease* 0 | quintic smootherstep | no jolt as a move starts or stops |
| *Hop profile* | sin / sin² / plateau | how the hop rises and falls over the move |
| *Triangle turn lift* | × the art's radial lift (0.118 R) | lifts straight out from the centre |
| *Triangle turn sideways* | × the art's sideways step | separate knob for the sideways part |
| *Pentagon turn lift* | below 0 dives under | |
| Scorecard *Off shell % R* | | how far a square's centre leaves the shell, which is what reads as a scale change |

**Found.**
1. **A rigid square cannot leave its place without grazing.** Squares sit edge to edge with the rhombi, so any slide along the shell pushes the leading edge in from the first frame.
   - Clearing every neighbour takes about 0.14 R of hop on the pentagon turn, measured at 60 Hz counting hidden clips too.
   - Radial-only lifts, sin², plateau profiles and dives under all clip more than a plain sin hop of the same height.
2. **The art file's sideways step matters.** On the triangle turn, squares belonging to neighbouring triangles turn about different axes and would pass through each other mid-turn; the sideways part of Step1 steps round them. Sweep (clipping pair-frames at 30 Hz):

   | | sideways 1 | 1.5 | 2 | 2.5 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | lift 1 | 420 | 324 | 258 | 1065 |
   | lift 1.5 | 90 | **9** | 78 | 105 |

So there is a real trade between smoothness and clearance, to be decided by eye. Three styles:

| Style | Off shell % R | Min size | Visible clips % (pairs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shipped today | 11.8 | 0.845 | 66.7 |
| **Glide** (rigid turns on the shell, quintic, no lift) | **0** | 1 | 32.1 (Space×Time 2.2% R deep, Space×Space 2.3%, Mass×Space 1.1%) |
| Low hop (0.07 R; tri 0.75 / 1) | 8.8 | 1 | 22.6 |
| Hop clear (0.14 R; tri 1.5 / 1.5) | 17.7 | 1 | 1.2 (Mass×Space only: prisms standing raised at the limb) |

All three: overlap 0, 2 stops per loop, prisms flush in view. Glide is the pure "smooth movement to the next location". Its grazes are the square's lower edge sliding into the side of the neighbour it passes, about a third of a plate's thickness. The clip metric counts any point not hidden behind the body, so it does not credit the square's own top face for hiding them.

**The Wandering arc (Charge effect 18, style "glide + wandering arc").**
- **Its life:** one bolt leaves the core, traces a petal's whole rim, and leaves from the rim point nearest its next petal. It arcs 10–80° over the surface to that petal (never straight back) and traces it too. That repeats 0 to (2 + 5 × Motion) times, so 0–7 at the default.
- **The finish:** it escapes 0.7 R up the last petal's normal and bursts into 4 + 4 × Bolts filaments, about 60% of them forking once. They grow and fade over 0.8 s with a widening glow; then a short rest and a new journey.
- **The look:**
  - the bolt is its last 0.4 s of path, brightest at the head;
  - each petal glows by how close the bolt passed and how recently (0.55 s), so the petal being traced burns and its neighbours catch the edge;
  - the lit petal glows from its OWN centre, so its rim is brightest.
- **Determinism:** every journey is seeded by its index, so the same t always gives the same picture.
- **Footprint:** no petal moves (travel 0, clips 0). It needs bolts, sprites and a shader light, but no bones. With the Charge beat on, one journey plays per beat.

**Checked:** `verify_lab.cjs` PASS. `runFullBatch` deterministic. A nine-frame contact sheet of one journey read on both subjects: core start, rim laps, hops, escape and spray.

**Decision needed (designer):** glide, low hop or hop clear, judged by eye. Whether the Wandering arc is the Charge effect, and its rate, hop count and spray density.

## Round 1 (2026-10-13)

### What the shipped data actually is (measured on load, `SQFIT`)

The Space squares (`OmniSpaceSquares.asset` ← `CrystalsRaw_V2.blend`, Action.008) have three keys over a 4.8 s loop: 1.56 s, 1.6 s and 1.6 s, back to back, each a cosine ease. So the squares stop three times a loop and are moving 97% of the time.

| Key | What it is, geometrically | Worst miss |
|---|---|---|
| `SquareRotateAroundPentagon` | a **rigid 72° turn** about a pentagon's five-fold axis, through the crystal centre | 1.8e-5 |
| `SquareOverTri_Step1` + `Step2` | together, a **rigid 120° turn** about a triangle's three-fold axis, through the centre | 2.9e-5 |
| `SquareOverTri_Step1` alone | that turn **halfway (60°), lifted 0.19 off the shell** (12% of the radius) | 1.2e-5 |

A blend shape moves every corner in a straight line, so each turn is played as a chord. **Mid-move, a square shrinks to 0.83 of its size (pentagon key) or 0.87 (each triangle step), and dips toward the axis.** That is the shipped look today.

The Time wave (`TimeCrystalFlipWaveProfile.asset`) runs every 2 s, with its rings moving for 1.8 s of it. So shipped, the squares and the rhombi move at the same time **87% of the time**.

### New on the page

- **Triangles tab:** *Seal at the horizon* (lab) and *Horizon margin*. The seal depth becomes `plateRadius / cameraDistance − margin` each frame, which is the edge of the visible hemisphere under perspective (slightly past 90°). The other tab rows are the shipped profile values and the material's face split and thickness. The ratchet is `DepthRatchet.Step`, ported line for line.
- **Cadence tab:** *Cadence* has three settings:
  - 0, shipped clocks;
  - 1, interleaved beats: pentagon turn | Time wave | triangle turn | Time wave, so one wave plays in each square stop, from a new start vertex each time;
  - 2, a control: two moves with Time on its own clock.

  The tab also has *Gap* between beats (below 0 the beats overlap; this is the planted control), *Time wave speed*, and an optional **Charge beat**.
- **Squares tab:** durations, ease, and the two paths.
  - *Pentagon path*: the art key (chord), or a rigid 72° turn with an optional lift.
  - *Triangle path*: Step1 → Step2 under ONE ease (no stop, but a corner at Step1); the keys overlapped (rounds the corner, no new frames); or **one rigid 120° turn lifted on a sine, which passes exactly through the art file's Step1 pose** (lift × the art's).
- **Charge tab + Charge gallery:** 17 effects plus Off, in six families:

  | Family | Effects |
  |---|---|
  | surface / shader only | Edge crawl (ships), Corona, Ripple |
  | pentagon motion | Capacitor, Stepper clicks, Dynamo, Hum |
  | wedges (the pentagon as five wedges) | Iris, Bloom, Lightning rod |
  | bolts between pentagons | Chain lightning, Spark gaps, Strike, Arc web |
  | field | Dipole field, Shockwave, Tesla core |

  Each has a "Needs" list: shader, bones, wedge mesh, bolts, sprites. Shared knobs: rate, motion, light, bolts, stagger, and wedges on the omni.
- **Subject:** omni, Charge crystal, or both side by side, with the same effect on each. **Compare every Charge effect** shows all 18 at once on either subject; double-click a cell to pick it.
- **Beat timeline** under the stage: Space, Time and Charge lanes. Amber marks every moment Space and Time move together.
