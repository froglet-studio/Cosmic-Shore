The pigments in OKLab

Up is lighter, around is hue, outward is chroma. Light and shadow slide a paint along a ray from black, so each pigment is a cone segment. Drag to turn the view. The faint dots are the edge of what a screen can show.

Mint

The rules every colour set keeps

  1. The band. Pigments of different domains stay at least 0.05 apart in OKLab, about 2.5 just-noticeable differences, as in the six-family truth.
  2. Lighting-proof. Their directions from black stay at least 0.05 apart, so no light or shadow turns one domain's paint into another's. This is what ruled out Dart Frog's first forest face beside a lime rim: dark green is shaded lime.
  3. Contrast. A domain's rim and face differ by at least 90° of hue and 0.12 of lightness.
  4. Identity. Every rim still reads as its own family in the six-family truth, so every set's Jade still reads as Jade.
