Omnicrystal Network Lab

The Meerkat's omnicrystal grows a tunnel network to every exit. You fly into the crystal and pick one of its six points; every later junction comes after a straight so you can choose, the turns come right after the fork, and the tube rolls you toward the way you chose. No path runs more than 20% longer than the straight line to its exit, and every path is scored for comfort.

Riding: pick at each junction with the arrow keys, WASD or the pad (it lights the ways that exist); at the crystal, Space or the centre button goes straight ahead and X or the corner button goes back the way you came in. With no pick you follow the path to the highlighted exit.

How the network is built

You fly into the crystal along a straight from the emptiest direction. The crystal is the first fork: its six points (ahead, behind, right, left, up, down, in the frame you fly in with) each take the exits nearest their direction. A point with one exit leaves on the single arc that meets the exit's facing; a point with several runs straight toward them to its first junction.

At each later junction the exits still to reach are split by where they lie around the rider (right, up, left, down) into up to four ways. An exit too near for another junction on its way, or one whose path ran over the detour limit on the last build, gets a way of its own. Each way is a split that peels off toward where it is going, a turn that never bends tighter than the tightest-turn radius and eases in and out over the turn ramp, and a straight into the next junction or the exit. The next junction sits where the turn naturally ends, so no way bends off a line and back onto it.

The rider's frame is carried along without spin (a rotation-minimising frame) and then rolled. Banking turns the rider so a bend is felt as pitch rather than a sideways shove. The twist roll rolls the rider toward the way they chose, during the turn after each fork: a helical feel that costs no length. An exit's own roll joins it. No straight before a junction ever rolls.
