Starlings Deck

The 80 cards of the Starlings, the mobile swarm-of-vessels vessel, revision 3. Each element has 20 and owns one job no other element does; each card goes from level 1 to 15. Drag the level to see every card at that level, then mark each one Keep, Tweak or Cut with a note. The next revision starts from your marks. Cards marked Changed say what revision 3 changed.

The vessel these cards are for, and the open questions
  • The swarm plays itself. It roams the whole cell in 3D. Nothing you do steers it.
  • You watch. You turn a viewing plane through the cell (drawn like the Wrasse's 2.5D), centred on the swarm's sampled centroid. Turning it changes what you see, never what the swarm does.
  • Phases. Omnicrystal → Elemental crystals → Opposing vessels → Opposing mass, then round again. A phase ends on its timer or when its goal is met: collect an omnicrystal, collect Y elemental crystals, debuff a vessel, reduce opposing mass by X. Proposed: 30 / 25 / 25 / 25 s, Y = 5, X = 30 prisms.
  • Minimum size. Like every vessel, the swarm has a floor: below its minimum size (proposed 6 members) nothing can harm it.
  • Hands. The end of the Omnicrystal phase deals four cards, one per element, crystal or not. Pick one; the other three go. Each card names the phase it shines in.
  • Levels. Upgrading an element makes its cards arrive at that level. Drawing a card you hold adds its level to yours, up to 15.

Each element's job. Mass: prism production (danger or base), shielding, supershielding. Time: your maneuverability, speed, positioning, movement. Space: more range or more prisms you can destroy, steal or shrink. Charge: silencing other vessels' Charge and Space abilities, killing fauna, stripping elemental crystals from other vessels.

No stuns. No card stuns another vessel or takes its control away (no stun, hijack, blind or shove); that protects the fun economy. Silencing Charge and Space abilities, slows, petals and stripping crystals are fine.

Readings to confirm: element upgrades are between-run (meta) progress; levels past 15 are lost; one form at a time; the phase timers and goals above are proposals.

Numbers assume a swarm of about 24 (cap 40, protected minimum 6), about 60 u/s cruise and a 1,400 u view. Each number is linear or a steady percentage step from level 1 to 15; counts round down.
