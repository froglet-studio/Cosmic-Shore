Swarm Deck

The 80 cards of the mobile swarm vessel, revision 1. Each element has 20; each card goes from level 1 to 15. Drag the level to see every card at that level, then mark each one Keep, Tweak or Cut with a note. Revision 2 starts from your marks.

The vessel these cards are for, and the open questions
  • Plane. The swarm is pulled onto a 2D plane, drawn the way the Wrasse draws 2.5D. A touch on the map is a joystick that turns the plane on one or both of its relative axes; each turn resets.
  • Camera. Centred on the swarm's approximate centroid, from a sample of members.
  • Objective toggle. Omnicrystal, Elemental crystals, Opposing vessels, Opposing mass. Each card names the one it works best with.
  • Draw. An omnicrystal draws four cards, one per element. Play one and the other three go. The next omnicrystal draws four more.
  • Levels. Upgrading an element makes its cards arrive at that level. Drawing a card you hold adds its level to yours, up to 15.

Readings to confirm: element upgrades are between-run (meta) progress; levels past 15 are lost; one form at a time; every card is passive once picked (no tap-to-fire).

Numbers assume a swarm of about 24 (cap 40), about 60 u/s cruise and a 1,400 u view. Each number is linear or a steady percentage step from level 1 to 15; counts round down.
