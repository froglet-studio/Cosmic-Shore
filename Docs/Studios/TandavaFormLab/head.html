Four forms · three variants each

A form it has taken is remembered: a cut grows back from the stomach in about a second. Press P to step through its poses: the serpents roll up in a coil, a constrictor's wrap and a figure-eight, every eater has its strike pose, the Many-Headed Serpent lunges every head reared and strikes with them all, and the Antlion lunges jaws wide and snaps them shut. In each the plates leave the head to circle it as danger plates, and it stops laying, so a cut there stays cut.

What this is. The 51 shipped Tandava body plans, upsampled as the game does, and a port of TandavaDirectorCore in the game's small closed cell (800 u): 27 plants of six species that regrow and replant, round-robin bites, funded regrowth, the threat, lunge and mood levers, the serpents' meal coils (a formation drawn per meal), the strike pose, the Many-Headed Serpent's all-heads strike, the Antlion's snapping jaws, the halo and its attendants. The flock is simplified (each tadpole springs to a slot of its element with the sort core's gain, spacing and inertia). Your dart and the wing are stand-ins for the hulls; times are a model, not a Unity measurement.
