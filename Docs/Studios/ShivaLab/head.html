Shiva's Cell

A barren cell, the planets of an orrery wheeling round a god. Your squad of three has come to break him. He sits inside three shells of danger prisms; you cannot touch him, so you thread his rings to get close.

  1. Thread the lit ring. The rings spiral in. Thread the whole circuit before the drum (the clock) runs out and one shell cracks.
  2. He claps. The wave wipes every prism your squad laid and rips every element off a vessel near him. The cell collapses and grows anew.
  3. He changes. The Still Shiva, then Nataraja (the rings dance), then the Devouring Mouth (a black hole in his mouth pulls you in).
  4. The swarm guards the rings. Many-headed serpents coil round them and lunge, sharks ram, piranha nip, clawfish latch on.

Keys: W/S pitch, A/D yaw (or the arrows), Space or Shift boosts. A gamepad's left stick and right trigger work too, and on a phone the left thumb steers and the right thumb boosts.
