Gate steering MinigameRegatta.unity

The shared gate-race pilot (GateRaceController.ArmRacers): far out it aims at a point 480 u behind the ring on its axis (never more than 40% of the leg), inside 420 u it aims 320 u past it, the side latched when the ring changes. A misjudged ring is believed two radii plus 6 u off its centre, in its plane, for up to 6 s. Each hull's drive (how it spends its speed verb) sits on top and is lab-only; see Signature moves.

Play style

On Regatta a style is the hull's starting element levels. Only Time reaches speed, and only on five hulls (regatta_balance.py): "Card" is the card's shipped StartingElements table (Manta Time -5; Serpent, Sparrow and Scarab Time 10).

Hulls

The card's eight hulls on their shipped numbers (the keys regatta_balance.py reads). Cruise = DefaultMinimumSpeed + DefaultThrottleScaler. Turn rate at full stick = scaler + speed × RotationThrottleScaler (deg/s), so a fast hull turns faster. Top = the hull's speed verb at rest; the model time is the balance model's competent pilot, three laps, at this intensity, on the card's elements.

Signature moves

Every hull's moves with their SHIPPED limiters (cooldown, ammo or meter), read from the action assets. On Regatta the rails are super-shielded and PvP is petals only, so only a move that changes SPEED changes a race: those are flown here; the rest are listed for reference.

Where the numbers come from, and what is not modelled
  • Course: RegattaCourse.BuildGates and BuildRails run for real (export_course.sh, the same compile as Tools/Build/regatta_course_harness): 8 rings (110 / 88 / 72 / 54 u mouths at I1-I4), three lanes 22 u off the spine at 120°, one 6 × 6 × 8 super-shielded prism every 8 u, lanes Jade, Ruby, Gold. Start: a 120 u ring 260 u behind gate 0, pointed through it (REGATTA.md section 5). A ring threads when a step crosses its plane inside the mouth, either way round (RaceGateRing.CrossedMouth). 3 laps = 24 rings.
  • Hulls: Manta Soar 180 × (1 + 3b) with yaw 30 + 60 × (1 - b) (maxYawDegPerSec); Rhino ramp to 16.8 × 50 = 840 u/s at +220 u/s² while the stick stays inside the 0.3 plateau (RhinoRampBoostAction.asset), coasting down at 120 u/s² (REGATTA.md section 6.1); Dolphin charge 3.636 s, discharge 2.5 s, peak 68 × 2.259² = 347 u/s (the code's product); Urchin 300 u/s on its own rail, 20 on a rival's (FriendlyTerrainSpeed); Squirrel +0.1 skim energy per rail prism to 5× (300 u/s), decay 0.3/s, a rail ram resets it; Serpent 4 pellets of 0.25, regen 0.07/s, 3× for 3 s; Sparrow afterburner 25 × 5 + 10; Scarab 216 u/s at 90 u/s² (C# defaults), Snap Dash 100 u/s for 0.4 s at Time 5.
  • Rail rams: a rival rail prism halves the throttle of a Manta, Dolphin or Sparrow for 1 s (VesselChangeSpeedByPrism: volume 288 × 0.1 capped at 0.5); the Dolphin also loses half its charge; any rail ram resets a Squirrel's boost; your own colour never slows you. Rhino, Serpent, Scarab, Urchin: no slow wired.
  • Element levels (Time only): Manta Soar × 1 + 0.3 × T/10 (floor 0.7), Sparrow afterburner × 1 + 0.5 T/10 (floor 0.5), Serpent boost and duration × 1 + 0.6 T/10 (floor 0.25), Scarab ceiling × 1 + 0.5 T/10, Dolphin charge rate × 1 + 0.5 T/10 (floor 0.25). The others answer to no element.
  • AI: the shared gate steering (Gate steering panel) plus one lab-only drive per hull: the Rhino keeps its stick in the plateau while the runway pays for the ramp and lets go before the turn (RampBoostAIPolicySO's rule); the Manta soars on lined-up legs and cuts Soar before a tight corner; the Dolphin charges to full (its speed held) and spends the charge with its nose on the ring, then recharges; the Serpent stacks pellets on straights; the Sparrow holds its afterburner on straights; the Scarab Snap Dashes on straights (2 s AI cooldown, ScarabAIBoostPolicy.asset); the Urchin heads for its own lane just ahead and rides it, slipping off a rival's lane unless the ring is within 80 u; the Squirrel flies its domain's rail 9 u off the lane (inside its 7.5 u skimmer from the prism face) to skim it without ramming. Easy and Medium are GateRaceHandicap (SkimRaceDifficulty.asset): a ring noticed 0.5 / 0.25 s late (× 0.5-1.5), 9.9% / 4.5% misjudged.
  • Not modelled: the comeback system, crystals and the crystal detour, collisions between hulls, team scoring beyond the gate sum, the Rhino's energised sword (the only thing that opens a rail), level-5 upgrades other than the Scarab's dash, sound. The game's pilots are richer (the Squirrel flies the Skim Race pilot, the Urchin the shared autopilot driver); read every time here as relative, hull against hull and level against level.
