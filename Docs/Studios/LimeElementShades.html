Cosmic Shore · Lime family · the four element shades

One lime, four lightnesses

Proposal: the four elements wear one yellow-green hue and differ only in lightness, so a group of them reads as one lime instead of four clashing tones, and the eye sorts them by brightness first. Charge is the darkest rung (its plasma lights it up); Mass, Time and Space climb the ladder in the order you pick. Every shade must rest inside Lime in the colour source of truth, clear of the band.

Today's shades spread across 15° of hue (OKLCh 126–141°), and Charge is the lightest of them, not the darkest. The first card shows them for comparison.

What a pick changes

Nothing changes until you pick. Then Tools/Build/kite_mote_colors.py, which picks the shades today, would take your ladder instead of its four-corner search, and write it to the same three places: the Wrasse kite view's motes (KiteNavigatorConfig.asset), the Wrasse Lasso Lab, and the elemental crystals (OriginalColorSetSO.ElementalCrystalColors, which the omni crystal's triangles wear too).

What this page cannot show. In the game the free elemental crystals are dimmed to 0.45 and bloom past a threshold, so a dark rung loses its glow first. Charge's plasma is meant to make up for that. Only the editor can judge the lit, moving result.
