The subject is Trace, a real living person in a world where people like this are ordinary: booby and jellyfish and human in one body: one creature, born this way, and no part of Trace looks added to another. Trace is a blue-footed booby whose head turns to jellyfish toward the back, as if the bird had dived once and never quite dried. It is one head with a booby's outline: a flat forehead running straight into a long, pointed slate blue-grey bill, and a rounded back no wider or higher than a seabird's. The back two fifths of the head is the jellyfish: thick, milky, pale lilac jelly in the shape the feathered head would have, the white flecks carried on into it as pale dots and the last dark feathers lying inside it. Its only edge is low at the nape, below the level of the eyes, soft and lobed, with shadow under it, and from under it hang four thick ruffled frills of the same jelly, the longest just reaching the collarbone.
