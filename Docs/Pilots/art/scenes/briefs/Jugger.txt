The display is as sharp as the face. The figure is seen from the knees up, standing. The head is sharp and lit, the whole face easy to read, the place behind it soft.

The subject is Jugger, a real living person in a world where people like this are ordinary: orca and eagle and human in one body. ORCA is the face and the shape of the head: an orca's large rounded whale head with no neck and no beak: a smooth blunt glossy black dome, a short wide snout, a long wide mouth showing a row of small conical teeth, an oval white patch behind each eye, and a white chin and throat. In detail: the head is glossy black with an oval white patch behind each eye and a white chin and throat; the eagle's snow-white feathers sweep back from behind the face over the neck to the shoulders; the folded wings are dark brown.

From the neck down Jugger is built like a person and stands, sits and moves like one: huge and smooth, broad as a doorway, heavy and unhurried. The bridge of a great ship at night, in the middle of a long fight: a wide low room of dark worn metal, patched and re-plated in a dozen different years, with a band of thick windows looking out into indigo haze where small lights move. It shows one great dark ship, no longer than a forearm in the display, its long hull built of many thousands of small blocks, with one round tunnel straight through it. A shoal of skeletal creatures is closing on it from every side, each a spray of slate-blue spindles round a faceted cream crystal heart, some finned like sharks, some round-headed like tadpoles, some rayed like brittle stars; where they have fed, blocks are gone from the hull.
