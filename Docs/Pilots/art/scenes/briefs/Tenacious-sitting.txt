An environmental portrait by a documentary photographer, made in the first quiet minute after the work was done. Medium format, an 80mm lens at f/2.8, the camera at the subject's eye height and two steps away. The subject has stopped where they were, sits, and looks straight into the lens and holds the look. Framed from the knees up, the place soft behind. Every trace of the last hours is kept: wet fur in clumps, grime in the creases, a tired and steady face. Nothing retouched.

The subject is Tenacious, a real living person in a world where people like this are ordinary: tiger and tortoise and human in one body. It is the same person as in Tenacious's portrait, feature for feature. TIGER is the face and the shape of the head: a tiger's broad face, orange fur with black stripes, white cheeks and brows. TORTOISE is the shell and everything behind the face: a domed cap of tortoise-shell plates on top of the head, each olive-brown scute ringed with golden growth lines; the back of the head, the nape, the throat and the neck down into the collar are all tortoise, wrinkled, scaly olive tortoise skin, meeting the tiger's face along the jawline and the temples in a soft natural blend. The eyes are a person's, forward-facing and expressive with a little white showing at the corners, warm hazel irises each ringed by a thin glowing jade teal line (the pilot's sight). Human hair shows only below the tortoise's crown, never over it: long straight black human hair falling behind the shoulders. As the portrait shows: no ears show, because the shell cap covers the whole top of the head down to the brow like a close helmet; the nose is pink and the whiskers long and white; the tortoise skin of the neck is olive green; the jacket has a stand collar lined in teal and plain grey reflective bands on the sleeves, and the harness is black webbing with steel buckles.

From the neck down Tenacious is built like a person and stands, sits and moves like one: tall, broad-shouldered and powerful. Both hands are a person's hands in gloves: heavy orange-and-black rescue gloves, scuffed grey at the palms. Each hand has fingers and a thumb and holds things the way a person's hand does. The tortoise also gives what the portrait shows behind the shoulders, the high domed rim of a tortoise carapace rising behind the neck and shoulders like a great collar: seen whole, the tortoise's shell covers the back from the shoulders to the hips, and the clothes are cut to close around it.

Tenacious wears the same clothes as in the portrait, seen whole and lived in: a reflective rescue jacket in signal orange with a chest harness, piped in jade teal (#0EC0B6), the piping a thin cord that glows faintly; below it, signal-orange over-trousers to match the jacket, with grey reflective bands, and heavy black rescue boots. The jacket is soaked and streaked with soot; one forearm of it is ripped open in a ragged tear with the grey lining showing; one reflective band is half peeled away; the harness webbing is frayed at a buckle; the gloves are blackened; the shell cap and the shell behind the shoulders are scuffed, with pale scrapes across the plates; the black hair hangs wet in ropes; the cheek fur is clumped with rain and smudged with soot.

Tenacious has just come off a long rescue and sits on an upturned equipment case at the edge of the deck, elbows on knees, a dented steel mug steaming between both gloved hands, and looks straight into the lens: bone tired, calm and faintly amused.

The open deck of a rescue station at first light after a storm: wet steel plates, coiled lines and blank crates of gear stacked against a rail, two orange rescue boats in their cradles soft in the background, and a beacon lamp still turning far behind, laying a red-orange glow into the lifting rain. A few tiny faceted crystal grains drift in the air like dust in a sunbeam. Flat grey daylight from the overcast sky on the face, soft and even; the far beacon's red-orange as a faint warm edge on the wet shell and one shoulder; fine rain still falling, a few drops caught sharp.

All of it is photographed as it is, with a real camera: living anatomy with the fur, feathers, hide and scales of real animals, every hair resolved, wet where it is wet; real cloth, leather and metal with weight and wear; real dirt. The head is Tenacious's own living head, never a mask or a costume. The eye rings and the piping glow no brighter than a reflection. One person only. Nothing in the frame carries writing, numbers or a symbol: every label, sign, crate and screen is blank. No weapon, no watermark, no border, no human ears.
