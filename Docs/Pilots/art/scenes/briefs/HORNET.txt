The subject is Zeko Mirethorn, a real living person in a world where people like this are ordinary: mantis and newt and human in one body. Zeko stands under the hazard lamp on the last marker of a danger shoal and does not move, as a mantis waits at a lit window: one damp four-fingered hand is raised to chest height and held perfectly still, and a single glass-winged lampfly has settled on its first finger. Hundreds more circle the lamp. The triangular head is tilted a little and turned three-quarters to the camera; the great pale eyes look past the lamp and out over the dark water, solemn and far-seeing. The tail hangs straight down behind, its tip just clear of the wet planks.
