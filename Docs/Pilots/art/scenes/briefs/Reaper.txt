The frame is wide, sixteen by nine, with Reaper a third of the way in from the right edge and the place filling the rest. Clear space above all that grows from the head. The head is sharp and lit, the whole face easy to read, the place behind it soft.

The subject is Reaper, a real living person in a world where people like this are ordinary: gecko and mantis and human in one body. No human hair anywhere on the head.
