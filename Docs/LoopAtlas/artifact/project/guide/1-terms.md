# The ladder of words

Eight tiers, from one input to the months-long game. Place a problem on a tier before arguing about it.

| Tier | One pass | What it is | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Era** | 2 wk to 6 mo | What changes without you: the living sea, sagas, the meta, new vessels. | Why come back next month? |
| **Arc** | 1 d to 4 wk | What changes between sittings: unlocks, the fleet, the squad, the crew, the hoard. | What do I have tomorrow that I did not have today? |
| **Session** | 10 min to 45 min | A sitting in one place: a ladder run, an arena set, a voyage. | Do I want one more, and does anything point me to another place? |
| **Round** | 60 s to 6 min | One objective, won or lost: an arcade or arena match, or a cell on a voyage. | Can I read who is winning and why, in about 90 seconds? |
| **Role** | 20 s to 3 min | The kit pointed at the game's goals: what this vessel does for its team. | Does what I am good at matter to the objective? |
| **Combo** | 8 s to 60 s | Verbs chained inside one kit. The loops a kit has baked in. | Does one verb set up the next? |
| **Verb** | 2 s to 20 s | One elemental ability, from trigger to payoff and back to ready. | Is the payoff worth the trigger, and where does the pleasure land? |
| **Pulse** | 0.1 s to 3 s | One input and the hull's answer. The feel of flying. | Is it a pleasure with nothing to do? |

## Drivers

Why a player stays inside a loop.

| Driver | Kind | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Sensation** | inner | The raw joy of the movement itself. |
| **Mastery** | inner | Getting better: a cleaner line, a harder rung. |
| **Creation** | inner | Making mass, growing, building something that stays. |
| **Destruction** | inner | Breaking mass. The release of a big hit. |
| **Rivalry** | inner | Beating another pilot: a place, a petal, a claim. |
| **Fellowship** | inner | Playing with and for a team or a party. |
| **Discovery** | inner | Something new: a toy, a hull, an uncharted place. |
| **Collection** | inner | Having it: vessels, pilots, artifacts, relics. |
| **Stakes** | inner | Something of yours can be lost. |
| **Story** | inner | People, factions and sagas that remember you. |
| **Objective** | outer | Goals laid out on the map by the game you are in. |
| **Reward** | outer | Crystals, pay and unlocks handed over at the end. |

## Hand-offs

What moves a player out of one loop and into another.

| Hand-off | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Tank full** | A resource the loop was banking is full, so you go and spend it one tier up. |
| **Objective met** | The loop's goal is reached and the tier above takes its next step. |
| **Loss** | The loop is lost and the tier above resets or sends you home. |
| **Reward to spend** | You hold something that is only useful in another loop. |
| **Something new** | A new hull, card, place or piece of news opens a loop you have not run. |
| **A friend** | An invite or a party moves you to where someone else is. |
| **Enough of this** | The loop has stopped paying and you look for another at the same tier. |

## Other words

- **Pass**: one time round a loop.
- **Step**: one bead on a loop's ring.
- **Step up, step down**: a move between tiers. Hierarchy.
- **Crossing**: a move between loops at the same tier, such as Arcade to Arena. Heterarchy.
- **Feed**: one loop's output is another loop's input, such as crystals from a round into the fleet.
- **Place**: the Toybox, the Arcade, the Arena, Voyages.
- **Kit**: a vessel's four elemental verbs, its omni crystal, its combos and its feel. A game mode never changes it.
- **Lean**: where a vessel sits between cultivator and destroyer. Measured from play, never set.
