# LoopRing

One loop, the way every diagram of ours should draw it: a ring, its steps as diamonds clockwise from the top, the faster loops it is made of inside it, a spoke from a step to the loop that step opens, a tag for the count of passes, and the loop's own card at the top of the ring. The preview shows a voyage's cell on the Planned layer. Ring colour is the tier (`tier-N`); `planned` and `proposed` replace it for loops that are not in the game; `selection` marks the picked ring; `hole` is the diamond count. Do not colour a loop by element: elements have no colour.
