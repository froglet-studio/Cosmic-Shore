# AI genes and activation expectations

Generated by `python3 Tools/Build/loop_atlas.py build` from `model/fleet/*.yaml`, `model/genes.yaml` and the
loops' `measure` blocks; do not edit. The rules: `Docs/LoopAtlas/README.md` (Genes), `Docs/AI_SYSTEM/AI_PLAN.md`.

Every combo is also an AI behaviour: one gene of the vessel's genome for one element (Docs/AI_SYSTEM/AI_PLAN.md, one AI per vessel per element). A gene names the cues that start the combo, the dials a search learns (each with the range it may try; a seed is whatever the shipped AI does today, and no dial is typed by hand after a search writes it), the moment it counts as done, and the pilot callsign whose sight leans into it. A pilot only changes what the AI goes after, never what the vessel can do. The same done-moment is the combo_completed event, so the AI and the player are measured by one count.

**Activation:** A new pilot completes the first combo listed for a vessel at least once within their first three rounds on that vessel. The Mentor teaches that combo after the Lesson (arc.activation, hole 1). combo_completed carries vessel, combo, genome, seconds and whether a person or an AI flew it, so the same number reads activation and AI coverage.
