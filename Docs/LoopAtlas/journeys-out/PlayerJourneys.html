Player Journeys

Five players move through Cosmic Shore's loops over their first four weeks. Each dot circles the loop it is in, at that loop's own pace, and jumps when the player moves on. Between sittings it rests in Away. Every rate comes from a hypothesis in the Loop Atlas's players.yaml, so treat this as a model to check against analytics, not a measurement.
