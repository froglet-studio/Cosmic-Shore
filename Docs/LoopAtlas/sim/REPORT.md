# Loop timescales across player cultures: simulation report

Generated by `python3 Tools/Build/loop_timescale_sim.py report` from the loop model and `Docs/LoopAtlas/sim/`. Do not edit by hand. The segment numbers are priors, not measurements (each says how sure it is in `segments.yaml`): read the comparisons between scenarios and personas, not the absolute values.

Each persona is simulated 200 times for 42 days (one Tide) in three scenarios that follow the atlas layers:

- **today** (Now): the master developer unlock is on, so every place and vessel is open, no guide runs, and crystals buy nothing.
- **planned** (Planned): the gate flipped. The quest chain (I4 on four modes) opens the Hangar, Arena and Voyages; a voyage is the planned 20 to 40 minutes; vessels cost 4,000 and only placing pays.
- **proposed** (Proposed): planned plus these fixes:
  - `tide`: P6 The Tide: a daily job, a weekly card, a six-week season (era.tide, build queued 2026-11-20)
  - `one_currency`: P10 One currency: a clean voyage pays about three arcade wins in crystals
  - `sinks`: P7 More to spend crystals on: Ark refits, pilots, colour sets
  - `chapters`: P13 Voyage chapters: dock at any location reached and resume next sitting
  - `puzzle_door`: P14 A puzzle-first door: the Ratel's board is offered on Home and in the first-login guide
  - `crew_seat`: P15 Every crew vessel seats an arena card and a voyage squad, so two people share one seat
  - `quest_rung`: arc.unlock hole: a quest clears at I2, matching ECONOMY_TABLES' ~3 games to the Hangar
  - `make_pays`: P16 Making pays: a finished painting pays a little and can be shared, so the Toybox feeds the long game
  - `breath`: P5 The breath: after each result one offered next step, so a sitting crosses places

## 4. What each persona's path shows

### Mira (casual-mobile)

A mother who plays the Ratel's hex puzzle as a casual puzzle game on her phone, and gets roped into her son's squad.

Challenge: The game has no puzzle-first way in. The Ratel is reached only through the Toybox Vessel Changer and a seat swap, the quest chain asks her to win four flying races first, and the Honeyguide has no arena card, so the day her son asks her in, there is no seat for her.

- D7 30% today, 41% planned, 71% proposed; D30 4%, 3%, 94%.
- Hangar: day 34 (1%) planned, day 3 (100%) proposed. First vessel: never planned, day 27 (91%) proposed.
- Found the Ratel: day 5 (49%) today. Asked into a squad with no seat for her: 1.6 times today, 0.9 planned, 0.0 proposed. Share of her rounds flown when she wanted the puzzle: 62% today, 100% planned.
- Voyages started and abandoned before the dock (haul lost): 47% planned, 0% today, 0% proposed (chapters).

### Jonah (teen-competitive)

Mira's son, 14. Arena most days after school with two friends; flies the Honeyguide when his mum rides along.

Challenge: The competitive core. How fast he reaches the arena decides whether he stays long enough to bring anyone in.

- D7 80% today, 93% planned, 100% proposed; D30 99%, 100%, 100%.
- Hangar: day 2 (100%) planned, day 0 (100%) proposed. First vessel: day 2 (100%) planned, day 2 (100%) proposed.
- Voyages started and abandoned before the dock (haul lost): 18% planned, 7% today, 0% proposed (chapters).
- The four authored sagas (12 chapters) were spent by day 29 (1%) planned.

### The Night Crew (pc-core-adult)

Four friends in their 20s on PC. The arena most nights, voyages binged on weekends, all on voice.

Challenge: Four people, three seats. A squad and a voyage party are three, so one friend sits out every match unless a crew vessel takes two of them; and a weekend binge of voyages runs out of authored sagas fast.

- D7 34% today, 50% planned, 80% proposed; D30 4%, 42%, 100%.
- Hangar: day 2 (100%) planned, day 0 (100%) proposed. First vessel: day 3 (100%) planned, day 3 (100%) proposed.
- Voyages started and abandoned before the dock (haul lost): 10% planned, 3% today, 0% proposed (chapters).
- Team matches with one of the group sitting out: 47% planned, 0% proposed.
- The four authored sagas (12 chapters) were spent by day 20 (36%) planned.

### The Painter (creative-sandbox)

Toybox only. Paints with trails, flies the Wanderway, shares what they make, never competes.

Challenge: Earns nothing. Crystals come only from placing in matches, so the Toybox pays no progress and the long game never starts.

- D7 24% today, 27% planned, 33% proposed; D30 2%, 1%, 9%.

Gets three or four friends into a party most evenings on mid-range phones; picks whatever everyone can play.
