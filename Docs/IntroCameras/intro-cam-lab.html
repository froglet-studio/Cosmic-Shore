    How this plays in Unity

    Each mode belongs to one of six families. Before a match the game picks one of the family's intros at random, never the same one twice in a row, and shows that mode's own title, tagline and objective over it.

    • The intros live in Resources/IntroCameraLibrary.asset, written by Tools/Build/author_intro_cameras.py.
    • Shots aim at things in the real arena (the cell centre, your vessel, rivals, crystals, race gates, the biggest wrecks and cacti), not at this page's coordinates, and scale with the cell.
    • Every caption is an authored text field; an empty one shows nothing.
    • The hand-off to the pilot, Skip (0.3 s) and the gamepad South button work as before. A mode outside every family still plays the old orbit.
