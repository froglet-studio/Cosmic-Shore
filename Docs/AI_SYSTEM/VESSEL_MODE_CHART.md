Generated by `python3 Tools/Build/vessel_mode_chart.py --write`. Do not edit by hand; re-run it. It reads the
cards, the prefabs, the ability maps, the AI wiring in the tree and every flight stored with Store AI record
(`origin/bugledger/*`: 148 flights from belac, gradi, mrglo, owner, shombith, yskha).

## 1. The AIs built for Regatta, and whether they are ready elsewhere

Every hull's AI has two halves:
- **Steering** comes from the MODE. Every ring race (Switchback, Headlong, Redline, Breakwater, Skein, Regatta, Waystation) shares
  `GateRaceController`'s steering. Other modes give the platform autopilot their own objectives.
- **The drive** (when to spend the hull's speed verb) belongs to the HULL and works in any mode.

Regatta-only extras (no crystal detour is every gate race's since 2026-10-26): the commit-from-approach-point fix, the Urchin's rail aim, and the
`_Regatta` tuning variants.

So a Regatta AI hull is **ready to fly** any mode whose card offers it. It is **only tuned and tested** in the
modes listed under *Tuned and tested in* (Regatta, plus Skim Race for the Squirrel).

| Hull | Speed verb | AI drive | Tuned and tested in | Other live cards that offer it | Of those, ring races |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dolphin** | drift charge, then discharge | ChargeBoostAIPolicySO | Regatta | Bends, Broadside, BroodRush, Rampage, Switchback | Switchback |
| **Manta** | Soar: boost costs yaw | Soar drive in MantaAnalogTurnBoostExecutor + MantaSoarAITuningSO | Regatta | Bloomrush, Broadside, BroodRush, Maelstrom, Redline, Scurry | Redline |
| **Rhino** | ramp: hold straight to wind up speed | RampBoostAIPolicySO (ramp / brake / off) | Regatta | AstroLeague, Broadside, BroodRush, Cleave, Headlong | Headlong |
| **Squirrel** | skims a rail for boost | SkimRacePilot (Skim Race crystals; Regatta: RegattaRingObjective) | Regatta, SkimRace I1, SkimRace I2, SkimRace I4 | AstroLeague, Broadside, BroodRush, Joust, Maelstrom, Scurry, SkimRace | none |
| **Stoat** | black-hole / white-hole pair: warp the path and fly it | dipole autopilot in StoatDipole/Sling executors | Regatta | Slingshot, Warpline | none |
| **Urchin** | rides its own rail | UrchinAutopilotDriver (rail modes: Regatta, Skein) | Regatta | Broadside, Hijack, Skein | Skein |
