Three domains.
A perceptual space
much larger than three colours.

A printed page fools the eye with three inks in a dot matrix. Here's the twist: a prism's HDR emission doesn't glow in its team colour — it glows near-CMY (Jade azure, Ruby violet, Gold amber). Our board is, physically, a print triad — rendered in additive light with a whole extra dimension to spend. This bench pushes the limit: what colour, value, form, image, and even motion can be tricked into perception purely by how prisms are arranged in 3D. Drag any stage to orbit.

The trick, generalised to a volume

Additive light is the honest medium here — glowing prisms on black sum, so an azure prism beside a violet one reads blue at distance, and all three together drift toward a cool white. That gives five independent knobs to encode a target the eye then reconstructs. Each station isolates one — and the Limits are stated honestly: the triad is a compact wedge, not the whole rainbow.

STATION 01

Value from binary dots

Density modulation. One domain, two states — dot or no dot. Vary how many prisms occupy a region and the eye reads a smooth luminance ramp. This is the ink-coverage axis of halftoning, lifted into a 3D shell.

Mechanism
Local coverage c ∈ [0,1] → apparent value; the retina low-passes the dot field.
Encodes
A Lambert-shaded sphere, rendered with a single-colour prism cloud.
Limit
Value ceiling is fixed by dot brightness; past full coverage it clips to a glowing blob.
STATION 02

Surface from a dot cloud

Connect-the-dots → splat. Sparse points with soft additive falloff read as a continuous surface — the perceptual bridge to the Gaussian-splat reference this branch was chasing. Nothing is solid; the surface is inferred.

Mechanism
Overlapping glow kernels close the gaps; contour + occlusion cues do the rest.
Encodes
A trefoil knot as pure position — no mesh, no faces.
Sweep
Drop the point budget and watch the surface dissolve back into a constellation.
STATION 03

A full-colour image from three

3D error-diffusion. A target picture is quantised to the nearest domain per voxel, the error pushed to neighbours. Up close you see Jade/Ruby/Gold grain; pull back and the eye fuses it into a continuous-tone image.

Mechanism
Floyd–Steinberg on a lattice: err → forward + downstream neighbours.
Encodes
A synthesised planet-over-hypersea scene, mapped onto a curtain.
Honesty
Blues & purples reproduce well; saturated greens desaturate — the triad rings green. See Limits.
STATION 04 · the 3D-only move

Three images in one construct

View-dependent colour. Print can't do this. Each prism is angularly gated — visible only within a cone facing one viewpoint. Orbit and the whole sculpture cross-fades between a JADE, a RUBY, and a GOLD word, occupying the same space.

Mechanism
Per-dot preferred azimuth; α = gauss(view − θ★). A lenticular lattice.
Encodes
Three messages superimposed — the same volume, three readings.
Payoff
Territory that literally shows a different face to each seat at the table.
STATION 05

Phantom depth & motion

Structured moiré. Two domain lattices at slightly different periods beat against each other. The interference fringes carry apparent depth, and they glide as you orbit — motion emerging from a completely static construct.

Mechanism
Beat period P = p₁p₂/|p₁−p₂| ≫ either lattice; small offsets swing it hugely.
Encodes
A third colour (the overlap) and a moving band no prism actually moves along.
Detune
Slide the period mismatch to speed up or freeze the phantom.
STATION 06 · beyond the triangle

Colour the triangle can't hold

Temporal PWM + assimilation. Flicker a site between two domains faster than flicker-fusion and persistence of vision blends them in time, not space — a fourth mixing axis. Paired with simultaneous contrast, apparent colours drift toward hues no static prism owns.

Mechanism
Duty cycle d sets the time-average; the eye integrates over ~50 ms.
Encodes
Left half temporal-mixed, right half spatial-mixed — same target, two routes.
Cost
Flicker below fusion is fatiguing; a real build must stay above ~50 Hz.
STATION 07 · the monument you can only see once

An image hidden in one line of sight

Anamorphic single-ray. Every prism is scattered to a random depth along the ray from one anchor viewpoint through one pixel of a target. From that single approach vector the cloud collapses into a floating pointillist crest. Step off the line and parallax shreds it back to embers.

Mechanism
Depth is free along a ray: x = iₓ·(d−z) projects to the same pixel for every z — at the anchor only.
Encodes
A crest that resolves from ONE angle; bury a second along another ray and each shows only to its own vantage.
Payoff
A shrine invisible except from one heading — signage the whole table can't read, only the pilot on-axis.
Limit
The lock is a few degrees wide — ~95% of viewpoints see noise. Great for a secret, useless for a billboard.

Where the trick runs out

  • △The reachable gamut is the triangle spanned by the three primaries — and measured against sRGB it's a compact wedge, ≈⅕ of the cube. It spans azure → violet → amber, so blues, teals, periwinkles, purples, plums and ambers are all yours by arrangement. What's outside: saturated pure green (there's no green primary, only cyan and amber flanking it) and pure spectral red — both desaturate as you push for them.
  • ◐There is no true neutral white: an equal mix lands at a cool lavender (D65 sits just outside the triangle), so "grey" always reads faintly cold. The single hue you cannot fake is the one that would need a colour outside the wedge — and simultaneous-contrast ("impossible" colours) can nudge a patch there, but only in the eye, never in the pixels or a screenshot.
  • ☼Value has a ceiling: additive coverage tops out and clips to a bright blob. Dark tones are easy (fewer dots); brilliant tones fight the black ground, and a perceptually-even ramp needs a cubic coverage curve.
  • ◴Temporal (PWM) mixing hits the same triangle at zero grain — a resolution tool, not a gamut expander — and below flicker-fusion it's fatiguing and strobes on capture.
  • ⊙Every spatial trick is distance-bound: it fuses only when the dot subtense drops below ~1 arc-minute. Too close and the illusion decomposes into its prisms — the honest signature of the medium — so dithering must be isotropic in 3D or it shows worms when you orbit off-axis.

Why this belongs in the real prism system

  • 01Prisms already are the dots. These constructs are trails/prismscapes at a particular density — no new primitive, exactly the "compose the fundamentals" mandate.
  • 02Domain is the ink. Colour comes only from Jade/Ruby/Gold material sets; the gamut is emergent from arrangement, never a hand-picked shade.
  • 03Mass is conserved. A construct is a fixed stock of prisms — it blooms in, holds, and is only removed by an active force. Nothing here needs decay or a timer.
  • 04View-dependence is a mechanic, not a gimmick. Territory that shows each player a different face is a design surface the 2D analogy simply can't offer.
