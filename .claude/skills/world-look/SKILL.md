# /world-look: the look of a thing in the world

## 0. The bar, in one paragraph

Garrett, 2026-10-18 (LOCKED): "Nothing in the environment should ever look flat like this, that is reserved for ui."
Garrett, 2026-10-21 (LOCKED): "always go for the 3d in world solution over the 2d flat text solution." A thing in the
world is a lit, faceted volume in the fleet's own two-tone (a dark body, a bright square-on face, the domain as one
accent), scaled with the thing it belongs to, and it shows a world fact on itself, never as a ring or a word on the
screen. One wormhole look took 26 rounds and had eight looks removed; the Orca's missiles "feel cheap"; the Urchin's
cradle was "proven by a harness and wrong on screen" twice. The owner's eye decides; this skill makes a session
look first and bring one look, not eight.

  "lit" means in this fleet: the facing two-tone, not Unity's Lit shader).
- `references/VOCABULARY.md`: the effect primitives the game already owns, each with its shipped reference and
  whether it is lit or a flat offender today; the ones still to draw.
- `references/LESSONS.md`: what each round taught, the owner's words first (the crystal capture, the Urchin cradle,
  the ring explosion, the switch ring's facets, the missiles, the prism's death, the vortex ring, the wormhole).
- `Docs/Shaders/WORLD_LOOK_AUDIT.md`: every world effect, its shader and render mode, the signals (regenerated by
  the kit). `Docs/Shaders/WORLD_LOOK_SKILL_PLAN.md`: the plan and the round status.
- `Docs/claude/SHADERS_AND_PERFORMANCE.md` (the two LOCKED rules, orthographic views, the PiP frame),
  `Docs/Shaders/WORMHOLE_SHADERS.md` (one look's whole history, with how to bring each removed look back),
  `/prism-morph` (prisms are the world's material) and `/asset-surgery` (how a prefab, material or graph is written).
- Who to ask: Garrett for the look, Yash for the Meerkat's wormhole, Shombith for anything a UI shader touches.

## 2. The pipeline

```
python3 Tools/Build/look_kit/audit.py                    # the audit: Docs/Shaders/WORLD_LOOK_AUDIT.md regenerated
python3 Tools/Build/look_kit/audit.py --check            # the gate: a NEW offender not in the baseline fails
python3 Tools/Build/look_kit/audit.py --baseline         # after an owner accepts a look: the baseline moves
python3 Tools/Build/look_kit/audit.py --json | --scenes  # for machines; scene-placed effects too
python3 Tools/Build/look_kit/audit.py --self-test        # the negative controls
```

The audit reads every prefab under `Assets/_Prefabs` (560 on 2026-11-07), finds the world effects (67: particle
systems, trails, lines, meshes on unlit or particle shaders, and the munitions by name) and raises seven signals:
`unlit-shader`, `unlit-by-content` (a graph on the Unlit sub-target with no normal, fresnel or light node, read
through its sub-graphs and custom HLSL), `additive-sheet`, `billboard-particles`, `screen-ring`, `thin-mesh`,
`no-normals`. 72 signals on 25 effects are the baseline: owed work, not a gate failure; a new one is stopped.

What does not exist yet (the next kit step, in the icon kit's shape): `effect.py`, an effect as a SPEC (geometry,
material slots, motion curve, size relative to its owner); the builders for the common shapes (shard burst, a ring
as a volume, lance, trail, veil) writing meshes, materials and prefabs with stable guids through the
`/asset-surgery` techniques; `verify.py` with the lit, depth, scale, phone-budget and comfort gates. Until the look
sheet (`/unity-look`) runs, the sheet is Blender Cycles for geometry and motion (the vessel sheets' way) and an
Amoebius clip for the effect in play (`Docs/claude/VIDEOS_AND_PREVIEWS.md`).

## 3. The round

1. **Brief**: the thing, what fact it shows, the owner it belongs to (its size is relative to that), the primitives
   from `VOCABULARY.md` it is made of, the rules in `LOOK_LANGUAGE.md` it must obey; write it as a design brief
   (`Docs/claude/DESIGN_BRIEF.md`) with the principle ids.
2. **Three thumbnails**, cheap: a lab, a Blender render, an Amoebius frame; pick by looking at the game's distance
   (the chase camera's follow distance, the far view, the card), not at a studio close-up.
3. **Build** it through the asset tools, lit in the fleet's two-tone, the domain as one accent, no additive sheet,
   no billboard, no screen ring; run `audit.py --check`.
4. **The sheet**: look at it before anyone else does (`/unity-look` when it runs; Amoebius or Cycles today). Write
   what fails the language in words before touching a number.
5. **The owner flies it**; one look, one round. Fold what they say into `LESSONS.md` and, if it is a rule, into
   `LOOK_LANGUAGE.md`; move the baseline only when the look is accepted.

## 4. Rules that keep being relearned

- A harness is not a sheet: two cradle rounds passed their harness and failed on screen (LESSONS 4).
- When told "looks terrible", do not tune the dials first; the surface is wrong, not the number (LESSONS 5).
- Brightness flattens; contrast between facets is what reads (LESSONS 9).
- A LOCKED rule in a topic file did not stop the next flat effect; a gate at the prefab does (LESSONS 17).
- One look lives in one config, not per prefab (LESSONS 2); eight looks in one shader is eight looks removed.
- A motion is judged at the camera's distance, not in units (LESSONS 13).
- The order of events is a look; what shows first is the fact (LESSONS 14).

## 5. Wiring and hand-off

- An effect reaches a prefab through the asset tools (`/asset-surgery`), never a hand edit; the perf gates
  (`Docs/claude/SHADERS_AND_PERFORMANCE.md`) and `audit.py --check` run before the commit.
- The checklist entry names the sheet; the PR body shows it; one team-task line per branch for the editor look.
- Round status: `Docs/Shaders/WORLD_LOOK_SKILL_PLAN.md` section 8 (R0 built 2026-11-07; R1 the Orca's missile and
  bomb, R2 crystal capture, R3 the black hole's ring, R4 a trail: proposed, Garrett's order).
