# The look of a thing in the world (DRAFT to put to Garrett; nothing here is confirmed)

Drafted 2026-11-07 from the two LOCKED rules and the owners' dated words, for round R0 of
`Docs/SKILL_TRAINING_PLAN.md` §3.2. The shape is the icon language's
(`.claude/skills/ability-icon/references/ICON_LANGUAGE.md`): a short rule, the words it comes from,
a test a sheet or a gate can apply. Garrett owns the look; Yash owns the Meerkat's wormhole; Shombith
the UI-side shaders (§3.2 "Ask"). Nothing below has been put to any of them. Distances are world units.

**What "lit" means in this fleet, as measured 2026-11-07.** Every project shader graph that draws a world
thing is a URP **Unlit** sub-target (`BlockGraph`, `CrystalGraph`, `VesselGraph`, `SpindleGraph`,
`ExplosionGraph`, `WispGraph`, `LaserGraph` ...; read from the graph JSON). Facets are told by FACING, not
by a scene light: the hull's slot is `lerp(_Color1, _Color2, facing)`, a rim colour and a face colour
(`Docs/VesselModels/COLOR.md:24-28`); the switch ring reads because an edge-on facet is the dark body and a
facet square to the camera is the signal, about 5.5x brighter (`Docs/SPINDLE_LOD.md:505-512`); the prism's
`BlockGraph` shades by its normal. So "lit" in this draft is that two-tone (a dark body, a bright face, the
domain plate brightest), with the sun's glint only where a lens already has one (`Docs/Shaders/WORMHOLE_SHADERS.md`
§14.1). It is not Unity's Lit shader. That reading is the one decision at the end.

## The one decision to confirm

What Garrett has to say yes to before the kit is built: **every world effect is built from the fleet's prism-cut
vocabulary in the fleet's own materials (a dark body, a bright square-on face, the domain as one accent), and
"lit" means that facing two-tone, which `VesselGraph`, `BlockGraph` and the switch ring already draw; so the kit's
flatness gate measures depth and facet contrast on a tile, not whether a shader is Unity's Lit.** Saying yes
carries two consequences: the line-and-ribbon drawings still in the game are owed a redraw as volumes, in rounds
(the sniper / lance beam on `Sprites/Default`, `SniperBeam.cs:201-202`; the `TrailRenderer` ribbons on the Manta,
Sparrow, Thresher, the Grizzly bomb and the skyburst missile; the Thresher chain's view-aligned ribbon); and a lens
(the wormhole, the black hole, the vortex ring) is the one shape that is not a volume, the world seen bent, so the
rule needs a lens clause: lit by what it bends (a rim, a glint), never tinted (`WORMHOLE_SHADERS.md:82`).

My recommendation, as the icon plan gave one (`Docs/Icons/ABILITY_ICON_SKILL_PLAN.md` §4): yes to the facing two-tone
as "lit", because the whole fleet already draws that way, a Unity Lit pass would be a second look beside it, and a
phone pays for it (rule 10); and the lens clause as written.

## What this draft does not know

- Whether "lit" may use the scene's sun. The wormhole takes a glint of `RenderSettings.sun` (§14.1); `VesselGraph`
  has no light input at all.
- The far distance's one number: the thread tier at 1,000, the band's 1,200 crossing, or `farFullEnd` at 2,000
  (`VESSEL_VISION.md:306`). Rule 9 names the first two.
- Whether the yardsticks were accepted. The record holds Yash's ask for §16 (2026-11-01) and nothing after it; the
  Manta's trail is a "follow-up" line, which the plan calls accepted (`SKILL_TRAINING_PLAN.md:243-244`); the ember
  has no owner's word beyond the colour language; the death bloom had Garrett's rounds 12-15 directing it and no
  closing word. "Shipped and not sent back" is all that can be said.
- What "cheap" covers beyond the two records (the missiles, the hulls): whether a silent effect or one the camera
  ignores is also cheap.
- How far the Kite's exception reaches: "the prisms that go out of plane for the kite should be unlit but not
  disappear" (Garrett 2026-10-18, `SHADERS_AND_PERFORMANCE.md:24-27`). An owner's explicit call for one view wins and is
  recorded where it is made; this draft assumes it is that one view.
- How a session judges the phone tile before the look sheet runs in CI: Amoebius's renderer is not Unity's
  (`Docs/claude/VIDEOS_AND_PREVIEWS.md:93-94`), so until then Blender Cycles stands in for geometry and motion, the
  hull sheets' way, and the final look is the editor's.
- Whether the HUD's petals and the Starlings' element marks stay out of the world. Assumed: they are UI
  (`Docs/claude/VESSEL_HUD_AND_ELEMENTS.md:7-15`; ICON vocabulary's last line).
- Whether a particle system (28 in 19 prefabs) can be a volume at all. Most draw additive sheets; the draft assumes
  `look_kit/audit.py` measures them and the failing ones are redrawn as shard bursts.
- Who decides when Yash's wormhole and Garrett's rule disagree. §3.2's "Ask" row splits them; this draft does not
  settle it.
