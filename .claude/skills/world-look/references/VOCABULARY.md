# The effect primitives the game already owns (and the ones it still needs)

The same thing is the same shape everywhere, as the icon vocabulary says of pictograms
(`.claude/skills/ability-icon/references/VOCABULARY.md`). Each row names the primitive, what it is for, the shipped
reference found by grep on 2026-11-07, the shader read through the material's `m_Shader` guid against the
`.meta` map (`look_kit/audit.py` does the same read), and whether it is lit / faceted today in the fleet's sense
(`LOOK_LANGUAGE.md`, the note on "lit") or a flat offender. "To measure" means the look was not read from the asset
here; `look_kit/audit.py` reads it. Nothing in this table has been confirmed by an owner.

**Measured once, for the whole table.** Every project shader graph that draws a world thing is a URP Unlit
sub-target; facets are told by normal nodes in `BlockGraph` (3), `CrystalGraph` (4), `VesselGraph` (3),
`ShieldShader` (1) and by none in `WispGraph`, `LaserGraph`, `ConicGraph`, `ExplosionGraph`, `RippleGraph`,
`ProjectileGraph`. Hand-written shaders that blend `One One` (additive sheets): `BallTrail`, `SoftSpark`,
`SolitonTunnelAdditive`, `CrystalChargeArcs`, `CloakGhost`, `EchoSightHalo`, `FulmenBolt`, `ArkLeashShell`.
Particle systems: 28 in 19 prefabs (`grep -rl 'ParticleSystem:' Assets/_Prefabs`), 15 of them under
`Assets/_Prefabs/Environment/Materials/FX/` and `Assets/_Prefabs/Projectile/`.

## To draw (the language needs them; nothing in the game draws them yet)

- **A lance as a volume**: the Serpent's and the Shrike's beam as a faceted bar in the domain colour with a core,
  replacing the `Sprites/Default` line (rule 1, rule 12).
- **The ward**: a hull-side volume that says the arena cannot bite this pilot, for the three states that hold one.
- **A ring as a volume**: a travelling front (the Stoat's ripple, a shockwave) as a faceted torus whose tube scales
  with its radius, so the Mobile look's "mode-2 ring" is not a flat ring (rule 5's exception, rule 3).
- **The fleet's trail for the ribbon users**: the missile's plume shape (or the prism chain) on the Manta, Sparrow,
  Thresher, the Grizzly bomb and the skyburst missile.
- **A spark that is a shard**: one faceted shard primitive the 19 particle prefabs can be rebuilt from, so a hit, a
  skim and a boost are volumes.
- **A blast front for the conic explosions**: the Brittle Star's form generalised (a front whose thicknesses are
  shares of its radius).
- **The jet plume as a volume**: the ember mouth's exhaust as a faceted tube that answers the throttle, beside the
  Engine slot it comes from.
- **A petal in the world**, only if an owner wants one: today the petals are the HUD's.

Every "to draw" is a proposal. Which one is drawn first is the plan's round order (`Docs/SKILL_TRAINING_PLAN.md` §3.2:
R1 the Orca's missile and bomb, R2 the crystal capture, R3 the black hole's ring, R4 a trail), and each is judged in
the editor by its owner.
